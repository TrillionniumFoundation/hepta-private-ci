## Scientific disposition

Adding a second relevant-looking *source* is not evidence that the Reader
can use it to improve accuracy. This real development comparison gives
negligible observed mean F1 gain and materially greater prompt cost on
the only eight eligible transfer cases. The other eight questions contain
no eligible E2, and must remain missing, not silent zero-cost wins.
None of the cited source windows is certified sufficient by an independent
human semantic reviewer. No prospective task family, statistical
superiority, citation entailment precision, parameter-only knowledge gain,
production switch or rollback can be inferred from this result.

**Decision:** preserve this result as a negative/limited capacity
diagnostic; do not widen Reader budgets or enable parametric memory from
it. Only run a new source-count intervention after demonstrating genuinely
independent eligible second-source cases and a calibrated same-budget
read/semantic-judgment comparison.
