## What this permits and prohibits

1. This historical sample indicates **Reader answer formatting, citation discipline and abstention** are immediate blockers. Sufficient evidence being present does not imply a successful answer. A stronger-Reader diagnostic is needed before claiming memory learning gain; any benefit solely from switching models is Reader capacity, not MemoryCell.
2. The mechanical lexical Top-2 contained the necessary target original declaration in only **one of two** exposed fault cases; the cross-file oracle had it in both. That is an *evidence selection* difference, not a successful learned policy or end-to-end task improvement.
3. The two questions share **one source root** and two pre-exposed synthetic behavior faults. No claim of source-independent statistical significance, independent human sufficiency, future calendar windows, semantic citation entailment, actual program diagnosis-and-patch execution, learned Head benefit, real long-term withdrawal/recovery or valid lifecycle TCO.
4. Existing selected CPython upstream tests were previously executed (70 real original test-method invocations) under controlled faults and rollback; **none of the 16 model answers attempted or implemented a code repair**, and no new program tests were invoked by the Reader. Thus `real_program_bug_repair_success=null` rather than zero or one.
5. Complete costs (extract/index/train/queries/raw storage/maintenance/revocation/rollback) are **unknown**. The measured CPU seconds/token totals are only per-query inference receipts. No production admission, no source/test owner replacement, no native deployment, no `blocking-ci` bypass, no main merge.
