## Costs must remain complete and nonduplicating

`lifecycle_accounting.py` represents inclusive measurement envelopes with explicit
parentage, receipt digests and clock domains. The training interval inside writing
and the model load/inference interval inside reading do not get charged twice.
Cycles, duplicate identities, incompatible clocks, nonfinite/negative values and
children exceeding their enclosing interval reject. Caller-supplied measurements
are not externally authenticated by their names or hashes.

The frozen experiment records extraction, indexing/planning, policy writing,
snapshot freezing, reader loading/execution, backup copy and replay. Original
sources and archived snapshot/index copies remain in storage counts. Referenced
model inventory bytes are reported separately; the model-free audit does not
re-download or verify tensor bytes. Actual model staging performs those checks.

Sums across recorded phase envelopes are aggregate recorded work, not a single
elapsed wall-clock or equal-hardware claim. Shared multi-arm costs cannot be
assigned wholesale to each arm. The reuse curve is a conditional projection of
the mixed-arm study average, NOT a learned-policy or production speedup. Its fixed
planning envelope includes already cached query encoding/retrieval and evaluation
planning. It must NOT be reused as a cost estimate for distinct new tasks without
separating those query-dependent operations. Read-stage means also include the
recorded protocol/verification overhead, not just neural inference. Per-arm
allocation and a representative future workload remain unmeasured.

Unknown maintenance and production recovery remain null in full projections;
clean replay cannot impute them as zero. Every projected query count is
hypothetical; no future query was observed by the projection. The objective is
C_extract + C_index + C_train + N*C_query + C_maintenance, including recovery.
Known subcosts support diagnosis, not an economic benefit claim absent complete
measurement, allocation, hardware and quality comparability. HNMF gates remain.
