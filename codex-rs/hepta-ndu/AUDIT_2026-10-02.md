## Reproduced defects and fixes

1. The open projection store checked directory/lock identity but trusted cached
   journal entries after the actual image was truncated, changed or replaced.
   A positive read or identical retry could acknowledge missing durable history;
   a new write could overwrite the damaged image. Every positive storage boundary
   now authenticates the exact bounded live image against its retained expected
   length/digest. Storage-image failure latches the handle indeterminate until
   reopen; externally restoring bytes cannot revive it. Successful persistence
   installs the new expected identity and verifies it before acknowledgement.
   Directory/lock ownership errors retain their existing error semantics.
2. Quantized FBSDE metric evaluation used unbounded affine predictions while
   training and independent reference replay clipped to the admitted value bound.
   A coefficient within bounds multiplied by an admitted feature could therefore
   produce conflicting RMSE/calibration/improvement receipts. Metric prediction
   now applies the same clipping rule. The regression compares complete metric
   objects against the independent replay, with both positive and negative
   predictions exceeding the bound.
3. Three original positive FBSDE tests failed `HoldoutRmse`: their terminal value
   included future increment noise despite a 0.1 predictive RMSE gate. Positive
   roundtrip tests now use a realizable conditional-value fixture. The original
   noisy target is preserved as an explicit rejection test. No efficacy or
   calibration threshold was relaxed, and no stochastic scientific acceptance
   is inferred from this deterministic fixture.

## Remaining boundaries

Image comparison detects observed corruption, not hostile simultaneous writes
that ignore the private-directory/advisory-lock contract. Reopen validates a
self-consistent journal but cannot by itself distinguish a complete historical
rollback; external owner witnesses and independent rollback domains remain
mandatory. Missing files are rejected on a live handle, not silently reinitialized.

The bounded linear pilot is not a general nonlinear FBSDE solver or scientific
validation of stochastic policy improvement. Frozen real datasets, independent
science/evaluation, named host/filesystem crash qualification, external backup
and restoration, protected authority, monitoring, operator acceptance and
activation remain separate evidence gates. Exact source/merge CI is still required.
