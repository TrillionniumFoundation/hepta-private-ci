## Task decisions: avoid turning value precision into invented accuracy

All fitted arms already achieved 100% non-tie decision accuracy and zero regret
on the primary new-terminal and longer-horizon choice panels. Consequently the
primary numerical improvement is NOT a demonstrated increase in task-answer
accuracy. In the larger discrete state space, mean non-tie decision accuracy is
A=0.990451389, B=0.989149306, C=0.998697917, B_LBFGS=0.993489583 and D=1.
The complete raw per-panel decision regret/accuracy is retained in SCORES.csv.

## A material failure, retained unchanged

The probability panel simultaneously changes risk to zero, discount to one,
running cost to zero and terminal values to indicators. It is an explicit
out-of-profile stress test, not a pure risk ablation or the original-law guarantee.
C's mean RMSE rises to 0.217396213, versus B's 0.0124118481. Individual C RMSEs
are 0.352299112, 0.0473878433 and 0.252501684. The fractions of raw final values
outside [0,1] are 51.0417%, 3.1250% and 30.2083%. Raw and clipped Brier metrics
are both saved; no clipping or favorable-only filtering conceals this failure.

The result identifies a need to test structural invariants and task/profile
coverage, not to conclude that the whole NDU approach failed. Conversely,
a smaller trained-domain residual does not license an unconditional generalization
claim. A follow-on must separate coefficient changes, use a new terminal family,
and compare structurally constrained B/C under the same family/information/budget.
The original exposed stress outputs must remain immutable.

## What C and D actually did

C solved a reduced-space nonlinear finite-law FBSDE collocation system over
shared Stem weights using trust-region reflective least squares and full
forward-mode Jacobians. Every depth and both sides of the equation differentiate
through the tied parameters. It did NOT solve the general Pontryagin system for
an unrestricted utility-maximizing agent or learn unknown environment/preference
coefficients. No continuous-time viscosity convergence is established here.

D directly instantiated the monotone entropic operator from coefficients supplied
to every arm. Its errors against the independent oracle are about 1e-16. That
is expected for this exact-structure reference, not evidence of learned language
intelligence. The same structure gives a gamma-Lipschitz error propagation bound;
every evaluated finite-domain envelope check passed. Floating-point evaluations
are not interval-certified proofs over every unobserved contract.
