## What is new

Keep R6's SAME continuous uniform initial law and R5's nonlinear shared forward
feedback, quartic terminal task errors and entropic recursion. The primitive
plant and observed preference law remain known. Conditional branches are exactly
summed; CC3/CC5 approximate the same continuous integral. This remains a small
finite-horizon NDU special case, not a general continuous-time FBSDE theorem.

C caches the explicit scalar-objective gradient graph at the current candidate,
then computes Hessian-vector products for SciPy trust-ncg without constructing
the full parameter Hessian. Reverse differentiation returns (Dg)^T v, which
equals H v for this smooth objective. Dense, forward-JVP and finite-difference
checks agree. Cache graphs cannot survive parameter changes or quadrature
promotion. HVP, graph-build, rejected trial and callback work is paid.

Ordinary-AD matrix-free trust-ncg, cached dense explicit trust-exact, Adam and
L-BFGS are mandatory controls. Dense is rerun in this new cache-capable harness,
not relabeled as R6's exact implementation. C's improvement combines matrix-free
algebra, graph reuse and truncated-CG trust steps; it is not isolated to one
instruction or an NDU-exclusive objective. Primary B/C have identical model
class, initial bytes, fixed CC5 rule, scalar objective and nominal CPU budget.
