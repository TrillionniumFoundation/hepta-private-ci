# Hepta Continuous Spiking Network research record

This branch extends the HCSN review at main 732e934ba496d90fbe042b8d41d479ef901643e5 and PR #7187 at b3fdc981568ab9ce122d2fb569372651e0b4b314. All new code and results in this tree are research artifacts. Each linked study freezes its own methods, cohorts, budgets and evidence. The point estimates below cannot be aggregated across outcomes.

| Question | Evidence | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Can different histories give the same prices and flow but different next spikes? | [Proof and trajectories](reachable/RESULTS.md). Common-reset reachability certified by interval arithmetic at 8 and 12 atoms. Three reported observables can hide a 0.006675 next-flow gap on eight atoms. | Exact finite-grid claim about one current message. Prior message histories or a receiver query supply extra information. |
| Can a compact channel preserve useful forecasts? | [Physical vs predictive ports](synapse/README.md). On 1,774 held-out retinal events, wider ports reduce loss vs the scalar barrier. The eight-wide predictive and intrinsic ports are statistically unresolved. [Strict lag and recalibration](https://github.com/TrillionniumFoundation/hepta-private-ci/tree/38ee155960780da6aa647f156b6bee4b43d85e81/qualification/hcsn_retinal_strict_lag_20261127). | Ten repeat intervals of one biological recording. Sub-millisecond event order is unavailable. Association is sensitive to the lag convention. |
| Does the network train end-to-end with BP? | [Trainable module](network/README.md). Two complete layers and causal, streaming, gradient and intervention tests. [Parallel transport](network/SCAN_RESULTS.md) for barriers determined without same-layer price feedback. | CPU double gradients and exact scan identities; speed depends on setting. |
| Does the literal law aid measured spike prediction? | [Recorded cortical spikes](neurons/README.md). Three of three cells favor the literal feature bank over equal-width smooth features on held-out waveform segments; the fast-spiking cell favors LIF on point loss. | Three recordings, common readout, same stimulus history, internal held-out split rather than official hidden competition test. |
| Does the risk-neutral drift support tonic firing? | [Stationary derivation](continuum/TONIC.md), [spatial/temporal convergence](continuum/README.md), [Allen cohorts](continuum/ALLEN_RESULTS.md). With the original positive drift and an unbounded domain there is terminal escape. At fixed relative reset a constant barrier does not change the finite-grid equilibrium flow. Separating physical drift or using an absolute reset changes the model. | The drift trichotomy and absolute-reset alternative already occur in earlier repository work. Empirical F–I checks are retrospective on previously archived, disjoint cohorts. |
| Can weights be edited without training-loss BP? | [Speech training and 52 held-out speakers](https://github.com/TrillionniumFoundation/hepta-private-ci/tree/7dcce23693ad3d8a06283c41acd6715fcdee3990/qualification/hcsn_independent_audiomnist_20261127). Forward hidden editing transfers to new speakers. At the joint early target, end-to-end times were 10.319 s vs 10.187 s for BP. | One domain transfer. The old-domain accuracy and shuffled-label control are retained. |
| Is a from-scratch forward-only learner equivalent to BP? | [Gas-domain drift, full weights and scores](https://github.com/TrillionniumFoundation/hepta-private-ci/tree/2ed352a60ec941a5f0726875066cf7054a37f37d/qualification/hcsn_scratch_gas_drift_20261127). HCSN Forward Projection scores 43.16% macro recall; width-matched ReLU-FP 33.82%, widened ReLU-FP 53.65%. A direct forward iterative method scores 41.60%, mixed hidden BP plus ridge 48.84%. | Three external collection batches, 4,364 observations. No method clears the quality floor in all three seeds. |
| Does continuous spiking outperform dense attention at a real image–caption objective? | [Flickr 15-model GPU completion](https://github.com/TrillionniumFoundation/hepta-private-ci/tree/aeeab662ac9dae57c4b48e4fe6a0be6189edd998/qualification/hcsn_research_20261127/flickr_gpu/final_2520). HCSN NLL 2.9080 vs dense 2.9096, matched smooth 2.9112, LIF 2.9206. HCSN vs LIF separates; HCSN vs dense/smooth does not. [Power coverage](https://github.com/TrillionniumFoundation/hepta-private-ci/blob/d1401b33db5f80dafdacdf6e71841f193da56ebb/qualification/hcsn_research_20261127/flickr_gpu/final_2520/PACKAGE_ENERGY.md). | 15 × 42 min = 10.5 GPU-hours. Best dev snapshots all occurred by 120 s. Available late-window package watts cannot establish energy at those early quality thresholds. |
| Do constructed image–caption encoders save total time? | [RSICD cold replays](https://github.com/TrillionniumFoundation/hepta-private-ci/blob/de41e8ab5f3190f830eee60348522fe13273372c/qualification/hcsn_rsicd_20261127/cold_replay/README.md). HCSN 85.89 s / 1.0892% R@1; CCA 37.14 s / 1.4004%; short CNN/attention 65.73 s / 0.5187%, longer 165.04 s / 0.8817%. | Three matched cold processes per arm. Scope begins with locally cached raw source. Low absolute retrieval, text/scene ambiguity and comparison width matter. |
| Do thresholds anticipate a future change of cost? | [Two-regime stopping and repeated firing](regime/README.md). The reduced cell has certified endogenous thresholds .433719/.575707 versus frozen .356544/.713087. Exact-time simulation covers 1.92 million first-hit and 1.44 million regenerative paths. An implicit PyTorch layer differentiates both boundaries. | Assumes an observable Markov cost and continuous value diffusion; replaces finite-maturity debt with a running cost. No biological fit is implied. |

## Active studies as of November 27 at 18:37 Eastern

The [metered intervention study](field/PROTOCOL.md) has acquired 9,865 French training, 1,996 selection, 3,435 French confirmation and 1,619 external Belgian anchors. The stored development receipts and [dedicated checkpoint branch](https://github.com/TrillionniumFoundation/hepta-private-ci/tree/research/hcsn-cost-checkpoints-20261127-e0bf/qualification/hcsn_frontier_20261127/field) preserve actual fitted parameters, optimizer and sampler. The endpoint is a constructed debit contract applied to measured consumption. French/Belgian test predictions remain sealed until all candidate fits finish.

The independently registered grouped-mark [COCO transfer](https://github.com/TrillionniumFoundation/hepta-private-ci/tree/fd120a7f7182fa4de5d2449087294d911d34c834/qualification/hcsn_grouped_marks_20261127) uses new images, new training clocks and local GPU confirmation. An independent [UCM size experiment](scale/PROTOCOL.md) separately tests physical, smooth, row-mean and dense prefixes on a common randomly initialized causal decoder at two sizes. The [pre-dispatch amendment](scale/AMENDMENT.md) preserves per-caption losses because training captions often repeat verbatim in the official splits. The requested cloud T4 worker failed before training and a dedicated device probe returned CPU-only PyTorch. An [independent Apple M5 run](scale/MPS_PROTOCOL.md) passed all four native-GPU gradient checks and a real 24-example step at 10,892,280 and 39,797,240 parameters. Its full matrix and a [single-core CPU replication](scale/CPU_PROTOCOL.md) are underway.

A separate [three-modality MOSI encoder study](https://github.com/TrillionniumFoundation/hepta-private-ci/commit/5db0404968a602d0a1c847be8b25d5ba42c3a0b6) compares full hidden-layer learning at widths 64, 256 and 1024 with video-disjoint folds, counterfactual audio/vision/text inputs and a common quality target. The inputs are released upstream features.

The reference paper is He and Xiong, Rollover Risk and Credit Risk, Journal of Finance 67(2), 391–430, DOI 10.1111/j.1540-6261.2012.01721.x. The equity boundary satisfies value matching and smooth pasting. Discounted credit loss, first-hit probability and equity are separate observables. A regenerative reset, refractory period, physical drift and finite-dimensional port are neural modeling decisions. The most recent directly tested feedback-free training reference is O’Shea and Rajendran, Closed-form feedback-free learning with forward projection, Nature Communications, DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-69161-1.
