# Anticipating the next cost regime changes the firing boundary

Freezing He–Xiong coefficients in each processing interval gives a causal neural cell. If the neuron anticipates a stochastic change in its future operating cost, each regime's continuation value also depends on the other. This directory implements a reduced model in which those coupled values and the regime-marked Laplace transforms of firing admit piecewise power solutions. It includes a global optimal-stopping certificate at the registered coefficients, an independent obstacle-grid solver, a differentiable PyTorch layer, an exact-time path sampler and the regenerative extension. The model deliberately replaces the original paper's maturing debt cohorts and debt-issue price by a running cost. It therefore supplies a tractable extension and does not evaluate the full paper under switching maturities.

## Endogenous thresholds and the second channel

Let dV/V=(r−δ)dt+σdW and let S switch from 0 to 1 at rate u and back at rate w, independently of W. The stopping objective is the supremum over stopping times of the expected discounted integral of δV−c_S until stopping, with zero liquidation payoff. Numerical parameters are r=.08, δ=.02, σ=.23, (c₀,c₁)=(.04,.08), (u,w)=(.12,.16). If L is the price diffusion generator and Q the two-state Markov generator, the value satisfies E≥0, (L+Q−r)E+δV−c≤0 and equality in live states. The continuation value vanishes with zero slope at its own threshold. The second channel Hᵢ=Eᵢ[exp(−qτ)] with q=.09 gives the discounted mass of a unit first-hit claim. It has magnitude one at immediate stopping and generally a nonzero one-sided derivative. This is a chosen unit-loss convention. Substituting a monetary recovery schedule changes the payoff.

For i fixed in perpetuity, ν₋(r) is the negative root of (σ²/2)ν² + (r−δ−σ²/2)ν−r=0. Its static threshold is ν₋/(ν₋−1) times cᵢ/r. Those thresholds are 0.356544 and 0.713087. When future switches matter the two coupled values give **0.433718802 and 0.575706910**. At V=.50 the cheap regime continues and the dear regime stops immediately. A switch can therefore induce an event while V is unchanged. The values and expected-flow messages are continuous as functions of V within a fixed regime. The observed regime is discrete, and its switch can make a realized threshold or event jump.

Below the cheap threshold both values are zero. Between thresholds only the cheap regime is live and its equation has discount r+u, particular solution δV/(δ+u)−c₀/(r+u), and power exponents ν±(r+u). Above both thresholds the particular solution is V−(rI−Q)⁻¹c, with a common decaying mode ν₋(r) and a regime-difference mode ν₋(r+u+w). The individual matching and zero-slope conditions remove four coefficients. The remaining two equations match cheap-regime value and slope across the upper threshold. First-hit channel coefficients solve a separate four-by-four linear system with Dirichlet conditions. Different exit rewards give joint transforms by the regime at firing and by the cause of the hit.

## Independent checks

- Interval arithmetic at 256 bits gives root enclosures [0.43371880195599659152 ± 3.08e−21] and [0.575706909988585481021 ± 7.00e−22]. The interval-Jacobian contraction is below 2.77e−10 on a box of radius 1e−11 around the numerical solution. Positive middle-region power coefficients give positive curvature; the first decreasing upper mode dominates the signed faster-decaying curvature coefficient in the cheap regime, while the two curvature terms in the dear regime are positive. Smooth fit then gives nonnegative values increasing above each wall. The stopped-region continuation residual is maximal at the respective boundary. Both maxima are strictly negative as certified in certificate.json. Linear growth and δ>0 make the discounted terminal continuation term negligible. The stopping verification inequality and equality up to the first boundary identify the global optimum at this law.
- An independent linear-complementarity policy iteration used five grids with 401 through 6,401 log-asset nodes and asymptotic endpoints. At 6,401 nodes its boundaries are 0.433850 and 0.575466. Sup errors over .15<V<4 are 1.17e−6 for E and 1.27e−3 for H. The second channel's grid error is sensitive to boundary placement. The PyTorch and NumPy functions agree to 1.95e−16 on the central test inputs. The four-cost/rate Jacobian has maximum discrepancy 1.31e−9 across 88 entries; 110 asset derivatives agree to 8.30e−11. Fitting known synthetic curves reduced mean objective from .013696 to .00007169 in 90 updates. These establish differentiable identification of the readout functions at this setting, not unique estimation of every biological parameter.
- 1.92 million first-hit paths sampled exact exponential event times and a drifted Brownian bridge between competing switches and an independent exponential censoring clock. The largest discrepancy in discounted first-hit and switch-marked means across the 12 starting regimes/values is 1.66 Monte Carlo standard errors. At V=.52 in the cheap regime, H=.695853; the discounted component due to a boundary jump is .091028 (13.08% of H). This share is discounted and conditional on this calibrated cell. It is not an observed neuronal statistic.

## From first hit to repeated spikes

Write Kᵢⱼ(V,q)=Eᵢ[exp(−qτ)1{Sτ=j}]. The two columns come from the same four-coefficient Dirichlet problem with a different terminal reward. After a hit the neuron enters a deterministic refractory period D=.25. The external regime continues switching. At release the neuron resets to Vᵣ,ₖ=Bₖ exp(.30) in the current regime k. Let Uᵢⱼ=Kᵢⱼ(Vᵣ,ᵢ,q) and Pᴰ=exp(QD). The total discounted number of spikes from each reset satisfies

$$z=U\mathbf{1}+e^{-qD}UP^D z.$$

For general V the answer is K(V,q)[1+exp(−qD)Pᴰ z]. A two-by-two solve thus gives an exact frequency-domain neural readout without discretizing the asset state. It encodes a discounted sum of future events. An instantaneous time-domain spike density would require inverting or otherwise resolving that transform. At q=.09 the reset-state means are 1.05708 and .72172, with kernel spectral radius .44973. A separate 1.44 million path check across three discount rates and four start configurations gives maximum discrepancy 1.76 standard errors. K's columns sum to the separate unmarked first-hit transform to roundoff. No performance task was used to select these coefficients.

## Reproduction and scope

Run stopping.py, verify.py, gradcheck_model.py, jump_check.py, renewal.py and certify.py. The last requires python-flint. implicit.RegimeCell accepts a positive double-precision asset tensor and returns conditional values, transforms and the differentiable threshold pair for one shared law. Both the law parameters and the asset tensor participate in ordinary first-order BP away from the stopping surfaces. The scalar equilibrium initialization uses SciPy on the host. Higher-order implicit derivatives, coefficients crossing the ordering c₀<c₁, and high-throughput GPU root solves are outside the verified interface.

Regime-switching smooth fit and matrix renewal are established mathematics. See Zhang and Guo, SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics, DOI 10.1137/S0036139903426083. The initial 7,394-object in-path repository search found one wording about switching among scalar drift cases; it does not implement this coupled problem. The scientific claim here is the explicit neuron and its checked behavior. Biological fit, an equal-quality speed advantage or priority over all prior work do not follow from the certificate.
