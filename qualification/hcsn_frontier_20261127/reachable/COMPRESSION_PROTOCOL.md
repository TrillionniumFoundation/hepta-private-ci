# Compression of a common-reset finite population under a new driving distribution

Use twelve active atoms, the same nominal initial and terminal barrier and the exact intervened transition. Generate independent training histories of 20 changes, and two heldout sets of 20 changes, one from the training mixture and one with persistent runs, sign flips and abrupt changes. The allowable log-boundary range is ±0.17. Compare loss of information about the final flow in future 1,2,4,8-step control sequences. The receiver is given each future schedule and knows the kernel. Future schedules include previously unseen schedules with the training drive law and with the shifted law. The experiment studies transmission of state, with no uncertainty about the receiver’s schedule.

Compare three nominal outputs (flow/equity/loss), seven with the four published horizons, same-width state PCA, and an optimal Euclidean low-rank basis of the train-policy future-flow dictionary using SVD. Fit a common least-squares reconstruction of state from a port on training histories only. Quantify RMSE, 95th percentile and maximum error in output probability; report singular values, encoder and decoder costs. Include full state and a schedule-aware exact one-scalar projection for each individual requested endpoint. The latter changes what is known at encoding time; it is an analytic check and does not solve general unknown-future compression. Report results at K=3,7,11 for learned bases. One fit, independent deterministic seeds, no hyperparameter tuning or selection on heldout. The housing and image/text outcomes are unrelated to these synthetic trajectories.
