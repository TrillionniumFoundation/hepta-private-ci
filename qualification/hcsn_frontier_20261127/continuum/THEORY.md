# Exact renewal law and a failure of time-only refinement

Write the live signed log margin as X(t)=x+mu t+sigma W(t), for a stationary boundary over the comparison. The killed first-hit CDF F(x,t) is the two-image Gaussian expression used by the sender. It is defective when drift points away from the boundary. After each hit, let the process wait a physical duration d then start again at x. The hitting-time Laplace transform for distance x has the exponential form exp[−x k(s)]. A product of n such transforms is therefore the hitting-time transform for distance n x, also when a trajectory may escape forever. Equivalently split the path of a drifted Brownian motion at levels separated by x and invoke the strong Markov property. The time shift for the nth hit is (n−1)d. The expected number of hits up to T is consequently the sum over n≥1 of F(n x, T−(n−1)d), with zero summand for nonpositive time. This is an independent benchmark for the neural reset model. Under discrete observation, snapping a crossing to the interval end changes renewal timing.

For any fixed finite spatial grid with its lowest representative strictly greater than zero and each representative strictly inside its bin, let h>0 be the smallest distance from a representative to either the absorbing wall or a finite bin boundary. During a sufficiently short interval of length dt the deterministic drift has absolute displacement below h/2. The reflection principle and a Gaussian tail bound give a uniform upper bound C exp(−c/dt) on leaving a representative's bin or hitting the wall. The number of intervals up to a fixed horizon is at most T/dt+1. A union bound shows that the probability of even a single move or hit goes to zero as dt goes to zero. The exact diffusion has positive first-hit probability for every T>0. Thus the spatial grid from the implementation cannot be held fixed while refining time in a claim about convergence to the continuum neuron. This argument applies at a stationary barrier; changing the barrier introduces additional input-induced events. Exact closed-form integration within each step is compatible with a nonzero multi-step spatial error.

The original implementation keeps fired mass refractory for a single subsequent time interval. That modeled refractory duration changes with the chosen time step. A physical refractory duration can be held fixed with an integer delay pipeline. The scripts report both time conventions and use exact one-dimensional renewal references for each. A nonlinear mean reset and a full population generally define different stochastic processes.
