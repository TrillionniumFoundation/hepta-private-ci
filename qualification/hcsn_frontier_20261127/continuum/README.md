# Time refinement alone can erase firing

At nominal constant boundary, the exact first-hit probability from log margin 0.30 by time 2 is 0.29151417. The native grid at dt=0.25 reports 0.35892943 with eight atoms and 0.32052703 with twelve. The latter is 2.90 percentage points above the continuous value. On twelve fixed atoms, changing dt to 1/128 yields 0.04111288, and to 1/512 yields 5.57e−12. THEORY.md proves that the fixed-grid first-hit probability tends to zero under time-only refinement, even in exact real arithmetic. The two zero values in the floating-point table are numerical zeros.

On the wider 2.60 domain with true midpoint cells, coupled refinement from 24 atoms / dt=1/8 through 384 atoms / dt=1/128 yields first-hit estimates 0.330418, 0.309561, 0.301174, 0.296290, 0.293884. Corresponding mean cumulative spike-count errors for a fixed 0.25 physical refractory interval decrease from +0.052494 to +0.003053. Across all 86 reported configurations, the largest total mass error in a renewal run is 5.11e−15. These numbers are conditional on constant forcing and the specified physical model. Probability conservation alone does not verify trajectory accuracy. The implementation aids selection of a spatial and temporal resolution for a chosen error tolerance when interpreting this network physically.
