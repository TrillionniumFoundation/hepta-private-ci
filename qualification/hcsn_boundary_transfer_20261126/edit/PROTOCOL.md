# UCI HAR released-cost adaptation, prospective design, 2026-11-26

Data: the public UCI HAR 30-subject smartphone dataset (DOI 10.24432/C54S4K). File hashes and retrieval mirror are pinned in SOURCE.json. Labels are the six observed activities. A free classifier sees the first body-acceleration X-axis mean and standard deviation (UCI columns 1,4). A paid classifier sees all 561 supplied features. Prices are hypothetical units per query, not measured hardware or payments. Both actions have observable counterfactual classification correctness. The project has not verified historic disjointness against all other uses of this public dataset.

Split by person. Of the 21 official training people sorted by ID, first 9 fit the two frozen least-squares classifiers and a cheap confidence distribution, next 6 fit candidates, and last 6 select. All 9 official test subjects, never used for selection in this experiment, evaluate once in a new process after sealing every checkpoint. Externally supplied data features retain the original preprocessing. Do not split overlapping windows across people or treat windows as independent replicates.

Deploy buy when a genuine SmallHCSN sender's one-step QVI boundary exceeds the cheap observation level, else use the free decision. Price changes from 0.45 (old) to 0.10 (new). Utility = correct class indicator minus price times buy. Receiver and long-horizon training are outside this experiment. Define cheap level as the nominal QVI boundary plus 0.022 times (training-reference empirical confidence rank minus 0.5), a fixed engineered coupling which lets a scalar cost edit move the purchase threshold. Nominal means raw_cost=0, unmodified input drives. Cheap inputs also condition drift and effective impulse cost through the original code. Fit the incumbent cost on the representation group under the old price with an exact scalar threshold search; the two experts also train there. This resubstitution carries possible incumbent overfit. Evaluate per-person retention separately.

Compare KEEP, an exact raw_cost edit via inversion at observed levels and a threshold sweep, same-raw_cost-only gradient training, wider gradient training of raw_cost and the original sender drive weights/bias, and a ridge purchase-benefit head. Every non-KEEP arm always includes KEEP as a selection candidate. Direct uses all behaviorally distinct scalar thresholds in [-3,3] plus parent; admits train candidates when worst-person old utility change >= -0.05; exports optimum new mean subject utility and parent only. BP uses true first-order QVI backward with a logistic relaxation of the deployed decision, original deployment remains hard for all. Two step sizes (0.02,0.12), 48 full-batch Adam updates each, return states at 4/12/24/48. Temperature in boundary units falls geometrically 0.006 to 0.0015. Wider BP bounds added drive increments in [-0.35,0.35]. For both BP arms a 3x differentiable per-person old slack penalty uses the same 0.05 limit. Ridge uses three fixed regularizers (0.1,1,10) and the new decision price or offsets +/-0.10. Exact/direct and BP candidates use the same adaptations and paired outcomes, fixed upstream model, deploy criterion and legal raw_cost range, with distinct optimization algorithms and candidate counts. No-gradient and BP search time includes all tries. Shared expert preparation, model import, test, per-arm wall and forward verification are reported separately.

On dev choose maximum mean new utility across subjects subject to worst per-person old change >= -0.05, and prefer KEEP for ties. Test success requires delta new >0 on average, lowest subject old delta >= -0.05 and positive lower endpoint of the two-sided, 95% subject-bootstrap paired mean new delta (10,000 replicates, fixed seed 20261126). No alternate thresholds or arms selected on test. Separately report mean and worst old/new deltas, buys, apparent time/quality and the oracle benefit of choosing the higher utility action after observing correctness. Failure counts and held-out non-adoption remain evidence. Report these as a real-label policy task with a simulated acquisition tariff, a known one-step input interface, a reused public population, limited coverage and no measured sensor/energy costs. No GPU timing or generalized backbone training claim.
