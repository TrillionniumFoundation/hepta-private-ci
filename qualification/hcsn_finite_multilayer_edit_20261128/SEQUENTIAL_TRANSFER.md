Use at most 30 calibration and 25 development recordings per digit; select lowest sha256(path) within each category. Include KEEP, wrong-new-label forward response on each physical parent, and same law smooth controls. Score stability after freezing and reloading. Report accuracy by domain, class and speaker, all panel sizes, old calibration shifts, actual update norms, negative controls, costs inclusive and exclusive of precomputed features and stages.
