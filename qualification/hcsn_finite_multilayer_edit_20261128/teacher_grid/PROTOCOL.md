# Can two internal matrices absorb the stronger acoustic ridge?

This new experiment was designed after the first-stage held-out evidence at 9db378b9. The original additive acoustic ridge was stronger and faster than the new two-site hidden edit on A/B. This is an explicitly outcome-informed next question with fresh recordings and a new source. The original A/B/C results remain fixed. None of the candidate updates in this study uses an outcome from the new panels.

## Fresh assessment

A2/B2 use the same original 52 excluded-from-fit/development AudioMNIST speaker IDs, assigned alternately from sorted IDs. Use repetitions 24–26 for A2, 27–29 for B2, yielding at most 780 new recordings per panel. The older studies already exposed some of these speakers; these are newly acquired utterances. Use old FSDD 300-recording held-out set only for retention.

The genuinely new source is the original audiovisual GRID corpus (University of Sheffield, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.3625687). Use its author word-level alignments to crop contiguous real digit words in complete spoken command sentences. Register the names zero through nine. Use at most 8 aligned clips per digit per available speaker in canonical SHA256(filename) order. Over every returned file verify both the original CRC and normalized PCM hash; exclude any collision with the previous three sources and the new support. Crop original 25 kHz audio by the author's tick boundaries. Confirm the relationship between final tick and sample count, and report duration and class distributions, rejections, speaker count and ambiguity, before model scoring. GRID speakers and their command contexts supply an independent test of robustness to coarticulation and different recording conditions. Labels are genuine corpus words; word boundaries are supplied annotation and their use is explicit. Do not fold GRID training examples into this fit.

Predeclare the separate outcomes. A faithful native compression needs every literal seed to retain old accuracy within two points of its unedited parent, gain at least two points with a positive 95% speaker-paired interval over KEEP on both A2/B2, and be noninferior to the external ridge teacher within two points (paired lower bound) on both. A robust cross-source result also requires every literal seed to gain two points over KEEP on GRID plus a positive paired lower bound. Compare matched-smooth behavior and the two hidden matrices' effective update ranks. A speed claim requires this primary native compression gate for same-site Adam and the direct student, matched accuracy noninferiority within two points, and at least 20% lower median measured total elapsed, charging extra data acquisition and teacher construction on both paths. Report cold and warm timings separately. Strong results on fresh repetitions alone do not close cross-source performance. Multiple related studies share the original development source and parents; confidence intervals are conditional on these models.
