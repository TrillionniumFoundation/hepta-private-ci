# Time to matched validation quality and image conditioning

This supplementary analysis was frozen after the first twenty-second round and one second-round cell had run on CPU. It does not change model selection or the primary test in PROTOCOL.md. No test labels or predictions have been inspected. All arms and seeds use the same targets computed from training token identities alone. Compute the natural-log entropy H of the empirical training next-token distribution (exclude BOS and padding, include EOS and unknown). The five fixed validation thresholds are H minus 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0 and 2.5 nats. A model reaches a threshold at its first scheduled 128-image evaluation with NLL at or below the threshold. Report the interval between the previous and crossing observation, cumulative measured training and cumulative validation time, optimizer steps, tokens, and right censoring. No interpolation. Pair seeds for speed ratios only when both methods attain a target. Report the denominator and both positive and negative speed comparisons.

For each trained arm, report full validation and test NLL, perplexity, conditioning gain (cyclically mismatched image NLL minus matched image NLL), paired 95% bootstrap over image groups, and each seed separately. Full-panel confidence intervals average the named seeds first. They measure image uncertainty conditional on these three seeds. Do not infer variance across arbitrary model initializations. Preprocessing, orchestration and retries are reported separately from per-model optimization and validation. Weight and prediction hashes distinguish completed cold scores from progress. Execution receipts are additive and never alter a sealed model.
