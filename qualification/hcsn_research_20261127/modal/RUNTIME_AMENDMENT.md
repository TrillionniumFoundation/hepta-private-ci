The first two concurrently running original physical training workers exited on SIGKILL before producing a checkpoint. Both had batch size 24, no structured OOM diagnostic, in a shared CPU/memory environment. Other arms had completed seven fits. No confirmation split had been opened. The original 24 identifiers per optimizer update and same AdamW steps are retained. The registered comparison is restarted from scratch for all methods on one worker, using six microbatches of four averaged losses per optimizer step across every neural method to reduce peak memory. Gradient clipping is applied once after all six backward passes. Development and confirmation prediction batches are reduced to 8 for all methods. The first incomplete staging result is retained as an engineering attempt, excluded from the restarted experiment. All time measurements in cross-arm comparisons come from the restarted run.
