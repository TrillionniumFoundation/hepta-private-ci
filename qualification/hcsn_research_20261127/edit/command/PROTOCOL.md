# Sequential external-target editing of a physical HCSN on a second English speech source

## Frozen before opening either new final panel

The parent for each seed is the first-stage direct physical prototype editor selected by development on FSDD in the adjacent protocol (including KEEP if selected). At stage two every arm for a seed starts from that identical state and keeps its original Arabic ten-way readout frozen. Speech Commands v0.02 of Google/TensorFlow supplies a separate English digit corpus with known speaker IDs. Download the official tarball at https://storage.googleapis.com/download.tensorflow.org/data/speech_commands_v0.02.tar.gz and record its published MD5, size, observed SHA-256 and exact member manifest. Extract digits zero through nine. A distinct word outside those ten is not a label for this study.

Keep official validation and testing lists, reject any speaker overlap. Map 16 kHz mono utterances to 8 kHz with polyphase resampling then compute the same MFCC and six positions as stage one. For each digit and partition, deterministically order speaker IDs and members by SHA256 of a constant salt and full strings. Take at most one recording per speaker per digit, up to 48/24/120 examples per digit for train/dev/test. Record actual sources and resample people in the final paired bootstrap. Standardize using only the new training rows. Input-domain choice and corpus identity are common to all arms and assumed known. Do not alter the original Arabic or FSDD scalers. The first-stage FSDD confirmation and the Arabic official test are historical retained panels at this stage. Selection sees only the two separate development protections. The new Speech Commands confirmation is held through sealing of candidates and all fit code.

## External label assignment methods

Editable parameters are the same 224 hidden physical sender coefficients. Measure the previous network at old Arabic calibration and new train examples. For each digit derive a physical drift prototype per slow update by averaging old labeled drifts, inverting the nonlinear drift law and fitting local preactivation corrections with an old-key penalty. One contrast per digit: use that class's new residual with all other target residuals set to zero; form ten basis directions from these fits. Complete the basis to rank 16 by fixed seeded Gaussian directions after QR. If a constructed matrix loses rank, use the retained true directions and report it. This is local physical observation from the same parent, not an external trained teacher.

KEEP; direct original physical law; black-box secant with physical basis; black-box secant with an independent random basis; same-basis first-order BP; unrestricted same-site BP. The two secant arms use actual forward input-output scores (ten final classes) to assign credit, with symmetric perturbation of norm .03 in each orthonormal direction. Build a local output derivative matrix on the same deterministic presample of 6 new/3 Arabic/3 FSDD examples per class. Fit least squares for a one-hot new objective and old score preservation (weights 1/.25/.25). Use ridge .003 and cap the full step Fröbenius norm at 1.5. Generate half and full candidates after the first step; use its full step as the center for a second directional linearization and produce half and full second-step candidates. No loss.backward, Jacobian autodiff or off-task teacher in the secant arm. The cost of every probe, local basis fit and selection is included. Ordinary and subspace BP use the full identical new train set, two Adam rates .002/.01, 96 updates per rate, snapshots at 24 and 96, new minibatch 16 + retained pairs of 8 each; squared error and old penalties match the direct method. Secants only see a subset of those labels. The direct prototype control uses the previous study's four proposal fractions.

For every primary arm allow KEEP in selection. Insist that both old-Arabic and prior English FSDD validation accuracy drops are <=.03 each. From qualified candidates maximize new accuracy, then minimize new Brier then change norm. Freeze complete candidate lists and selected models for every parent before unsealing new test. Cold reload separately for prediction. Score new accuracy, per-speaker paired contributions (bootstrap uses a paired cluster of all utterances for each speaker and pooled utterance accuracy), old forgetting and total counted time. Full edit-cost accounting includes all candidate construction, probes, comparisons and fresh-process verification. Report previous parent preparation and raw preprocessing as separate shared costs; full parent inclusion separately. If possible independently replay scored weights and verify unchanged non-writable tensors, semantic class order and source digests. Fixed seeds 107,211,431; an arm improving only a selected seed remains inconclusive.

The new-task superiority target vs KEEP requires new pooled gain >=2 pp and the 95% paired speaker bootstrap lower bound >0, with old Arabic and FSDD losses <=3 pp in every seed. The BP replacement target additionally requires new accuracy within 2 pp of the stronger selected BP control and lower total counted wall time for all seeds. A failed criterion is retained. A separate simple flattened-MFCC ridge on new labels is a quality and compute baseline with a domain-specific separate head and its costs explicitly identified; it has a different writable scope and must not be counted as a same-site match.
