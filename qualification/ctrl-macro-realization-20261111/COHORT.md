# Frozen cohort and content/control factorial

The exact source/case/seed × arm inventory exists independently of returned
results. Absent observations are UNKNOWN, explicit unstarted records remain
NOT_STARTED, and failures/cancellations cannot become zero scores.
Every factorial cell requires its own execution identity. Each control factor
uses the same policy across content modes; a policy substitution is rejected.
An unchanged learned-content hash may represent a legitimate NoUpdate.

Primary comparisons require all planned observations and complete task plus
fit/calibration/tuning costs. Incomplete observed subsets remain labelled.
Source groups receive equal weight; one-off costs use the planned denominator.
The interaction is on/on - on/off - off/on + off/off, with no confidence claim
for unseen sources or authority inferred from declared hashes and labels.
