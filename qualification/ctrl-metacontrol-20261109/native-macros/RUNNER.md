The runner executes the two explicit macros through the real `EpisodeHost` by
default. Each branch creates a fresh ordinary/reset actor with pinned weights,
control plan and runtime source closure. The initial public action budget is
identical; actual initial candidate, public feature, metadata, memory and legal
action digests must agree before a paired return is admitted. This is a checked
fresh-reset comparison, not a claim of identical hidden heaps or physical state.

READ packets are prospectively declared as original, repeated overview or seeded
noise. Those treatment names do not establish information usefulness. Noise uses
the actor's existing ROI schema and cannot be regenerated silently. Both branches
pay initialization, native execution, the shared terminal finalizer and artifact
observation. The measured wall-time tariff excludes outer collector/control-plane
overhead; money, energy and full physical cost remain unknown.

Every planned pair retains its source/split and both operation attempts. Failed
or mismatched branches retain measured elapsed time and available artifact digests;
their paired value stays null. Explicitly injected test hosts always emit fixture
evidence. No record grants owner authentication or production admission.
