# Preregistered longitudinal and mechanism evaluation

Scope: evaluation design for the existing learning/NDU/Neuron/HNMF/structural claims. This document defines data collection and decision rules,not actual future outcomes or an acceptance receipt.

## 1. Estimands and independence

Freeze an EvaluationPlan identifying objective class,task distribution,baseline,candidate,model/resource tuple,legal action set,assignment policy,observer,evaluator,selector,data splits,metric units,interval method,thresholds and multiplicity budget. Outcome definitions are controlled outside the evaluated policy. Names alone do not establish independent identities; authenticate roles and key/control chains.

A fixed-boundary ranking intervention may use a single-decision estimand. An episode policy uses a history-conditioned finite-horizon estimand with complete actions,behavior/evaluation probabilities,reward,discount,terminal/censoring and observation-generation records at every step. Document consistency,exchangeability/support assumptions; a model cannot fill missing causal support by predicting an outcome.

## 2. Dataset and calendar protocol

S0 is the qualified baseline. Collect a training interval ending at a locked event-time frontier; fit normalizers,conditional moments and policy candidates only there. Use a disjoint validation interval to choose S1. Publish a preregistered first future window starting after S1 is frozen. Its endpoint is real observation time,not a future timestamp generated by a fixture.

After the first confirmatory result,construct S2 under a newly frozen training/selection protocol and evaluate on a second later future window. Keep an old-task sentinel set and protected/OOD slices independent of tuning. At least three independently identified snapshots and two real future windows are required for the existing longitudinal claim. Window duration/cluster count is chosen by preregistered effect-size/power or precision analysis,not a magic sample minimum.

Keep dependent rows within one principal/trajectory/time cluster. Use disjoint folds for Q/V,normalizers and calibration. A final holdout cannot be reused adaptively until it is explicitly retired as confirmatory evidence and replaced. Delayed outcomes retain watermarks; pending,censored and revised outcomes remain distinct from zero reward.

## 3. Estimators and support

Report IPS,SNIPS and an appropriate doubly robust estimate with confidence intervals and support diagnostics. For finite-horizon history policies,one allowed reference recursion is D_H=terminal_value and D_t=V_hat(H_t)+rho_t*(r_t+gamma_t*D_(t+1)-Q_hat(H_t,a_t)),rho_t=pi_e/pi_b. Terminal reward accounting is explicit to prevent double counting.

Cluster uncertainty at the independent sampling unit; do not bootstrap correlated decisions as independent trials. Report cumulative ratios,depth-wise ESS,largest weight,subgroup support,censoring and omitted trajectories. Clipping/censoring correction and sequential monitoring rules are preregistered and disclose any bias or estimand change. Zero behavior propensity on an evaluation-supported action returns insufficient evidence before division.

For joint system/longitudinal claims,the ESS floor is max(400,ceil(0.1*n),all stricter registered slice/depth minima). A local HNMF floor of 200 cannot lower it. ESS does not create independent clusters or guarantee statistical power.

## 4. Decision gates

Retain the conservative canonical candidate LCB > baseline UCB gate. A paired-difference interval may be supplementary,not an unreviewed replacement. Apply every compatible threshold by stricter intersection after verifying units,scale,direction,estimand and dataset scope. A missing metric blocks the relevant claim.

Report independent task success/evidence quality,latency/tokens/CPU/memory/energy where measured,calibration/OOD,safe abstention,old-task retention,subgroups,deletion non-resurrection and rollback. NDU internal utility is a separate diagnostic and cannot substitute for fixed external task success. Safety/authority/deletion violations remain zero-tolerance regardless of score.

Repeated candidates,versions,windows and monitoring require a declared alpha budget or a fresh confirmatory holdout. Stop the experiment on authority/evaluator collision,leakage,unbounded costs,unresolved effect,failed rollback or corrupted lineage. Do not quietly lower thresholds after the first failure.

## 5. Mechanism ablations

Compare full model against deterministic/no-NDU,no-neural,no-recurrence,no-inhibition,no-homeostasis,no-eligibility,no-replay and shuffled-modulator conditions as applicable. Prompt arms include no intervention,single factors,pairs,full portfolio and fixed/learned timing. Freeze which arms address which hypotheses to avoid an unbounded factorial study.

Equalize or explicitly account for model identity,training data,training compute,foreground resource budget and tuning trials. Measure whether a mechanism improves future tasks and retention after its costs,not merely whether its state variable changes. A lesion must remove the intended mechanism without changing hidden authority or data access. Stability,calibration and energy/latency comparisons are independent outputs.

## 6. Safe continued learning and unlearning

Runtime temporal/homeostatic state may advance within its registered bounds; selected weights,code,objective and topology stay frozen for the run. Updates produce immutable next-snapshot artifacts. This supports long-term adaptation without in-place mutation of the active execution semantics.

A source deletion revokes affected datasets,normalizers,optimizer checkpoints,models,policies,prompt graphs,recall caches and backups through lineage. Mark selective parameter unlearning unsupported when it has not been demonstrated; revoke and retrain instead. Re-test restore and new-process load under current deletion/authority frontiers.

## 7. Reporting and capability ceiling

Each result binds exact source/data/code/model/host,calendar interval,independent issuer,all required metrics,failures and unsupported claims. Engineering C1 loading success,an analytic DR result or a paper approximation theorem cannot certify this experiment. A failed or inconclusive window remains visible. Functional biomimicry,self-iteration,physical capability and release advance only through their own applicable evidence and independent decisions.
