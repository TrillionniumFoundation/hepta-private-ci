# Measured within-cell event-response contrasts across waveform and dose

## Trial design, groups and primary targets

The two Allen repeated-noise recordings contain distinct imposed commands (across-family correlation near .916), repeated within their own family. Inspection of prior stimuli shows 3-second active episodes at 0, 8 and 16 seconds, approximately one, two and three times the first amplitude. Each episode contains three one-second cycles. This allows a paired intervention contrast under identical command phase without treating one stimulus label as proof of a strong distribution shift.

For training and validation use only the 24 already collected *current-only* donors from ../allen, first 18/last 6 by locked rank. Fit only Noise 1 amplitude one and two, 2/8/16 ms horizons. No high-amplitude labels or Noise 2 labels are used for model selection. Evaluate the first 48 new eligible distinct donors in the existing SHA rank excluding previous 108 and both previous cohorts in prior48.json. Collect the first two qualifying trials per waveform and the true imposed current; all outcomes of all 48 are held out until the coefficients, feature source and cold exclusions are sealed. No donor is rejected due to firing or model error. If a waveform fails to satisfy the intended triple-pulse structure, retain the donor and disclose the mismatch instead of relabeling it. Confirm pulse identity, cycle amplitudes, active windows, and extent of stimulus correlation from the commanded current alone.

In each complete 1s stimulus cycle use the 2-ms phases j=30,32,...,480 (60–960 ms), separately for cycles 0,1,2. At each phase estimate event probability from the measured binary first hit within 4 or 12 ms, averaged over the two independent sweeps and three matching cycles for each dose and family. Each approach sends one FP32 probability per query before averaging. Primary target is the within-donor difference in hit probability between amplitude three and two under Noise 2. Secondary target is the waveform contrast (Noise 2 minus Noise 1 at amplitude three). Evaluate mean squared error of predicted differences, then per-condition NLL and Brier. In addition, use the two independent-sweep contrasts d0,d1 to report mean (prediction−d0)(prediction−d1) and empirical repeat variability. This avoids squaring sampling noise under independent, equal-mean trials; ordinary paired differences between models are also recorded. Because dose order is fixed in the source, a dose effect can include history or time effects. Confidence intervals resample the 48 donors (10,000 draws, seed 855949) with equal weight. Compare only per-phase/within-donor protocol response, without a mechanistic causality or real-cell optimal-stopping identification claim.

## Senders and success criterion

Include a smooth learned current readout, a strong boosted current-only comparator and ablations with fixed, slope-matched tangent, and literal Eq12 boundaries. Preserve the regenerative 12-atom plus one refractory population in ../model.py. The primary moving-wall query uses the exact straight log-boundary diffusion transition per segment, including the slope in the relative drift. All set the predefined reserve drift gain at .7, and no outcome is used to change the hidden population. Also include a step-held literal wall forecaster. Compute true conditional stationary equity at active atoms using the original killed Green solution, and the one-year hit law, with zero contribution from refractory population. Compare feeding their two population means (plus common causal current summaries) to feeding the same means AND a future conditional susceptible-hit mass. All query augmentations receive the same common means, timestamps and causal current information. Compare equal-sized augmented fixed/tangent/step-literal/moving-literal models using the same readout family and validation budget. Record sender preprocessing and readout costs. Use a regularized logistic linear readout on shared smooth features and predeclared L2 choices; optionally show a flexible supervised boosted control with 160 iterations, 15 leaves, minimum leaf size 60 and learning rate .08. No retrospective threshold adjustment on held outcomes. All methods see identical past currents and prescribed future commands, receive no measured voltage/spike history, and return one scalar probability. FP32 per-query size excludes local state storage and externally shared schedule costs.

Gate a measured, specific advantage on the primary contrast only if moving-literal augmented contrast MSE has smaller negative upper paired 95% bound versus its fixed and tangent equivalents, the smooth comparator and boosted comparator, AND its pointwise single-condition Brier on high-dose Noise 2 does not deteriorate versus those methods. Include plain dual versus dual-plus-query as a separate sensitivity. Report the secondary contrast and noise ceiling even if negative. Public source, all fitted weights, per-donor outputs and an independently executable recount are part of the result.
