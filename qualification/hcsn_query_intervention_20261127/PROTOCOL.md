# Conditional first-spike messages under a known injected current

## Primary measured endpoint

Use previously archived public EPFL/INCF 2009 challenge A, B and C recorded responses to somatic injected current (A,B) or somatic/dendritic currents (C). Those are three distinct cells. The experimenter knows the future prescribed current trajectory. Given only prior currents, that trajectory, and a requested interval, predict whether at least one measured spike occurs in the interval on a fresh repetition. This prospective protocol uses existing public training recordings and earlier chronological partitions. These recordings appeared in earlier studies; this is a new conditional outcome and budget, not a newly recruited cohort. Subsequent per-piece analyses cannot treat repeated trials as independent cells.

Use spike events and inputs on the archival two-millisecond grid. Fit on anchor bins [100,12990), select on [13100,15490), score [15600,18990). Use identical anchor sets across arms. Fit and select on horizons 1,4,8 bins; score previously unsupervised 2,6 bins (4 and 12 ms) and report scores for the three previously supervised horizons too. All horizons end within their time partition. Score each trial's Bernoulli log loss and Brier averaged equally across the two primary horizons, time and repeats. Report separate cells. For uncertainty use 500 ms contiguous time blocks shared across arms/horizons/repeats, 5000 paired resamples. Strong primary success requires improvement in both point metrics and each two-sided 95% log loss upper bound below zero versus both the learned smooth query and fixed-boundary transport on all three cells. For literal curvature attribution compare against tangent-smooth transport on all cells separately. Receiver-query utility requires improvement over the matching transport with a held-flat future drive. Include every comparison and finite precision cost.

## Signal and controls

Every method may inspect the same causal previous current measurements and the specified upcoming current protocol. No future membrane potential, event indication or trial-specific history is available. Report known-protocol and flat-future forecasts separately. The transmitter sends exactly one IEEE FP32 probability per requested horizon. If both ends share the imposed waveform, the request can specify the horizon in one byte. If only the receiver holds the imposed currents, charge the horizon plus all future FP32 current samples (two currents at C); report both conventions. The receiver merely decodes the transmitted scalar. Baselines may do all their feature construction on the sender, including nonlinear hazard integration and training. Do not compare a queried projection with a weak linear decoder of present voltages as the sole control.

For the literal cell the time-local structural boundary uses the He–Xiong Eq. (12) Table I parameters and exogenous liquidity intensity xi=exp(1.5 tanh(drive)). Include a matched smooth boundary with identical value and first derivative at zero drive but a tangent relation between log boundary and bounded drive; compare a fixed boundary with a driven diffusion sharing the same drift and reset. In all three variants propagate a killed one-dimensional Brownian diffusion on a common positive-margin grid plus one refractory slot. At each tick extract first-hit mass, reset the previous refractory mass, and shift surviving coordinates when the boundary changes. For a query, retain the refractory mass existing at query start but do not reinject any newly hit mass before the first hit has been scored. Use the same logistic calibration of physical logit probability and log horizon with intercept. Candidate physical drift/current scales (0.35,0.70,1.40); trained calibrator penalties (0.1,1,10). Select one scale and penalty per method per cell by mean development log loss on training horizons. Calibrate separate flat-future and actual-query methods on their own features. Also evaluate the instantaneous raw log-boundary as the only sender state (with horizon calibration) and a trainable smooth hazard model. The latter fits a logistic single-bin hazard using five causal exponentially filtered currents per input, plus quadratic and saturating transforms, and forms the cumulative conditional response from successive hazards over the given waveform; use the same three penalties with all temporal supervision available in the permitted training time. A calibration layer, chosen solely on train/dev, may account for within-repetition dependence. Record both calibration and total times. For threshold-change subgroup scoring define challenging waveforms by the upper quartile of maximum absolute departure from the initial current in TRAIN at each horizon. This is a contrast of observed prescribed waveforms, not an assigned randomized pair of otherwise identical counterfactuals.

## Structural checks and scope

Independently verify killed-kernel survival against the closed-form first-hit probability, zero live mass at a crossed wall, probability conservation under regeneration, direct survival vs backward projection, positive query under an upward boundary manipulation on a frozen population, and same-byte float32 error. In an explicitly model-generated panel vary future boundary and drift separately, including interventions outside the training range. Compare exact conditional projections to source-blind fixed messages at matched outbound precision and account for query costs. Synthetic recovery of forecasts under the generating model establishes numerical validity; it cannot establish the architecture has a biological advantage. Commit source and chosen configurations before scoring the measured holdout. Archive scores, waveform/target provenance, selection, bootstrap and failures. No new raw material is required for the three published recordings.
