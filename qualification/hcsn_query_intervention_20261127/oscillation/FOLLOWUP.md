# Confirm regular flux modulation separately from exact integer periodicity

The original 132-case grid produced zero flux sequences satisfying its strict integer-return rule. Six parameter settings (g=64 or 128, h=1.5g, beta=.05 across all three matched laws) showed large late-window flux modulation. This observation is exploratory. To test it, lock the same three laws and run both signs of the physical initial perturbation for 8,192 steps, with new independently held intermediate gains 80, 96, 112 and the previously observed endpoint settings. Evaluate the last 4,096 samples separately from the original short tail. Also test g=0 as an open-loop null. For repeated, regular pulses require at least 20 interior peaks, prominence ≥.01, peak-time separation of ≥8 steps, interpeak-interval coefficient of variation <.05, global tail peak-to-peak >.02, and maximum-to-minimum amplitude ratio of the four equal tail blocks <1.10, in BOTH perturbation directions. Record the exact-integer criterion separately; passing regularity does not imply an exact integer-period orbit. Retain all settings even when one criterion fails. Record archive traces, peak times and intervals to enable independent checks. The study tests this externally designed nonlinear feedback; at fixed g,h,beta no financial or physiological parameter estimation is asserted.
