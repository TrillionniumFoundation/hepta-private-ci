# Known future current as a four-byte first-spike query

On each of three measured cells, a population with a literal He–Xiong liquidity boundary sent a single FP32 probability of at least one recorded spike by the requested endpoint. Both evaluated time windows (4 and 12 ms) were absent from training and selection. All forecast arms observed prior and, where specified, the scheduled future injected current. We used 3,390 held-out time anchors on each cell and 13/9/7 recorded repetitions respectively. Every method emitted one float per question. The eight senders were frozen at [b865d902](https://github.com/TrillionniumFoundation/hepta-private-ci/commit/b865d9022320b425fc1e563a7c12255e458c61a5) before opening these held-out scores.

The known-waveform projection improved over holding the literal future drive flat on every cell. Its log-loss advantage over the first-order matched structural threshold was .001430/.002303/.004744. The 95% paired 500-ms block intervals for literal minus tangent were [−.002082,−.000810], [−.003623,−.001097], and [−.009752,−.000715]. The nonlinear empirical hazard substantially improved over the literal cell on all three cells; the prespecified broad superiority gate failed. These within-cell intervals do not quantify generalization to all neurons. This study introduced shock intensity as a deterministic function of prescribed current. On 512 reachable finite-grid histories and 32 preprogrammed future schedules, the optimal shared one-coordinate linear encoder with an arbitrary affine decoder per query had test-design RMSE .00040581 even when fitted retrospectively to all histories and schedules. Its best rank-four and rank-seven analogues gave .000004283 and 5.87e−9. In contrast, querying the sender for each specific schedule gave the exact scalar projection to double roundoff (FP32 maximum wire error 1.49e−8). 32 separately requested values cost 128 outbound bytes; a reused four-coordinate state costs 16 bytes once. A shared finite menu can dispatch a schedule by one byte. Sending arbitrary waveforms pays one horizon byte plus four bytes per future current per step, plus the four-byte reply. Thus the beneficial protocol depends on the number and identity of distinct future questions. Three independent 400,000-path Brownian-bridge checks were within 1.16 Monte Carlo standard errors. This model-generated panel is separate from the recorded cells. The rank restriction is precise for fixed linear messages and affine per-query decoders.
