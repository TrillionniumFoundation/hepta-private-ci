# A differentiable He–Xiong equity, boundary and hitting operator at zero liquidity premium

The baseline repository module at commit fff9a90720deaf848ad30991158f2b6180e61f4f raises ZeroDivisionError at zero incremental liquidity yield. At yield 1e−14 it returns equity 4.781594 in the tested .1-log-margin state, versus the frictionless limit 4.084713. The financial value itself is regular there. The implementation in equity.py computes it and its derivatives without perturbing a zero input away from the intended model.

For width s and location l, introduce T(k,l,x)=∫ₓ∞ exp(k y) Φ((l−y)/s)dy. For k≠0 integration by parts gives [exp(kl+k²s²/2)Φ((l+ks²−x)/s)−exp(kx)Φ((l−x)/s)]/k. In the limit k=0 this equals (l−x)Φ((l−x)/s)+sφ((l−x)/s). Around zero the implementation also supplies derivatives using six Taylor orders. The coefficient of kⁿ/n! is E[(Z^(n+1)−x^(n+1))1{Z>x}]/(n+1) for Z normal with mean l and variance s², calculated using the truncated-normal moment recursion. This removes the equality-of-roots singularity in the financial formula.

The stationary equity source g_B(y)=δB exp(y)−(1−π)C+d(B exp(y);B,m)−p decomposes into constants, payout and four Gaussian first-hit terms. The Green smooth-pasting condition is ∫₀∞ e^(−ηy)g_B(y)dy=0; it is affine in B. Evaluate the Gaussian integrals with T, solve that affine equation and apply the half-line Green kernel for equity. The direct digital first-hit and discounted-first-hit readouts reuse the two image-term pairs. All computations are torch tensors in FP64; the caller supplies log(V/B) and incremental yield. For an absolute-V derivative one must also differentiate the conversion x=log(V/B).

At Table I parameters, zero premium and log margin .1, the implementation gives B=65.04840294, E=4.08471251, one-year hit probability .62103936 and discounted hit mass .60710963. Derivatives with respect to incremental yield at fixed log margin are +240.1433741, −21.3434513, 0 and −.17076136 respectively. The zero in the hit-probability derivative follows because the base GBM's drift and volatility are fixed and the margin is already specified relative to its endogenous boundary.

Eighty independent checks covered the registered small and large premia, multiple maturities and volatilities, and near-contact/interior margins. Maximum absolute discrepancies against quadrature for B, E, F and G were respectively 1.99e−13, 1.68e−12, 1.12e−16 and 2.23e−16. Maximum derivative differences scaled by max(1,absolute reference derivative) were 6.69e−11, 2.59e−9, 3.71e−11 and 1.01e−10. For finite-difference references, a one-sided fourth-order stencil was used where a centered stencil would exit the permissible domain.

On the same 81×12 crossover price table, FP64 single-thread CPU batched direct evaluation took median .01621 s across nine warm batches; independent scalar adaptive Green quadrature took median 4.59659 s over three runs. The measured ratio was 283.6, table discrepancy 3.98e−12. The median direct one-point call took .00118 s. Shared-machine scheduling produced a wide direct-batch range (.00309–.02300 s). This comparison includes vectorization and the removal of quadrature; it measures table construction and gives no foundation-model training-time or hardware-energy estimate.
