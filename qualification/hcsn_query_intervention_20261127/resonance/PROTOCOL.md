# Stable exact stationary equity and its derivative at zero rollover liquidity friction

The published stationary equity and free-boundary expressions contain divided differences such as (z−z_hat) in denominators. At q=r (zero effective rollover-liquidity premium), z_hat=z and direct evaluation can produce a removable 0/0. Small learned premia can approach this regime. Derive an equivalent stationary Green expression containing truncated-normal integral primitives, provide the finite limit together with derivatives, and test whether both channels and the free boundary permit ordinary BP there. This does not refer to the separate non-smooth extension in the initial point state at default.

Use the existing Table I risk-neutral parameters as the anchor, vary nonnegative liquidity yield increment q−r in 0, 1e−14, 1e−10, 1e−6, .001, .01, .04, .5, and run maturity/volatility stress values (.1,1,5 years and .15,.23,.40). Quantify agreement with independent adaptive Green integrals and the independent smooth-pasting root, including very small positive log margin, .02,.1,.4. Compare autograd derivatives at positive premia with central differences and at zero with one-sided differences or a high-order analytic limit; report cancellation error and tolerances. Test both the scalar Python and repository native direct formula where applicable without changing mainline. Benchmark the new direct stationary equity+boundary operator at the exact 81×12 table in crossover/features.py against independently evaluated scalar quadratures, under the same numerical precision. Include warm-up, batch versus scalar times, CPU/thread settings and precision. If computational gain exists it is relative to that numerical operation; it is not an end-to-end training speed claim. Archive numerical values, formula, verification and code.
