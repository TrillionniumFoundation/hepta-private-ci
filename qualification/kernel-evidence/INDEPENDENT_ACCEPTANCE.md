# kernel.evidence independent acceptance procedure

Independent acceptance is intentionally not generated by the implementation
branch. A distinct authorized principal must review the exact candidate and
sign the registered \`IndependentDecisionReceiptV1\`.

## Required separation

The accepting principal, service identity, signing key and delegated chain must
be distinct from the candidate generator. The role must be one of the
registered independent roles:

- \`independent_evaluator\`
- \`architecture_reviewer\`
- \`security_reviewer\`

A second display name or a second key controlled by the same principal does not
establish independence.

## Exact-candidate evidence set

The reviewer must bind the decision to the pull-request head commit/tree and
the deterministic synthetic merge candidate. The evidence set includes at
least:

- Lane A exact-source source/native execution receipts;
- Lane A synthetic-merge source/native execution receipts;
- focused \`codex-hepta-contracts\`, \`codex-hepta-evidence\` and product-host
  qualification tests;
- schema/migration and external-checkpoint recovery evidence;
- the current contract/data-authority registry projection;
- unresolved external gates and nonclaims.

Hash the canonical evidence-set manifest and place that value in
\`evidenceSetDigest\`.

## Receipt admission

The external reviewer produces a valid \`IndependentDecisionReceiptV1\` and a
matching \`QualificationEvidenceEnvelopeV1\` with
\`claimClass=independent_review\`. The envelope payload digest is the canonical
decision receipt digest. The reviewer signs the envelope with the authenticated
issuer key. The product host authenticates the issuer certificate against the
pinned trust root and current revocation head, then calls
\`append_independent_decision_receipt\`.

The store rejects candidate, role, principal, signing identity, digest or
expiry mismatch and preserves the accepted/rejected decision append-only.

## Claim boundary

Until a distinct actor completes this procedure for the exact candidate,
\`independentAcceptance\` remains false. Operator acceptance, physical target
qualification, promotion and release remain separate external states even
after an independent decision is stored.
