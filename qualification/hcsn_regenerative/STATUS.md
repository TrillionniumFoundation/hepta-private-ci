## Prospective experiment admission

[The causal package](../hcsn_causal/README.md) defines analytic/smooth kernels
crossed with fixed/feedback mechanisms. Its A/B/C/D arm names are not the
original architecture A/B names. Matching metadata alone is insufficient:
original model/asset receipts, active trainable capacity, persistent state,
data exposure, cumulative submitted training and realized tuning costs need
verification before a new comparison. The ledger verifier checks its declared
owner scope, not the absence of attempts anywhere else.

The real-media readiness source still documents a 1,536-dimensional audio
descriptor versus a legacy 55-feature interface, with only historical encoder
custody evidence. This adapter can preserve such widths but supplies no strong
checkpoint itself. Real acquisition must verify the checkpoint, common clock,
causal crop, preprocessing and complete measured cost, then evaluate the
requested interference/query-swap/OOD conditions. No proxy core, symbolic
router or adapter unit test counts as that experiment.

Backbone expansion stays conditional on those measured gains. No inference
about broad adoption, deployment reliability or large-model superiority is
licensed by the present scalar reference.
