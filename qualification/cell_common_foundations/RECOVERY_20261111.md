## Defects and repaired invariants

`LearningClock.restore` previously accepted a rehashed state in which an
8-tick row had already released at tick 1. With gradient 8 on the first tick
and gradient 1 on the next seven, the legitimate average at tick 8 is 1.875;
the accepted altered checkpoint produced 1.0. Restoration now reconstructs
the deterministic schedule from mode, periods and tick before loading pending
tensors. Pending sample counts, update counts and the round-robin cursor must
all agree. This reconstruction executes no gradient or optimizer step.

`ReplayPool.restore` previously accepted mutually impossible counters, such
as one presentation after zero selection calls and zero numerical bytes.
Restoration now requires:

```text
presentations <= 32 * calls
presentations <= timesteps <= 32 * presentations
numeric_payload_bytes == 88 * timesteps
```

The 88-byte factor follows from the validated `(n,2), (n,1), (n,), (n,7)`
FP64 arrays. Historical distinct IDs must be a duplicate-free list of bounded
nonempty strings, nonempty exactly when presentations are nonzero. Historical
IDs may have been evicted; they need not be members of the current pool.
These constraints establish local consistency, not genuine historical
admission, an externally authenticated source, or physical I/O cost.

revision. It compares uninterrupted execution with resumed execution. This
exercises the research components; it is not an Agentd/Root owner restart or
an external-effect exactly-once claim. The unrelated Torch temporal-kernel

## Research interpretation and next acceptance gates

The [cell-type plasticity study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-74869-1)
and [Nested Learning](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/4309616aaed8e848009bc4a7ef73b493-Abstract-Conference.html)
motivate heterogeneous learning responsibilities. They do not establish the
optimal software lineage count or the configured periods. Preserve both
uniform and matched-update round-robin controls. Count event-state writes,
parameter updates and version adoption separately.

The [dendritic teaching-signal study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10190-7)
motivates local credit experiments. Correct and shuffled routing must receive
the same information; extra local labels, teacher calls and derivative work
must be counted. A vector carrying extra target information is not an
information-matched comparison with terminal scalar reward.

The [PNAS diffusion study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13598169/)
primarily varies sparse feedback connectivity. Keep feedback connection
coverage, reward-event frequency and delivery delay as separate factors.
Sequence horizon is not itself feedback delivery delay. Local diffusion
must be compared with shuffled diffusion and a simple direct reference under
the same gain normalization and resource budget. The prior eight-stratum
development result did not demonstrate a diffusion advantage.

[Independent procedural replay](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-026-02362-5)
motivates testing separate replay mechanisms; it does not establish the four
software strata. Retain uniform replay, exact-key retrieval and fixed skills.
The [continual-agent preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.25858) motivates
conflicting-workflow and old-task tests. Do not infer preservation from the
aggregate new-task mean or treat obsolete, revoked targets as valid retention.

Scientific acceptance remains **HOLD_NO_GO** and production adoption remains
**not granted**. Independently sourced prospective tasks, matched full costs,
frozen evaluation B, original owner NoUpdate/Adopt, distinct task C, and native
owner cold recovery remain separate gates. Passing recovery or reference
tests supplies none of those missing observations.
