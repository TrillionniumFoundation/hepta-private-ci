# ECB reference-rate first passage at observed daily fixings

## Question and outcome
Does a physically calibrated first-passage law predict a real diffusion-like event more accurately than simple conditional frequency, historical paths and conventional encoders? Does replacing a conventional encoder by the literal He–Xiong equity/boundary/first-hit encoder improve out-of-horizon Brier? The source is the official ECB historical euro exchange-rate CSV archive, not an executable trading quote. The analysis concerns forecasting under the observed probability law. Rates are quoted as foreign-currency units per euro. At each anchor include exactly three lower log barriers m ∈ {0.005,0.01,0.02}. Set barriers exp(−m) times the anchor rate. The outcome is the first subsequently published daily fixing at or below that level, within 1,3,6,12,24 future published reference dates. Intraday crossings are unobserved. Brownian continuous monitoring and discrete monitoring consequently have distinct hypotheses. No future volatility may enter a predictor.

## Source separation and cohort
Use complete positive quote histories from the official file for USD, GBP, JPY, CHF, CAD, AUD, NOK and SEK. Disregard all observations beyond 2025-12-31. Anchor on every fifth file date, global offset zero counting from the earliest available row, common across currencies. Require a complete contiguous history for the prior 300 publication dates and the next 24 publication dates for that currency; omit gaps and record counts. Use USD/GBP/JPY/CHF anchors from 2001-01-01 through 2013-12-31 to train. Use those same currencies 2014-01-01 through 2018-12-31 for development. No label may extend past the end of its own date period. Use 2019-01-01 through 2025-12-31 for two simultaneous sealed panels, four original currencies and entirely withheld CAD/AUD/NOK/SEK. Historical past quotes from test currencies may be used for causal features and local naive forecasts, never for shared fitting or global model selection. Calendar-only separation with shared EUR factor exists between the panels. Date ranges, source sets, barriers and planned models remain fixed even if candidate support is poor.

## Causal features and classical comparators
At each of the eight most recent ticks use that tick's own log return / trailing 20-tick realized RMS return, 5/20 and 20/60 RMS vol ratios, mean previous 5 returns divided by RMS20, fraction negative in trailing 20 returns, signed drawdown from trailing 20 prices divided by RMS20, and specified barrier divided by RMS20. RMS floor 0.0001. Train-only standardized/clipped channels and current dimensionless gap min(3,m/(4*RMS20)) are common inputs to each learned arm. No pulse is inferred from an unavailable high/low. The raw history accessible to each model is identical, engineered comparators summarize up to 300 quotes.

1. Previous quote unchanged, hence no future hit; unconditional training event curve by barrier with one positive and one negative pseudocount; volatility-conditioned empirical curve using training quartile/octile/16-quantile or 32-quantile bins of m/RMS20 crossed with sign of recent 5-return average. Shrink conditional rates toward all-training mean using 8 pseudo-rows. Interpolate the cumulative hazard at 3 and 12 using only 1/6/24 outcomes. Choose bin count on trained-horizon development Brier.
2. Historical-path empirical. Use the most recent 252 prior complete 24-return paths ending no later than the current observation. Standardize each past path by trailing 20-return RMS available at that path's start and rescale to current trailing RMS. Obtain daily fixing crossing probabilities from the empirical CDF of running minima. Add one positive and one negative pseudo-observation.
3. Driftless Brownian continuous first hit with distance m, local RMS volatility; Gaussian random walk with discrete daily observations calibrated to that same volatility. Freeze 262,144 length-24 standard Gaussian paths with NumPy seed 783401 and common random numbers; store sampling error bound. Each model selects a common volatility multiplier from {0.75,1,1.25} and lookback from {5,20,60} using trained-horizon development Brier; also report raw untuned 20-day value. For both mechanisms the canonical formula is the driftless member of the hitting-law family. The literal-cell and GRU Brownian heads provide learned drift alternatives.
4. Reuse the repaired frozen survival program's N8 literal financial cell with Brownian and Weibull heads; GRU16 Brownian/Weibull/piecewise and smooth16 Brownian/Weibull controls. All use identical random minibatch indices, seeds 83,181,283, 384 Adam updates (.003), batch 64, one CPU thread per model, clip norm 1. Evaluate development at 0,96,192,288,384 and select earliest minimum mean Brier on 1/6/24 only. Fit each seed once. Retain checkpoint, learned parameter count, costs, predictions and failures. External FX barriers are fixed by the observed task; the literal neuron has its own internally computed He–Xiong boundary. Both are recorded as different objects.

## Endpoints and decision
Write all fitted model states plus selected calibrations before scoring either held-out panel. Primary endpoint is observation-weighted average Brier at the two withheld horizons 3 and 12, equal barrier/observation weighting. For each panel compare literal FHT with the strongest conventional/classical model by trained-horizon development loss, and test daily Brownian sampling against continuous monitoring using the corresponding independently development-selected versions and the matched raw-20-day versions. Separately compare GRU FHT versus GRU Weibull. Report all arms, per barrier/currency and horizon, AUC, calibration and process cost. Average seeds at loss level then take paired effects. Use 2,000 independent moving-block draws of three consecutive calendar months with replacement and shared month indices across currencies and barriers; resample to the number of test months, seed 783409. These are conditional descriptive intervals. Require literal-minus-conventional upper CI below zero in both panels for a physical-encoder advantage; discrete-minus-continuous upper CI below zero in both panels at matched volatility for the daily-monitoring hypothesis. No intraday, causal-trading or financial valuation claims. A subsequent paid decision task or general HCSN advantage would require its own evidence.
