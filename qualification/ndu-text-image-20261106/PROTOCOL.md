## Unified numerical and external objectives

Select and pin one risk profile for each actual comparison. #4875's finite
nested entropic objective includes policy randomness in the risk lottery,
immediate action cost, duration discount, and STOP semantics. V3 population
entropic aggregation is a different objective. Verify the actual runner's
preference schedule, budget boundaries, failure penalty, gradients, and optional
stopping against the selected reference. The current probe executes neither
control objective.

The external endpoint must be fixed independently of learned internal preferences.
Use the preregistered normalized task rubric and a common physical-cost unit.
The deployment owner must supply a defensible conversion and cost_weight;
this plan does not invent a monetary/energy price or treat CPU time as free.
Predeclare net_margin and retention_margin rather than choosing them after the
canary or validation gains. The example's margins and cost units are synthetic
software inputs, not defaults adopted for real science.

## Costs, sample size, and progression

Register encoder, shared, decoder, critic, planning, codec, failure, retry and
synchronization as disjoint categories. Include work performed before initial
delivery, unsuccessful attempts, and waiting/synchronization that affects latency.
Record wall time, peak memory, available energy measurements, and actual charges.
Null is never a measured zero.

Record training, search and evaluation separately, including unsuccessful runs.
Freeze training/search receipts when locking the comparator. Discuss amortization
at predeclared deployment frequencies, with common setup allocated consistently.
Avoid double-counting evaluation inference already in the research ledger.

The auditor requires at least 20 independent clusters per validation/test cohort
and complete terminal rows for all 16 profiles. This lower bound is not a power
analysis. Determine the real sample size from a prospectively chosen effect,
variance estimate and multiplicity policy. Protect the confirmation split even
when a pilot result is disappointing.

The primary benefit decision compares to the validation-locked strongest
deployable policy. The simultaneous net-gain lower bound must exceed the minimum
useful effect, evidence and cost must be complete, and retention must qualify.
Report subgroup/ability regressions rather than relying solely on average
retention. Preserve all four factorial effects and uncertainty regardless of
their direction.
