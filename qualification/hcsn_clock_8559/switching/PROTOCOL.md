# Endogenous two-regime boundary with finite-maturity issuance prices

The earlier separately solved regime-switching neuron replaced finite-maturity issuance by a running cost. This experiment keeps the original paper's rolling debt inventory in the feedback loop. A regime-independent diffusion of log fundamental state has original drift and volatility. The observed liquidity regime switches from 0 to 1 and back. The sale intensity times haircut is lambda_i. A default can result from the continuous diffusion or a regime jump. Recovery on a jump is alpha times the actual fundamental value at that instant, divided by the debt maturity. This is an explicit recovery convention for the extension.

For trial stopping sets, price the new bond at its actual remaining maturity with state-dependent discount r+lambda_i, coupon c, and terminal principal p. Use that price in the *equity* stopping objective with flow delta V − (1−tax) C + d_i(V,m) − p. Solve the equity obstacle and iterate stopping sets with the newly priced issued debt. Check for multiple discrete fixed points and record sensitivity to initialization rather than treating convergence as a uniqueness proof. The original paper remains the constant-regime control.

Freeze financial and physical coefficients at r=.08, delta=.02, sigma=.23, tax=.27, alpha=.6, C=6.39, P=61.68, maturity=1. Primary regimes lambda=(.01,.04), switching rates (.12,.16). Controls set switching to zero or set both frictions to .01. Report both stopped boundaries, values and hitting/switch recovery contributions, maximum obstacle error, discrete policy consistency, and corresponding single-regime analytic boundaries. Evaluate grid and time refinement and domain sensitivity, with monotone spatial generator and full finite-horizon pricing. Preserve all tested starting boundaries and failures. Independent Monte Carlo or a different time integrator should validate conditional price values; a held-out learning gain remains separate. If the discrete root is not identifiable or smooth across parameters, do not claim an ordinary gradient of a quantized stopping index.
