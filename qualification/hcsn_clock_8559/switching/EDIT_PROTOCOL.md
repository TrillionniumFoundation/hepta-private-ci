# Editing a fitted equilibrium while preserving other observables

Test whether the finance-derived neural operator itself admits a direct finite policy write. Start at λ=(0.01,0.04), rates=(0.12,0.16), maturity one. The neural message in this trial is the vector of two conditional endogenous barriers. Form three target changes to one or both boundaries, (−0.05,+0.05), (+0.10,0), and (0,+0.10), in fundamental-value units. Also demand exact retention of both pre-edit equity values at V=100. Keep all four rates within admissible bounds and preserve ordered frictions. Compare unchanged, minimum Euclidean norm scaled first-order wall edit, joint first-order wall/retention edit and a fully resolved nonlinear finite edit. Warm and cold cost includes rebuilding bonds/equity and solving for barriers. No task labels, pretrained model, reverse pass through a sequence or external training dataset enter this experiment.

Write the four target residuals using the desired walls directly. The first two residuals are equity smooth fit at the chosen stopping boundaries. The last two are the differences between edited and old equity at 100. Use bounded least squares to obtain an admissible calibrated candidate. Independently re-solve the resulting equilibrium from the normal forward operator and evaluate both the constraints and out-of-constraint equity/debt at V=75,90,120,140. If exact local matching is impossible or ill-conditioned, publish the residual and parameter proximity to bounds. Recheck on a finer independent grid, using its own unedited operating point. Targets are illustrative and do not establish an end-to-end training claim.
