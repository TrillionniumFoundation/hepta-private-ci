# Physical-clock refinement of a learned receiver, at frozen SHD weights

The repository has already proved that time-only refinement of a projected diffusion on fixed spatial atoms can suppress boundary hitting and checked constant forcing numerically. This study asks a different empirical question. Take already sealed learned two-layer SHD models, at the early and final original training budgets as soon as the respective panels are frozen. Rerun them on the identical original test recordings without learning, at 50, 25 and 12.5 milliseconds. Use both a duplicated piecewise-constant neural input (so the physical driving path is exactly the same) and re-binned original cochlear events (50-ms-equivalent rates before log1p and the original training means). The data clocks and causal input normalization are named separately.

Recompute the four-atom physical kernel at the actual time step and retain the same pricing horizon and the original learned parameters. Explicitly compare (a) the unmodified one-step absolute refractory implementation and (b) a delay pipeline holding the nominal 50-ms refractory duration fixed. For each input and seed keep held-out logits and first/second-layer integrated emitted flux across all windows and units. Average the readout over the same four time quarters and weight each subinterval equally. The risk probes remain 50/100/200 ms. All drift, principal, feedback and recurrent input coefficients remain those of the frozen discrete-time network. A discretized feedback controller is allowed to change with update cadence in this diagnostic; a measured difference cannot uniquely be assigned to fixed-atom diffusion. A fixed-drive one-layer constant forcing control separates this contribution.

Check that the 50-ms reimplementation agrees with stored logits before interpreting a changed clock. Report speaker-macro accuracy, NLL, per-speaker differences, output disagreement, total flux differences and per-seed distributions. For a numerical-invariance claim, require at most 1% changed labels and at most two points change in speaker-macro accuracy under exact repeated 25-ms inputs, for all three seeds. Observe two finer clocks before claiming convergence. These diagnostic thresholds describe a time-refinement property and are independent of the original recognition gate.
