# Independent teacher competence and cold student inheritance

Lock on 2026-11-23 before any teacher forwards. This controlled English-language experiment is independent of the six repository software patches in the existing E3 readout. The original teachers' restricted outputs are excluded.

- Teachers: Apache-2.0 HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM2-135M-Instruct at 12fd25f77366fa6b3b4b768ec3050bf629380bac and google/flan-t5-small at 0fc9ddf78a1e988dac52e2dac162b0ede4fd74ab. Use local original tokenizer and safetensors, official chat template on the causal model, text input on T5. Compare first-token A/B log probabilities for both orders of Yes/No; choose Yes on positive order-corrected margin. No prompts after discovering scores will be changed. This measures constrained-choice inference.
- Freeze panel.py, whose canonical 460-row generated panel has SHA256 5bae588836fd72c8aca79351bacbef87929605698de2bd1e921a268017371835. There are 432 benchmark items, 16 rehearsal and 12 old-skill evaluation items. Exact question texts are unique; lexical templates, concepts and individual words can recur. These are authored families, with no human participant. Splits per family are discovery 12, training 32, new paraphrase 20, distinct composition component 8. Positive and negative truth are balanced.
- Discovery criterion for each teacher: outperform the other within a family, score at least 7/12 and have at least two unique successes. Confirm observed family advantages on the 20 held-out items; publish every family. Best single teacher arm selected on pooled discovery accuracy (T5 first on ties). Fitting sees only the two teachers' hard answers on TRAIN. All students share the same initial parent and old rehearsal. The frozen baseline and single-teacher, equally mixed, fixed routed and learned sparse routed students are all retained even if unsuccessful.
- Students are independently reloadable lightweight regularized linear text classifiers using an owned fitted token/character n-gram vocabulary. Use equal feature, ridge penalty (0.20), task count and old capability rehearsal per expert; mixed target averages the two labels. Two-expert graphs hold the two single-teacher students. The fixed graph chooses by disclosed task family using discovery-only teacher accuracy. The top-1 learned gate takes only question text and uses discovery to prefer the correct teacher on disagreements. Ties favor T5. Record teachers' labels and supervision usage; mixture and routing have access to two teacher streams, routing stores two specialist models and has selector overhead.
- Primary readouts: per-family and macro exact accuracy, worst family, unique wins and losses, and retained old capability. Cross-family composition asks for both answers to a pair of constituent questions; combine the 48 separately reserved components into 24 disjoint cross-family pairs without access to answers. No cross-family item is used to select a router. Report exact pair success and any pre-existing limitations in coordination.
- Freeze models, vocabulary and routing before teacher evaluation on held-out and composition items. Run a fresh student-only process with no teacher files loaded or contacted. Report environment, inference/training/serving wall and process CPU time. All programmatic evidence and bundle are research records. Independent users, downstream adoption, settlement and general language-model capability require other experiments.
