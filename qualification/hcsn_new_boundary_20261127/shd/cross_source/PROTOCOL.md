## Rationale and operators
For upper nonlinear HCSN receivers followed immediately by pooled linear task readout, total class logits equal a sum of independent receiver contributions. Once the lower activations, class readout and biases are frozen, changing one column of the actual upper input matrix changes one summand. One may evaluate the exact response of each neuron to signed perturbations of a shared synaptic direction simultaneously, recovering their separate logit contributions algebraically. With k input directions and W receivers this estimates kW coordinate responses using two vectorized receiver evaluations per direction. It does not apply to layers separated from logits by arbitrary unknown nonlinear mixing or to coupled recurrence in that upper hidden state. Appropriately unpruned cold evaluation should equal the recomposed response; test FP32/FP64 differences and charged preparation.
