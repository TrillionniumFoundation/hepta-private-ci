# Data and attribution

Steven R. Livingstone and Frank A. Russo (2018), *The Ryerson Audio-Visual Database of Emotional Speech and Song (RAVDESS)*. Original data [Zenodo, 10.5281/zenodo.1188976](https://zenodo.org/records/1188976). Paper [PLOS ONE](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0196391). The source recordings are distributed for academic and other noncommercial uses under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). See also the authors’ [licensing description](https://affectivedatascience.com/licensing).

The accompanying inputs preserve only prefix-level summaries and scores against two gallery recordings, query/gallery identifiers, time, labels and integrity receipts. The transformation uses a frozen causal audio/video feature extractor documented in the implementation. Four queries from one actor/expression were quarantined before model scoring because the source has a byte-identical pair under two names. The uncorrected retention shard and original extraction sources in the historical folder document the provenance of the correction. Reproduction that accesses the recordings uses the authors' original distribution and terms.
