# Spike-train likelihood during recorded Allen current ramps

Select public Allen Cell Types Database sweeps named Ramp, with recorded stimulus, Allen-estimated spike times, sample rates and documented observation end. Exclude all known identifiers from previous 72- and 36-cell long square and 24-cell noise cohorts. Sort remaining cells by SHA256 of hepta-9de7-ramp-v1/ followed by decimal specimen ID. Require at least two usable sweeps, keep up to three ascending sweep numbers per cell. Keep the first 24 eligible specimens. The first 12 cells undergo estimation, next four select the time constants/shape for each model, final eight receive no fitting. The new episode is a distinct stimulus family. Record all attempts and original identifiers. Hardware or transfer failure must be reported and must not select on outcome.

Time 0 is the first sample of the documented imposed ramp. Use the common valid portion of the official experiment epoch, measured current and recorded voltage extents, at most the first 30 seconds. Exclude the calibration pulse. Retain the actually recorded current waveform in pA and every Allen-provided spike time inside observation, measured in seconds; preserve same-bin multiplicity and reports. Archive hashes of full NWB and the extracted arrays. If a legitimate zero-spike sweep has an explicit empty recorded spike-time dataset, score its full survival. Distinguish an absent spike-time dataset from an explicitly empty one. Use 2-ms bins; the Bernoulli outcome is one or more spikes. Verify per-sweep physical current units and compare source rates.

The output is the conditional probability of an occupied next bin given the current prescribed for that bin and earlier observed spikes. Voltage after onset is unavailable to any predictor. The primary score is Bernoulli negative log likelihood per observed second, averaged within specimen and across the eight test specimens. Also report per-bin NLL, Brier score, pooled bits per occupied bin versus a train-rate constant, and censored first-spike log score from the product of no-event probabilities up to first event or sweep termination. Show actual number of zero-spike sweeps, observed duration, all spikes, occupied bins, and duplicate bin events. Preserve all raw probabilities and all trial-level scores.

The financial candidate uses a killed Brownian four-atom population, a boundary that moves with bounded current drive, the stationary He–Xiong financial price law, positive equity restoration toward reset and loss feedback toward absorption. At each step condition on survival when no spike is observed. On an observed spike, reset and impose one 2-ms refractory bin. Report model time explicitly: base price/risk dynamics use one 0.25 model-time step per 2 physiological milliseconds. Competing ablations have no price force, only equity or only loss with identical boundaries and readouts. Use shared trainable scalar calibration of the precomputed conditional hazard in each financial arm. Tune bounded current-drive scales on development from 50, 100, 200 pA. Input drive tanh(I/scale) uses physical units, without held-cell calibration.

A stochastic LIF with exponential-escape threshold has leaky integration of recorded current, observed-event reset, one refractory bin, and an exponential intensity fitted to the integrated membrane. Select membrane constants from 5, 15 and 40 ms. A conventional smooth point-process GLM receives current, 5, 20 and 80ms filtered currents, and causal spike-history filters. Learn its parameters only on training. Compare an intercept-only train-rate model. Use the same conditional observation and scoring convention across rivals, including same refractory handling. Numerical clipping of probabilities to [1e-10,1−1e-10] is reported. For each financial model evaluate both restored and un-restored channels under the identical physical scale options.

Select one variant per named arm by specimen-balanced development NLL, break ties in the order listed. Refit selected specifications on the 16 development-exposed specimens; freeze selected parameters, exact input and output source hashes before scoring the held cells. Use 10,000 specimen-paired bootstrap draws (seed 950183) for the differences from LIF, GLM, and the three price ablations. Report them without multiplicity correction. Primary directional-finance superiority requires the upper 95% limit below zero versus both conventional baselines and the no-price ablation, alongside lower mean Brier. All curves and adverse or neutral outcomes are retained; this is a forecasting study and can proceed regardless of sign.
