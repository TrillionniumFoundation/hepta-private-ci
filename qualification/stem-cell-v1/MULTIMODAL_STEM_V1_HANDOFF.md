# V1 source-bound multimodal Stem/Cell research (non-authorizing)

**Frozen base:** `main@f8c9419df6ddeb36ff8b522a50219981fb8d9345`.

## What physically exists

- A stdlib-only, deterministic, source/feature-envelope checker for text, image, audio and video.
- Mandatory SHA256 identities: original and feature bytes, Encoder, tokenizer, preprocessor, quantization and projection.
- Principal/purpose/generation/epoch/source and modality coordinates are included in cache identity.
- Binary feature digest verification, 384D/256-slot limits, audio/video timestamps, image regions and top-32 Cell selection.
- Per-Cell independent learned-weight *interface* and deterministic linear score, with no pretrained Cell weight and no optimizer.
- Read-only split outcome arithmetic requiring 200 groups and returning unconditional `DENY_ALL`.
- Source-only adversarial tests; no external model material or labels.

## Explicit scientific and operational nonclaims

This is **NOT** a pretrained Encoder forward, authentic issuer attestation, multimodal learning, multimodal generation, Q24 Rust feature admission, production inference, independent source validation, trained Head evaluation, real observed CPU/RSS/energy, safe cross-owner caching, NDU efficacy or Cell Split execution. The ledger now calls this counter `unique_feature_admissions`: a *logical count of distinct admitted envelopes*, never actual Encoder forward executions or proven saved compute. The misleading `physical_forward_claims` field has been removed. No `main`, model selectors, CNS/Control/FinalUse, WAL, authority or external effects are modified.
