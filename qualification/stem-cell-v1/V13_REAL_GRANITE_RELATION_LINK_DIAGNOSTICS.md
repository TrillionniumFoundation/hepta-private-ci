This suggests that the **explicit evidence candidate-selection / entity-slot / multi-hop state-composition machinery** is the next primary research bottleneck; merely increasing the shared Encoder is not supported by these results.

## Explicit limitations

1. The V13 recognizer gives the model a PERFECT public-grammar operation schema, starting-entity lookup, two candidates per hop, and destination relation parsing. This is **not end-to-end neural language reasoning or a deployed general graph engine**. This oracle is a material advantage over original V10 Reader.
2. Cohorts are synthetic, not natural human data, not prospective. There are **only 120 original independent source groups** in each held role; 1,440 stage choices are correlated within source and seeds are repeated evaluations. Some depth-specific known-vocabulary results are much weaker than the aggregate.
3. Frozen mean cosine on novel links beats every 256-step learned pair probe, so there is NO new evidence that extra training is worth its cost here. The symbolic grammar parser remains stronger at effectively zero learned parameters.
4. This is exploratory same-day diagnosis, NOT a preregistered full-answer trial, not independent external science certification, not real multimodal evaluation or evidence for NDU/FBSDE value.
5. Original V7 joint retention / natural-task gates remain failed. Authority is DENY_ALL; zero model-selection or FinalUse rights.
