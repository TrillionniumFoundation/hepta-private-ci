## Hypothesis

A shared frozen Encoder is a cost mechanism, not automatically a learning gain. Test `no_change`, `linear`, `gated_residual`, `continued_single`, and `split_router` against *identical paired source/case records*. Keep the strong cheap no-change baseline. Never grant CellSplit merely because a specialist wins on its own training examples.

## Strict protocol

Original model/source material and registration must be fixed before any future outcome. Independent publisher custody, human semantic outcomes, two prospective calendar windows, old-task retention and full target-host costs remain external obligations. Per-root paired inference is descriptive until independence and statistical thresholds are independently verified. The schema includes *all* lifecycle CPU phases (encoder, cache, train, inference, router, Reader, evaluator, persistence, recovery), avoiding free pilots and hidden replay. Those CPU seconds are **not** a monetary price, GPU energy or NDU utility.

The auditor refuses incomplete or mismatched experimental arms, malformed SHA256, altered case/root/cohort identities, duplicate observations, omitted cost phases, non-finite cost and attempts to claim authorization. Its output always says `DENY_ALL_UNATTESTED_RESEARCH`, even if some candidate gains future correct cases. Row IDs or hashes are not signatures. The local test fixture is synthetic; no new real pretrained forward, real learning task or independent reviewer is claimed.
