# cognitive.types adversarial audit, 2026-10-02

## Scope and documentation

Reviewed canonical draft PR #1134 at `a300c5d544eb05f0e1626d609a5770b546686d20`,
its actual type/codec/context/consumer implementations and qualification runners.
The module has a detailed 17-section technical guide, an execution dossier,
invariant and consumer registries, migration/qualification supplements and a
current-state ledger. The current canonical development plan is V8.0.0 with the
V8.2 readiness overlay. Documentation existence is not product completion.

This is a stateless contract module depending on platform.types. It must not own
SQL, daemon state or write authority. The five consumer bindings cover
cognitive.read, cognitive.store, memory.retrieval, compact.engine and
intelligence.control. Canonical shadow/parity and full authenticated default
consumer convergence remain distinct states.

## Findings and repairs

1. Final-use context identity omitted memory, learning and deletion frontiers.
   Independently valid owner cuts could produce an identical context digest.
   Full owner-cut equality already rejected replay after changes, and no product
   caller of this new contextual proof was found. The fix binds the complete cut
   under explicit digest framing v3; it preserves Shared Experience V2 DTOs,
   historical canonical wire bytes and both canonical V1 digest profiles.
   Native field-sensitivity, replay and independent 361-byte golden framing
   regressions cover the correction. Historical v2 context digests cannot be
   upgraded by relabelling; retained observations must be revalidated/rehashed.
2. The standalone fuzz workspace lacked a committed Cargo.lock. Historical
   [native qualification artifact](https://github.com/TrillionniumFoundation/hepta-private-ci/actions/runs/36796263906/artifacts/11137946477)
   reports every command passed but `clean-tree` failed on the generated,
   untracked fuzz lock. The [depth fuzz job](https://github.com/TrillionniumFoundation/hepta-private-ci/actions/runs/36796263552/job/110174916464)
   fails at `chmod` of that absent file, before the campaign. The actual generated
   lock is now retained and the native check requires `--locked`. Check-plan v5
   also requires the new independent context vector; old plan receipts reject.
   Clean-source and evidence gates are not relaxed.

## Completion and remaining gates

After the scoped repairs, review of strict integer JSON/canonical re-encoding,
byte budgets, immutable checked wrappers, paired digest reuse, logical identity,
recall resource lower bounds, transitions and consumer profile/current-binding
checks found no additional demonstrated local defect in the examined paths.
This is a bounded audit result, not a universal “no optimization possible” claim.

The module remains short of product completion: current exact-head/synthetic
merge and OS receipts, sustained fuzz/allocation evidence, all authenticated
physical final-use paths, revocation/epoch/restart races, cross-host experience
transport, influence removal, compatibility retirement and independent acceptance
remain separate obligations. No completion flag is promoted.

Historical owner qualification also failed strict Clippy in codex-shell-command
and the cognitive_store_product_writer recovery-anchor assertion. Those are
outside this type-local repair and are not represented as fixed by it. Historical
CI and the successful local tests do not establish that the final review head's
whole repository CI passes. Keep the PR draft; no merge or deployment is included.
