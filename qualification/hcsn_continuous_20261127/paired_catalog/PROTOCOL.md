# From-scratch paired product retrieval
The photograph and productDisplayName of each Fashion Product Images (Small) product form a real image–title pair. Freeze public packaging revision and file SHA256. Use raw thumbnails and tokenized titles. Other structured metadata are withheld from the encoders. Form connected components of identical normalized titles or identical resized RGB pixels before splitting components by deterministic hash into 80/10/10 train/development/final. Report actual group counts, near-duplicate diagnostics and exact-pair versus duplicate-tolerant recall. Fit vocabulary, scaling and cross-modal covariance only on training. Gallery is independently hash-sampled up to 5,000 products per split. All comparative decisions use development. Test once under frozen selection.
Compare a regularized linear CCA, random physical hidden features, supervised forward-constructed two-layer physical E/L features, identical slope-matched smooth features, physical contrastive BP and conventional strong convolution/Transformer contrastive BP. All models see the same pictures and titles. Raw patch moments and lexical encoding can be shared when comparing mechanisms. Record the distinct strength and token counts for untied strong models. Report both retrieval directions and R@1/5/10, equal-exposure and equal-time measurements, inclusive processing, hardware, rank, model sizes, full search and per-selected config costs. Non-inferiority margin is −2 pp in average directional R@1 at paired 95% confidence. Same-quality speed claim also needs at least 20% lower end-to-end wall, and reliably improved recall over an identically dimensioned random hidden basis. Training and acquisition failures, zero-length rows, censored endpoints and failed contrasts stay visible. This data supports product-domain pairing and cannot alone establish general multimodal pretraining superiority.
