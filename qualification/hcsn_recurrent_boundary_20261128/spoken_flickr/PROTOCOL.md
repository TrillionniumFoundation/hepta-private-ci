# Three-way generation on natural photos, speech and descriptions

Join the University of Illinois Flickr8k photos and numbered descriptions to the MIT Flickr Audio Caption Corpus by original Flickr image identifier and numeric caption index. Each spoken utterance and transcript describes the same photo. The earlier CREMA-D decoder has only twelve transcript types. This study adds natural language variation and speakers reading natural image descriptions. MIT distributes the speech recordings under CC BY-SA. Preserve source attribution and hashes. Keep raw media at the compute host and publish the experimental representation, evaluation evidence and code.

Use the published train/dev/test photo lists. Remove identical image-content hashes across splits, preferring test then development, then earlier train occurrence. Record every exclusion and unmatched member. Fit all quantizers and the vocabulary on training examples only. A photo is a 4×4 sequence of patches, a speech utterance is sixteen temporal slices of spectral energy, and the text spans the whole official reference up to a predeclared 48-word capacity (including end). Publish the fraction shortened and unknown-word rates per split. Quantize a multidimensional patch or spectral slice to a learned 64-code dictionary per modality with fixed training-only samples and seeds. Use frequent training words with occurrence count at least three and a maximum 4,096-word vocabulary. Neither learned representation nor task input is a full-resolution photo or waveform.

Generate camera patch codes from speech and text, acoustic codes from photo and text, and the full word sequence from speech and photo. Encode two conditioning modalities followed by the target start mark; teacher-forced and autonomous decoding use a strictly causal network. Compute head probabilities within the known target modality. No image identifier, speaker identity or truth token at the current target position enters the model. Use re-shuffled complete triples (three target modes per caption), common initialization-seed labels and optimization. The word embedding, causal blocks and output heads are shared by all three tasks.

Compare two financial recurrent blocks with a sole emitted endogenous log boundary against two same-width smooth controls, two hard LIF blocks, and near-parameter-matched full causal attention. Width 48 for recurrent models. Select attention width using parameter counts before training. Three seeds 137, 311 and 557. Save model parameters and development metrics at one epoch, three epochs and 600 seconds of actual updating per arm, including the first update to cross the time threshold. Freeze development selections across all 12 models before opening the held split. If the device or input limits an arm, report actual coverage. Use corpus target-token NLL separately by modality, autonomous exact word sequence and token edit distance for words, and generated-code accuracy for the two coded observations. Compare matched inputs with cyclically mismatched examples in each split and with the inter-block wire ablated. Pair uncertainty by underlying held photo; the five descriptions share an image. Report clocks, selection cost, memory, device and stream-caching assumptions. Keep one-pass and fixed-time comparisons separate.
