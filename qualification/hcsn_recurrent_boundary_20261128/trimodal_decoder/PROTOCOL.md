# Three-target generative model with shared weights on aligned real recordings

Use all 7,442 CREMA-D recordings from the already-studied actor-disjoint split (4,984 / 1,147 / 1,311). Reuse the authenticated low-bandwidth observed audio and visual nibble codes, 4 frames times 4 features per modality, and the corpus maintainers' 12 spoken transcripts identified by the recording IDs. No emotion or actor labels, utterance IDs as text tokens, target modality features in the conditioning prefix, or hidden modality beyond the three streams. Learn a single conditional generative model on three tasks per clip: audio codes from visual codes and words; visual codes from audio codes and words; and transcript words from visual and audio codes. Training uses next-token cross-entropy over a shared vocabulary. The targets include one end token. Normal text words come from the training split. These are finite scripted sentences and low-bandwidth objective features, not speech waveform, video pixel or unrestricted language generation.

Use two layers in each from-scratch arm. The financial arm may communicate only through the per-unit normalized log of actual endogenous debt boundary (the normalization is fixed). Quote both original contract channels and first-hit probability at fixed physical asset using an input-proposed contract, accumulate a strictly signed restoring / loss force with first-hit-dependent memory, and emit the true boundary of the resulting driven contract. This deliberately defines a discrete neural encoder/decode cell; the population PDE stability theorem applies to a separate continuous construction. Compare with a matched nonfinancial smooth arm, a hard-spike LIF with surrogate training, a small genuine causal SDPA Transformer with nearest parameter count, and a same-width SDPA reference. All share task conditioning, positional embeddings, tokenizer, unmasked softmax across modalities, causal prefix and identical train ordinals. Do not mask source token *inputs*. Mask all losses preceding the target. Hold text words together as one modality, and use unambiguous separate audio and visual token ranges. Freeze maximum text generation length at the largest training transcript length plus two. AV emits up to 16 symbols, penalizing early stopping and invalid outputs for any remaining positions. Report true teacher-forced NLL by target and unmasked free-running greedy token accuracy (and exact match for the transcript). For financial models ablate the one communicating wire after training, thus checking for bypasses. Evaluate a per-target-position train-only unigram prior as an inexpensive independent baseline.

Prespecify seeds 137, 311, 557 and AdamW learning rate .002, weight decay .01, batch size 64 or 96 held fixed for all arms. Every cell must include at least one complete shuffled pass over all 14,952 training conditional examples and 60 actual seconds of optimizing on the assigned host. Record both the full-pass and time-60 endpoints; select the best of those two by mean of the three development per-token NLLs and freeze before any test decode. Save every model and every original target-token log-probability and greedy target sequence, preserving hashes. Compare models on identical actor sets with actor-cluster bootstrap across paired seeds. Report parameter counts, true token and cell clock, cost of table builds and conditioning, and feasibility gaps separately.
