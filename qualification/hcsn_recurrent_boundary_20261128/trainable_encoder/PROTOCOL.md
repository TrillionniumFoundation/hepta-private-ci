# Trained six-layer conditional switching-price populations

The frozen low-bandwidth feature study and the parent sustained run have different architectures and objectives. This experiment asks whether allowing a future return to the low-cost state changes the performance of a trainable six-layer population with audio, images and prompt texts. Use the original CREMA-D tensor and source hashes in the parent sustained protocol, with the complete image and acoustic representation and all 72 text prompts. Reuse the disjoint-actor training/development/held division and document historical exposure to this corpus. At each step update all trainable parameters by AdamW at 0.0003, batch 16, the parent multi-positive contrastive loss, clip norm at 1. Use single-thread FP32 CPU PyTorch. Width 96, six layers, three matched initializations (137, 311, 557) and one data-order stream for each initialization. At every full shuffled epoch the last minibatch may have fewer than 16 examples. Keep the existing residual and the four-atom physical transport.

The financial branches use identical parameterized neural mixers, wall normalization and numerical resolution. Compare (i) the stationary finite-grid price law, (ii) one-way deterioration and (iii) returning liquidity regimes. All three use 65 uniformly spaced normalized drives u. P(u)=61.68(1+0.28u). In the switching arms the upward intensity is 0.75+0.70u. The downward intensity is 0 or 0.35. The current pricing regime is low. Reprice each node on the two-year regime horizon with 96 steps per year and the fixed-rate infinite terminal tail. The two signals are equity and monetary credit loss at each of the four positive log distances in the original population model, divided by 20 as in its original financial price channels. The communicated mark is log(B(u)/B(0)). The physical first-passage and reset states use the parent diffusive transport under the moving mark. This deliberately separates the conditional pricing prior from the physical event law. The current physical state is not a sample of the two-state pricing chain.
