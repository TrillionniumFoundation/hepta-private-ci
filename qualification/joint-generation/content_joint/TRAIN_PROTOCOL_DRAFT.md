# New TRAIN protocol proposal: native semantic head versus strong static controls

**Draft only. No training is authorized or executed by this analysis.** Before a
future run, the parent must settle the unified budget/architecture design; then
complete implementation, numerical checks, source/input identities and an
executable protocol must be committed and frozen. Old CORE/r2 data stays closed.
This cannot confirm, rerun or reverse any old failed experiment.

## Fixed range and controls

Use only existing physical-source TRAIN fit/inner artifacts:144 fit sources,
48 inner sources,16 rows/source, three seeds11/23/47. Inner rows have already
served model selection. Report them as developmental observations, never new
independent confirmation. Bind their saved source manifests and input hashes;
do not construct any old or new formal split or read formal markers.

Only one new architecture: native12 with sum-of-base-head initialization from
`ARCHITECTURE.md`. General144 is an analytical comparator, not a trained/search
candidate. No architecture selection, LR grid, threshold sweep, new gate,
refiner, anchor penalty or schedule is allowed in this proposal.

For each objective family S/H/A/N, propose a fixed paired comparison of two
recipes, original independent dense and native12, on all three seeds: **six
models total per family**, fewer than the old nine LR candidates. Both recipes
start from the same pre-adaptation base encoder/decoder; native initialization
is deterministically derived from that base. Each family gets the same recipes,
three seeds, batch64, fixed LR.01, optimizer, candidate allocations and decoder.
S uses pure CE coefficient1; H/A/N share coefficient.2. N uses only fixed rho1.5,
with the original categorical entropic loss, not a new risk search.

Original independent heads are allowed KEEP and the already fixed log2 static
reconciliation, evaluated for S/H/A/N alike. No tau0 optimization is added.
Native12 has agreeing outputs already; a repair is an identity alias, not another
service call or training opportunity. Preserve the strong original selected
S/H/A models, their KEEP/log2 results and full historical ledgers. The parent's
strong N artifacts remain a required independently bound reference if available;
this workspace does not invent their identity or claim to have compared them.

The paired new S independent control must get the same cap and training recipe
as native S. H is not a substitute for this stronger pure-supervised control.
Report every recipe/objective/seed, even if the architecture is worse. There is
no post-hoc winner replacing a failed declared contrast.

## Hypotheses that can fail

1. Native12 can use a legal same-semantic joint law with fewer actual operations
   while retaining quality versus strong static independent S/H and fixed log2.
   A gain over KEEP alone is insufficient; fail that quality hypothesis if it
   cannot exceed the applicable strong same-cap static CE and joint-success
   controls. Any cap violation is also failure. All marginal/error/pixel/greedy
   cases must accompany the comparison, even if aggregate CE improves.
2. Additive content utility improves **training after coupling**: native A−H
   should improve both original rho1.5 CE and joint success after real parameter
   changes. Nonpositive mean in either falsifies that developmental hypothesis.
   Always report all seeds. A/H coupling gains against their independent KEEP
   outputs do not establish this claim. Save native zero-update controls so that
   a new distribution's initial gain cannot masquerade as learned improvement.
3. If N is included, native N−A is a separate fixed-rho contrast with identical
   architecture/anchor/optimizer opportunity. No NDU success follows from A−H or
   from an architecture main effect. Do not wait for another group to pass.

These are proposed descriptive TRAIN contrasts, not approved formal gates or
alpha expenditure. A later independent confirmation needs the parent's full
history, independent physical sources, one-time opening and cross-confirmation
error-control design. No confidence interval on these reused inner rows closes it.

## One cumulative budget per objective family

Maintain the original **10 CPU seconds AND12B declared units** per family for
the entire proposed paired build. Shared source/data checks, old base/decoder
preparation, new ABI compilation/import/allocation, initialization/zero controls,
all six training candidates, all decoded observations/KEEP/log2 law compilation,
validation, any fixed selection, all model/gradient/moment/PNG exports and actual
service belong to the SAME cumulative family ledger. Shared work is attributed
fully to each family. S/H/A/N get the same cap and allocation algorithm; no
per-candidate fresh cap, padding, free controller or free historical baseline.

The old common base preparation alone cost3,486,924,537 units and2.075925876 CPU
seconds. Reusing its immutable files avoids duplicate execution, **not** that
cost. Add all new shared preparation costs, then reserve2 CPU seconds and500M
units for all observations/export/service before assigning remaining allowance
equally to six fixed recipe/seed slots. Training stops on either actual CPU or
atomic complete-step unit allowance. Recompute quotas from actual new common
costs; the calculator's upper bounds omit those unknown new costs and are not
an admission receipt. If even the fixed reserve is insufficient, fail/preserve;
do not enlarge caps or quietly skip controls.

Useful cheaper steps may permit more updates. Record all actual update counts,
batch prefixes and stop reasons; equal resource caps need not mean equal steps.
No encoder update is omitted, and no unused work fills the allowance. Rotate
recipe/objective order prospectively. Freeze exact observation/service call
counts and denominators in the executable plan before running, not from results.

**Do not reset the e7de continuation ledgers.** They already total H11,847,956,433
and A11,847,662,223 units, leaving only152,043,567 and152,337,777 respectively.

The primary paired within-new-build controls avoid that free-portfolio problem.

## Evidence required before/after any future execution

Before freeze: finite-difference derivatives for original marginal anchor and
flat A/N gradients; exact independent initialization for144 on hand inputs only;
exact conditioned-diagonal initialization for12; full-Q versus sparse CE parity;
gradient and actual Adam displacement/clocks/moments checks; actual decoder maps
and uint8 semantics; zero/tie/underflow rejection rules; sampler/greedy contracts;
receipt cap and full timing boundaries. These are implementation acceptance work,
not model training or threshold selection. The current draft implements none of
the optimizer/model/sampler changes and cannot be launched as an experiment.

During a future single build, retain initial/final parameters and digest, actual
per-group gradient norms/update displacements/clipping, per-parameter Adam clocks
and final moments, losses, fixed sparse checkpoints paid in the reserve, exact
Q/greedy and semantic outcomes, sampled probabilities/pairs/PNG output, full
preparation/search/service receipts and every failure. Avoid claiming a mechanism
from endpoint distances alone. Distinguish head-cost savings, distribution
coupling, objective gradients, representation learning and fixed decoder capacity.
