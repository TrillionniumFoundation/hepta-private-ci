## Freeze the boundary and dependencies before the next build

Maintain two linked accounts, with scope definitions and artifact inventories
committed before training. **M, method**, contains every necessary preparation,
actual used architecture/compiler, model/data construction, all candidate
training, any selection/validation, controller, mandatory output/self-check,
actual service and required export. Cost shared preparation in full per compared
system, and count every candidate/search option in the same system ledger.
Declare concrete output files, candidate count, service denominators, validation
laws and mandatory self-check before running. No free historical fallback.

**R, independent research review**, contains post-seal independent PNG/parameter
review, mechanistic diagnosis and manuscript/prose. It has separate measured
process receipts linked to M's output manifest; R may grow with further questions
without rewriting M's finished cost. It does not provide training, stopping,
architecture/threshold choice or candidate-selection feedback. If a review
computation is actually used by the method, it was not independent: its full
dependency belongs in M under a prospective protocol. A discovered hidden cost
or correctness bug can invalidate a claim; it cannot produce a retroactive pass.

Keep minimal necessary codec/semantic/finite-output checks, model reload/digest,
actual probability/source capture, resource checks and final model outputs in M.
For a fixed decoder, cache and pay its actual decoded R and semantic/uint8
contract once; service must render and export the real final outputs once.
Full repeated forensic verification of every saved PNG/state is R unless it is
explicitly necessary for the method's decision or output contract, in which case
it must be in M and reserved before training. The boundary cannot be moved after
seeing its cost or scientific sign.

For a six-model paired research portfolio both architectures' compilation and
initialization are M. For an independent-only deployable method, unused native
compilation/self-check is excluded prospectively and documented, and its own
actual common preparation is supplied to the allocator. The4.495378557-second
common example below belongs to the old mixed portfolio, not every future
architecture. All S/H/A/N get the same applicable recipes, supervision anchor,
optimizer/search opportunities, decoding opportunities and total caps.

## A real finish boundary instead of endless receipt accounting

The M process tree performs its own budget controller, required summaries,
model/probability/PNG export and mandatory checks before exit. A read-only
external meter records CPU of that complete process tree through exit and hashes
the already finished manifest. Its infrastructure cost is explicitly R, applied
the same way to every method; it does not create a model output or steer M.
The internal controller and all inference/controller overhead remain M.
This makes the last necessary M write measurable without recursively treating
every independent report about the report as more M computation.

Seal the method receipt once: input/source hashes, all final candidate outputs,
mandatory phases, actual full CPU/units, and any failure. R reads that seal.
There is no `append_method` after seal. Additional model construction/selection
is a new method protocol with its own complete dependencies, never a free R job.
The pure state machine in [boundary.py](boundary.py) enforces fixed-slot coverage,
mandatory phases, immutable M after seal, exclusion of unused architecture
events, and prohibition of review-for-selection. It retains actual overages as
failures. A future runner still needs to connect real process meters and artifact
hashes to these primitives; this task does not execute such a runner.

## Avoid another checkpoint-budget cliff

The old fixed1/128/256/512 neural diagnostic/serialization work was inside each
slot and required.10 remaining seconds; this produced255/511 stopping cliffs.
Do not change that old result. In a new protocol, distinguish information used
for training/selection from independent mechanism review. Keep a fixed minimal
loss/update/resource trace and required exports in M. Reserve any mandatory
intermediate observation/export envelope before useful updates start, rather
than discovering it near a due checkpoint. Initial/final output contracts must
remain complete if a later checkpoint is unaffordable.

One minimal prospective implementation is fixed lightweight parameter snapshots
whose copying/export cost is paid in M; full diagnostic forwards on those saved
snapshots run only after seal in R, with every checkpoint retained and no effect
on M selection. If a checkpoint is used to choose early stop/model/architecture,
all its evaluation and search becomes M instead. Neither path is implemented or
trained here, and no missing old checkpoint is created.
