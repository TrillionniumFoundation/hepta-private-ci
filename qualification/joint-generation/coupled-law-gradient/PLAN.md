were still trained against an independent joint law. Earlier transport alignment
showed that optimizing the final serving law can matter. This motivates a distinct,
prospective training-law alignment test,not rho/seed/greedy-metric tuning. Prior
control gains and original NDU failures stay intact; no benefit is assumed here.

domain. Positive exact ties are rejected,not assigned a hidden generalized gradient.
