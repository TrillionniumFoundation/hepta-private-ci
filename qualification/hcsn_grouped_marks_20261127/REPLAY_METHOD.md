# Portable cold-result replay and cost accounting

`export_cold_evidence.py ROOT DEST` runs only after the joint evaluation seal and final results exist. It verifies all 72 model assignments, source array SHA-256 receipts, native score totals and identical per-photo IDs/counts across candidates. It packages local NLL sums and shifted-image sums for every distinct arm/checkpoint combination in `heldout_losses.npz`, with a receipt map and model-step metadata in `heldout_index.json`. It also captures all 108 fit receipts and individual learning curves in `training_receipts.tar.gz`. Raw image pixels, captions, tokens, paths to accounts and model parameters are excluded from the portable score pack. The model and optimizer objects remain checksum-bound on the study device.

`replay_cold_evidence.py DEST CHECK.json` independently derives per-arm NLL, perplexity, image-conditioning gain, all available best/threshold assignments and up to 20 paired comparisons. It computes 5,000 bootstrap replicates by converting resampled indices into multiplicity matrices and multiplying them against paired losses and token counts. The registered evaluator instead gathers resampled images directly. It checks every reported interval and gating criterion to numerical precision. Evidence for a result exists only after the completed study pack passes this replay.

`account_grouped_costs.py ROOT COSTS.json` checks every selected/model and final optimizer-state hash, records completed plus interrupted child time and preserves the previous and first-attaining development observations for each of 216 arm/seed/threshold combinations. Checkpoint grid sensitivity uses the intervals only for paired attained seeds. An earlier transient crossing could have occurred without a scheduled evaluation. The development-bracket and primary first-observed estimands are different.

Native NVIDIA package readings use trapezoids from consecutive measurements wholly inside a given window. Gaps above 120 milliseconds are excluded from integrated time and energy. The program reports gap counts, actual coverage and compute contexts from outside the study. Startup-inclusive first-crossing windows reach from the beginning of the scientific child process to its observed `history.json` commit. Such intervals are reported only when a unique attempt can be reconciled to the checkpoint. These windows include allocation, warmup, data verification and checkpoint writes in addition to optimization and validation. They describe gross shared GPU board joules. All numerical validation of these scripts prior to real evaluation used a clearly separate synthetic 108-fit control fixture, with constructed losses and constant 50 W power; it supplied no experimental results.
