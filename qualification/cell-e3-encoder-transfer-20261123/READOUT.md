# Independent pretrained encoder and adapter results

Both original teachers satisfied the registered directional and absolute thresholds in a fresh eight-domain engineering experiment. In the 24-item confirmation groups, SmolLM2-135M-Instruct led FLAN-T5-small on addition (15 versus 10) and Python method completion (18 versus 11). FLAN led on subject–verb agreement (24 versus 11). Discovery was 10 versus 7, 14 versus 5 and 16 versus 7 respectively (16 per domain). Each confirmed domain has at least two exclusive successes for its leading teacher. The family generators repeat linguistic templates and compatible APIs; the denominators describe items.

The third encoder is all-MiniLM-L6-v2 with 22,713,216 frozen base parameters. The fitting pool contains 192 archived teacher-labelled items and 256 new items. Two routing methods receive discovery truth for 128 separate items. One real OS process per arm reloaded the third encoder, its tokenizer and learned material. Its workspace contained only the blind questions/candidates and frozen student material, with no original teacher weights, and kernel network traffic remained zero.

The rows use 16/16 identical simple old checks. Each edge ablation returned its remaining expert's answers on all 272 requests. The ridge sparse graph got 11/20 Smol-only and 11/13 FLAN-only questions right in the confirmed domains. The parent got 9/20 and 5/13, the preselected Smol student 11/20 and 0/13. This local gain coincided with a net loss of one question against the parent (26 recovered and 27 lost) and four fewer exact pairs. The corresponding LoRA sparse graph got 11/20 and 0/13. No behavioral arm met the complete prospective transfer rule. Targets from human-authored rules improved this LoRA architecture in its separate supervised diagnostic.

Every learned adapter trained 6,528 parameters on the last self-attention query/value projections and a scoring vector. The five independent fits each took 58 steps and 5.39–5.50 wall seconds after common preprocessing on that runner. The primary fit took 8.40 seconds for all ridge heads including encoding. These scopes and different runners do not support a time-to-quality comparison.

The first hosted job froze and published the models and blind seed, then halted when the network namespace's inherited sysfs mount exposed the host interface listing. The recovery reused its exact uploaded weights and seed, mounted namespace-specific read-only sysfs, and completed all nine arms. The supplementary LoRA protocol was recorded before seeing any new confirmation scores and before the primary freeze. It was fitted later using the same panel, with primary teacher confirmation labels available to its scorer only after fitting.

The complete arrays, blind requests, original teacher margins, cold outputs and score records are in `evidence/encoder-run.zip` and `evidence/encoder-adapters.zip`. The independent standard-library reader regenerated the original blind panel, recomputed teacher exclusives/gates, and checked 2,448 + 2,992 predictions from 20 arm-runs. Original discovery/fitting and all unmodified results remain in [#7035](https://github.com/TrillionniumFoundation/hepta-private-ci/pull/7035).
