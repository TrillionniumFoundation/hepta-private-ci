# Human references and model attribution

The English source sentences and French/German translations in this research sample are from FLORES-101, assembled by the FLORES project (Goyal et al., 2022). They are distributed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The study appends instructions and presents original translations as candidate and unrelated same-language foil, selected from the published aligned files. The same license applies to the included reference material and its adaptations. Source line numbers, partitions and upstream checksums are reproducible from SOURCES.json and the fixed generator. [Original release](https://github.com/facebookresearch/flores/tree/main/flores101).

The language-specific expert weights are derived from Helsinki-NLP [English–German](https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-en-de), CC BY 4.0, and [English–French](https://huggingface.co/Helsinki-NLP/opus-mt-en-fr), Apache 2.0. Per-row quantization changes the stored weights. No human from these historical datasets was enrolled in this experiment. The separate behavioral student uses the frozen [all-MiniLM-L6-v2](https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2) encoder, Apache 2.0, and newly fitted numeric heads. Model and tokenizer commits and file identities are included in the run.
