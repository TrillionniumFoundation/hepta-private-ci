# Matched update of a frozen pretrained encoder readout

Replay the exact mixed-teacher design matrix and residual target from the archived encoder study. The selection of this panel follows publication of its scores. This is a retrospective, same-objective optimizer comparison, with zero new people and no claim of a fresh generalization benchmark.

Freeze the encoder, parent, teacher labels, regularizer (0.2), original train/confirmation split and evaluation. Fit the same float64 linear head from zero on one CPU thread with a direct dual ridge solve and PyTorch L-BFGS through the autograd loss, including the L2 term. L-BFGS uses a strong-Wolfe line search, at most 256 iterations and history 50. No held-out score selects the stopping point or settings. Predeclare seven interleaved rounds, alternate first solver, report every timing and gradient pass. Retain objective gap, relative stationarity, paired changes of argmax on every historical blind row, match to the original published answers, source and model identities, feature extraction cost and fit-only medians. Report a failure to match as a measured outcome. Timings include optimizer setup and parameter writes into memory. Model acquisition, training-label creation, freezer I/O and holdout evaluation are outside the shared feature/solve cost. This block compares a frozen representation with a trainable readout, leaving full-network feature learning to a separate experiment.
