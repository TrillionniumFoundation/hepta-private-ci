canonical operator sources. For an interior uniform source cell of width

finite matrix-generator limit. With a fixed finite set of strictly interior
Gauss nodes, the scaled active off-diagonal limit instead tends to zero at
sufficiently small h. The unscaled operators can both tend to identity; the
noncommutation concerns the scaled limits. Phase, discount and returning-tail
effects are separate.

resolution categories, not qualification passes; the scale is not a certified
function-error bound.
