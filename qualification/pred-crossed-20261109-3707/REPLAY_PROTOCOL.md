# PRED cached-horizon checkpoint replay 001

## Prospective development extension

This is a separately registered analysis of all checkpoints in the frozen
189-fit train/development diagnostic. The formal training outcomes have not
been inspected when this protocol is frozen. Small newly generated software
test fixtures are outside that claim. The timestamp and hashes bind artifacts;
they cannot independently prove previous human information access.

The original training source, configuration, selection criterion, consumed
optimization budget and both sealed confirmation campaigns remain unchanged.
This extension performs **zero new fits** and generates **zero confirmation
episodes**. It reuses the same three development data realizations. Neither
189 checkpoints nor the 27 crossed cells are independent confirmation units.

## Fixed comparisons

All seven registered model/ablation variants and every data/init/order cell
are included, using each saved minimum-dev-NLL checkpoint. The three policies
are `matched_cached_fixed1` (1), `cached_short13` (1/3), and
`cached_adaptive136` (1/3/6). Every policy encodes its observed prefix once,
computes only the requested legal heads, and pays its own registered tariff.

Admission reserves the entire requested panel and its maximum terminal and
preparation support before inference. This conservative rule is part of the
comparison; it does not find an optimal affordable subset. Original resources
and the previously fixed budget-16/deadline-20 intervention are both retained.
They reuse outcomes and are not separate tasks. These are direct preparation
forecasts, not autoregressive latent rollouts or actual readback-driven plans.

All policy choices and an additional old-cheap bridge are fixed before
evaluator access. The matched single-step arm must reproduce the frozen
cheap Fixed1 forecasts and choices exactly, and its complete episode-level
outputs, costs and operation counts must agree with the original fit record.
The bridge is additional host computation, not another policy arm.

outer EXITED/0, exact source/configuration/registration bindings, and the
complete unchanged 189-fit grid. Verify checkpoint byte and numerical hashes,
full optimizer state, model structure, and regenerated development identity.

silently drop it from a mean. An incomplete replay receives no aggregate.

Save per-episode choices, statuses, expected and realized utility, prefix and
future cost, declared inference charges and time. Preserve per-policy head,
state and planning counts plus bridge, validation and generation timing.
Report each variant's means and paired short/adaptive-minus-single-step

decomposition. No hypothesis test, post-hoc winner promotion or new p-value.
