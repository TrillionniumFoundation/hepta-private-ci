# Follow-up on the observed two-stable-state region

The original 12-atom scan found 12 triplets among its 196 parameter triples. The new analysis focuses explicitly on three observed cases, including v=−.9 with both equity and loss feedback enabled at .65 and 6, and v=−.3 with restoring gain 0 or .02 and loss gain 3. Also repeat the moderate reference v=0, both gains .65. This is a robustness study selected after the scan. Repeat equilibrium and local stability estimates at 24, 48 and 96 atoms. Vary the maximum grid distance at similar spatial resolution. Use lower, reset and outer mass distributions, and prescribed positive/negative input histories with common initial reset, to distinguish physical basins from direct arbitrary reinitialization. Preserve roots that disappear or iterations that fail to settle. Check the finite-state Jacobian against the Jacobian of the actual PyTorch dynamics restricted to the normalized mass simplex. The grid collects the upper tail at its last representative, so extension of the domain has a different meaning from refining the same domain. Report these checks before attaching a phase-transition or hysteresis claim.
