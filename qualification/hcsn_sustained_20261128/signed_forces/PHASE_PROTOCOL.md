# Stationary bifurcations of a constant-drive finite population

For a constant boundary and principal, an interior stationary distribution admits a one-dimensional self-consistency test. Conditional on physical drift, solve the stationary killed finite-state transport plus one-tick reset in double precision; recover conditional mean log margin, expected scaled equity and expected scaled credit loss; solve the scalar equation for the feedback-adjusted physical drift. Reject negative-mass or nonconserving solutions. Construct the full one-tick Jacobian with the redundant refractory coordinate eliminated. Report the spectral radius and absorption flux of every discovered fixed point. For a mixed outward/inward set of starting distributions, iterate the actual population recursion to cross-check any multiple branches. For the fixed quoted contracts and K=12, prespecify input drives −.9, −.6, −.3, 0, .3, .6, .9; restoring gains 0, .25, .65, 1.5; loss gains 0, .25, .65, 1.5, 3, 6, 12. Search for open sign changes, near touches and sampled extrema of the self-consistency residual over the attainable physical drift interval. Compare sensitivity for K=24 and K=48 at the two registered moderate gains and the strongest pair. A finite scan can report discovered equilibria or instability, and does not exclude other behavior elsewhere. Coincident roots at exactly tangent thresholds can be missed. This is a test of the proposed physical model, separate from comparison with subjects' measured spike counts.
