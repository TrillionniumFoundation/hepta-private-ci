# Path and price interventions on the six-layer direct-edit study

This post-hoc study asks if the explicit lower-boundary port or either price channel affects retrieval after direct writing, same-site BP, or leaving the initialized network in place. The aggregate unmodified results of the width-96 CREMA-D study have already been seen. Select exactly the lowest original raw development NLL per method and seed (steps ascending for ties) from the original seal, and evaluate all five original methods and all three seeds. Confirm unmodified logits against every original selected cold score array before running interventions. Do not refit any parameter, temperature or label prior. All arms use the original 1,311 cold clips and 72 gallery texts.

In each of the five receiving layers replace the output of the boundary linear map with zero on audiovisual encoding only, gallery encoding only, and both encoders. These paired interventions preserve the other residual and price paths. On both encoders separately set the equity, loss or both price channels to zero in all six physical cells using the original cell's named gates. Report the raw mean NLL and top-1; the primary effect is ablated minus intact NLL averaged across the original three trained seeds, with 4,000 paired actor-bootstrap draws across sixteen held actors and clip weighting. Also report mean absolute logit change and fraction of top-1 decisions changed. Price ablation removes a price both from feedback and from the learned read, so it cannot distinguish these mechanisms. A positive NLL difference means the intact component helped for these fixed weights and actors. Measured interventions and confidence intervals are descriptive, subject to correlated comparisons, prior use of this corpus, and possible distribution shift from a zeroed channel. The primary code and original selection and cost accounting remain in the accompanying study.
