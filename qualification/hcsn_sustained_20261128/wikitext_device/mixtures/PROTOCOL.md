# Frozen validation-only combination of real WikiText predictors

No base training or network edits in this experiment. Draw on the pre-existing two-epoch seed-137, 311, 557 HX, SDPA and smooth models with 128-token original reset, and on the existing twelve-epoch native seed-887 HX/SDPA/smooth checkpoints. The official training-only discounted continuation bigram uses discount .75 and .1 pseudocount. Each parent provides the exact probability of each observed next word under a normalized 33,278-word distribution.

For two parent distributions, deploy the convex mixture `p=(1-a) p_A + a p_B`. Fit its only scalar on all official validation words by the monotone derivative of negative log likelihood, checking endpoints. Freeze it before evaluating the mixture on the official test split. Compare with fixed half-mixture, both frozen parent scores, and the unattainable diagnostic that chooses whichever parent assigned more probability to the answer after observing it. A secondary observable gate uses four mutually exclusive conditioning buckets: previous word is EOS; otherwise the previous word appeared 0–9, 10–99, or at least 100 times as a training context. Fit each bucket on its validation instances; buckets with under 256 use the global scalar. The actual next-word identity must never participate in gating. Report raw original parent predictions or pinned sources, per-block sufficient verification data, and deployed coefficients.

At two epochs each, for each cyclic seed (137,311,557), compare same-seed HX+SDPA with SDPA+next independent SDPA seed, HX+next independent HX seed, and SDPA+smooth. These have two equally exposed, equal-size parents. Also pair the identical train-only bigram with each neural family and examine a two-stage combination of the validation-fixed neural pair with the bigram. No claim that two parents have the cost of one. Retain per-seed and three-seed-averaged paired discrepancies; obtain descriptive confidence intervals by resampling contiguous 4,096-word test blocks. The seed-887 sensitivity analysis compares existing original 128-reset HX/SDPA/smooth, periodic 2,048-state HX, global-periodic 2,048 SDPA, and the same count distribution. These results are post-training combinations requiring access to both parents at deployment; no evidence of training a large parent model without backpropagation follows from them. This study is registered after the component single-model scores were known and before calculating mixture scores.
