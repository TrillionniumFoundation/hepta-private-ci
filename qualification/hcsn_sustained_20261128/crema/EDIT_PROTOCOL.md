# Direct writing of six population read matrices on natural audiovisual and text inputs

Use the preregistered width-96 literal encoder, original actors and prompts, initial seeds and identical 16-item sample prefixes of the sustained study. Evaluate separate fits after 16 and 128 batches (256 and 2048 possibly repeated clips). Compare a forward-only six-site construction followed by a fitted projection, shuffled hidden labels with the same true projection targets, an output-only direct fit, and AdamW on exactly the same seven writable matrices. Report the initial unedited control. This is a new finite training experiment on a previously studied corpus.

For each layer collect its actual 80 signed/group features at the last frame of each audio-video and paired text sequence. Fit a shared ridge adjustment to its real 96-by-80 read matrix toward fixed seed-specific random 96-vectors for the observed 72 class labels. Normalize the prototype scale to the root mean square of the observed residual stream and blend the update by one half. The targets in raw space are prototype minus current residual-plus-mixer output, before the feedforward sublayer. Propagate the edited layer on all twelve positions before constructing the next layer. The final shared 256-by-96 projection is fit to independent fixed unit prototypes from last-token LayerNorm features, followed by the original L2 normalization on both modalities. Use ridge penalty 0.01 times the mean Gram diagonal, with a 1e-9 absolute floor, and FP64 solves. No loss gradients are used by the direct arms. Hidden target shuffling permutes across clip pairs and preserves alignment between the sound-video and text views of a given pair. Ground-truth prompts are only available as labelled training pairs and as an unlabeled 72-item gallery at inference.

For the same-position BP comparison train only six population read matrices and the projection, starting from the same seeded weights with the original loss, batch order, optimizer and rate. Record number and rank of genuinely altered parameters, all timings, development mean NLL and accuracy, exact input identity and selected checkpoint bytes. For each method, including isolated data preparation and evaluation, use the independently restarted prefix costs; charge both prefixes if both were inspected for model selection. Freeze the chosen candidate and every fixed-prefix candidate for all methods/seeds before opening the cold set. Report raw 72-way scores for all 1,311 held clips, all three seeds, paired actor-bootstrap intervals and measured earliest qualifying end-to-end cost at development AND held-out NLL thresholds 4.1 and 3.6. Cold comparisons from previously used actors constitute a further test on the same benchmark. The construction fits seven matrices, leaving the fronts, financial constants and other hidden matrices at seeded initialization.
