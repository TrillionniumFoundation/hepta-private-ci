# Frozen CREMA-D encoders on a second public audiovisual corpus

Re-evaluate already trained encoders on Livingstone and Russo's RAVDESS speech videos. Use all 24 actors' full audiovisual speech trials in the six emotions shared with the initial CREMA-D training corpus. Map RAVDESS 05/07/06/03/01/04 to original emotion columns 0/1/2/3/4/5 (anger, disgust, fear, happy, neutral, sad). Other expressions and singing are outside the six-way target. On the official complete release there are 44 eligible clips per actor, 1,056 total. All actors and both lexically matched RAVDESS statements belong to a separate corpus. The stated measure is zero-shot six-emotion transfer from CREMA-D. RAVDESS itself is public and previously used in the research field.

Apply the same pinned FFmpeg, midpoint video frames, audio resampling, FFT, pooling and CREMA-D training normalization. Keep original prepared resolution, 12×28×28 luminance plus 12×64 log spectrum. Compute the original 72 CREMA-D prompt logits with their fixed token dictionary. Turn each into a six-emotion distribution by summing the twelve sentence probabilities for each emotion. No fitted RAVDESS coefficient, retraining, threshold, early stop, or label-specific class reweighting is allowed. Report raw six-emotion NLL, ordinary top-1 and class-balanced accuracy, per-emotion counts, and mean paired-seed differences from causal SDPA or matched-site BP, with 4,000 paired resamples of the 24 actors. A class prior from CREMA-D training and the uniform distribution are fixed baselines. A cheap supervised ridge on source-only video/audio means and standard deviations selects its ridge and probability scale using the source development emotions, then is frozen before new-corpus scoring.

Fixed model groups: (1) full-corpus width-96 direct writes, same-seven-matrix BP, and full-parameter BP, three seeds, each with the already sealed source-development choice for raw NLL; (2) all five registered full-detail analytic encoder variants at both one-pass and 60-second fixed endpoints, three seeds; (3) source-development-best sealed endpoints from the sustained 3,150-active-second-per-cell literal, smooth, LIF and SDPA experiment, three seeds. Each group is independently scored only when its complete source seal exists, and all code/choices across the three groups are fixed before first external scoring. Keep development and RAVDESS actor identities distinct. Only actual sources with checksummed licensed release data count. An incomplete actor panel will be reported separately and cannot substitute for the complete endpoint.
