# Whole-training-set six-layer direct writes versus both matched-site and full BP

The earlier finite comparison had at most 2,048 sampled training presentations and failed its joint three-seed quality test. This experiment gives every method the entire set of 4,984 CREMA-D training clips in each pass, using the original width-96 six-layer audiovisual encoder, complete 72-prompt text gallery, all 1,147 development clips and the same 1,311 previously studied held clips. This is a development of the method on an already observed corpus. It has no external-confirmation status.

Use seeds 137, 311 and 557; in each of four passes shuffle all 4,984 unique indices once with a seed-specific common generator. Compare full direct writes, shuffled hidden labels with real output labels, direct head only, same-seven-matrix AdamW and full-network AdamW. Direct methods use the original fixed random label prototypes, regularizer and learning rule. Apply the hidden correction to the current matrices for each pass; refit the shared gallery projection. BP methods take gradient steps on the exact same sequence of batch-16 clips, including the final short batch, with AdamW lr 0.0003, weight decay .01, and global norm cap 1. Only the last arm opens all weights. Record the full model state at the ends of passes 1, 2 and 4. Count setup, fit, save, selection, calibrated validation and held scoring. Validate estimates in FP64. Use one physical CPU thread and retain source, content and weight hashes.

For each regime fix a candidate independently on the complete actor-disjoint development set before any held scoring in this experiment. Primary scores are raw normalized gallery logits. The predeclared secondary regime fits a temperature in [0,4] on development and adds the same full training-class log frequencies for every arm; the secondary rule benefits from known template frequencies. Report every candidate's held result, all three selected-model individual seed agreements at NLL 4.1, 3.6 and 3.0 on development and held subjects, and paired 4,000-draw actor-bootstrap intervals. Separate the earliest observed gate at the fixed 1/2/4 checkpoints, measured workload seconds to that point and the retrospective cost of performing all exploratory fits and scoring. The same corpus in other studies and the generous supervised labels for the direct intermediate objectives limit generalization.
