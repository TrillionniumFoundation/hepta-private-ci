# Finite-window renewal operators on 108 archived Allen current-clamp specimens

The archived analysis compared an asymptotic frequency formula and empirical curves on approximately one-second current steps. This retrospective follow-up fits the analytically exact finite-window expected number of threshold crossings to the original training levels, independently per cell. Reuse the original cell-specific withheld current levels. The 72 summary-derived cells and 36 NWB-derived cells are disjoint. Do not mix their errors. The established cohort has already been evaluated by other methods.

Fix initial and reset distance to one, fixing the unidentifiable voltage scale. For normalized clamped drive I, define physical log-margin drift μ=−s(I−θ), s>0. The nominal financial prices play no role in this biological-rate test. A regenerative unit has a fixed refractory time d and noise σ. Predict the expected count over time T as the sum over j≥1 of the first-passage CDF from distance j evaluated at T−(j−1)d, retaining positive time terms only. Compare this with an independently calibrated stationary rate times T, using the same current encoding, voltage scale, common s/θ/d bounds and training levels. Evaluate two finite models, with σ fixed to 2 and with σ fitted in [0.15,16]. Three starts per model; select the lowest training sum of squared count errors. The test endpoints are per-cell mean absolute held-current-count error in each cohort, paired per-specimen bootstrap intervals (10,000 draws), and zero/positive-level errors. Retain all original predictions from the source for comparisons to LIF, QVI, interpolation and softplus. The fixed noise and fitted noise models are separate results and neither is chosen on cold data. This study does not substitute neural spike timing, a new cell population or a learned price mechanism for the available aggregate counts.

Use the one-second training window for which the compact files give no per-training-current duration. At held levels use the original per-level acquisition durations. Freeze every fit in both cohorts together before reading either set's held labels for this analysis. Check analytical Jacobians against centered finite differences away from contact with a refractory endpoint. Record all fits, all held predictions and source/archive hashes.
