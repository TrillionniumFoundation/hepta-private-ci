# Minimum tested quote budget at fixed prediction quality

The six-call study fixed the two initial probes in common. This separate experiment prices prefixes of length 2–6, and also permits the two initial interventions to use the entire allowed range. A single purchase and its two sensor errors have exactly the units, ranges and distribution registered in the first protocol. Fix the quality gate before generating either of the budget-study populations. A contract/noise realization succeeds when all three stationary outputs on all 16 previously specified off-design locations have monetary error at most 0.004 times true principal. At most six fresh calls are available.

Compare three predetermined design families: spread with the original two pilots and predetermined continuation; rate-aware D with the original two pilots; and rate-aware D seeded by the two near-contact probes (0.72,0.02) and (1.28,0.02), spanning the whole allowed principal range. All three use the same estimator on every prefix. For the D designs plan remaining observations from known rates and the fixed population-center monetary coefficients. Candidate options and repeated observations follow the original six-call protocol. For each design, select the minimum prefix over 2,3,4,5,6 whose one-sided 95% lower contract-bootstrap bound on success reaches 0.95 on an independent calibration population of 128 contracts with 12 noise realizations each. If none does, mark the family unqualified. Freeze all selected budgets and rules. Then score the whole fixed prefix frontier on a further 256 untouched contracts with 12 noise realizations each. Report success rate, one-sided 95% contract-bootstrap lower bound, mean nominal-retention error and mean off-design error. Use seeds 85202 and 85203 for calibration and test; bootstrap draws use 9,200 for calibration and 9,201 for test.

“Minimum” is scoped to the three named families and five fixed budgets at the chosen quality. Adaptive stopping would have a different acquisition rule and error distribution. The four-scalar injectivity theorem supplies the independent noiseless lower bound of two quote calls in this two-coordinate sampling model on an open four-dimensional parameter domain.
