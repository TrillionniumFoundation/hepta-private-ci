# Equal-cost controlled contract measurements

Each independent stationary probe returns its absolute He–Xiong boundary and one unit's monetary credit loss at an assigned principal scale and common log margin. One call costs one two-coordinate measurement, including reset. Six calls (12 scalar readings) per contract are available. The cost model assumes a simulator with constant query cost within a bounded intervention range. Each coordinate has independent Gaussian sensor error with standard deviation 0.02 cash units plus 0.002 times its true absolute magnitude. Each comparison uses the same pilot and noise at each chronological slot. All designs know the rate, payout, volatility, maturity and premium. They infer coupon, principal, recovery and tax. Bounds for the synthetic populations are in the numerical program. All results concern that bounded experiment and this noise law.

Fix common pilots at principal scales 0.84 and 1.16, both at log margin 0.12. For the four remaining calls allow principal scales 0.72, 0.84, 1, 1.16, 1.28 and margins 0.02, 0.10, 0.24, 0.42, including repeats. Compare four strategies with the same estimator and call budget.

* A fixed spread design uses (0.72,0.02), (1.28,0.02), (0.72,0.42), (1.28,0.42).
* A pooled D-optimal design greedily maximizes the mean log Fisher determinant over 32 independent development contracts, then freezes one sequence.
* A rate-aware preplanned D-optimal design uses the known physical inputs and population center for the four unknowns. Its sequence never uses the held measurements.
* A sequential D-optimal design refits the unknowns after its pilots and after each additional probe; it uses only the already purchased observations of the same contract.

The local design objective uses sensitivities of mean quotations and their sensor standard deviations; it omits the information in the parameter dependence of the noise scale. Evaluate 128 new contracts from a separate random seed, each with 12 independent noise realizations (1,536 trajectories per design). Generate development and test draws using the explicitly registered seeds 84732 and 84733. Measure acquisition cost by probe count; CPU wall times for optimizer overhead are secondary and are machine-specific.

Primary inference compares mean maximum absolute out-of-probe prediction error across the boundary, monetary loss and equity, divided by true principal. Score the realized surfaces at principal scales (0.77, 0.93, 1.07, 1.23) and margins (0.05, 0.19, 0.35, 0.50). A subsidiary retention measure repeats scoring at the unmodified scale 1. Include the 95th-percentile errors, maximum component-relative parameter errors, and chronological selection frequencies. Bootstrap contracts after averaging over their replicates, with 2,000 paired draws, to estimate a 95% interval for the mean paired difference. An adaptive superiority claim over rate-aware preplanning requires at least 10% improvement on the primary mean and the paired interval strictly below zero. Equal cost refers to fresh quote acquisition; preparation and adaptive decision overhead will be recorded separately. Frozen held data are synthetic and independent of the article's empirical calibration and CREMA-D. Gates for architecture or training replacement are separate empirical questions.
