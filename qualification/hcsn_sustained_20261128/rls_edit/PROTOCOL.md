# Sequential RLSEdit on trained six-layer audiovisual–text HCSN parents

This prospective editing experiment uses the three previously completed whole-corpus full-BP parents. Each was selected solely by raw development NLL for seed 137, 311 or 557 at epoch 4 under the frozen development seal `6ef8baad733525893762dd8fd7e79e30b133cf2bc023e409ff570627699d100c`. Keep all upstream weights and buffers fixed. Edit the shared, bias-free 256-by-96 PyTorch output matrix. Vectors from both the video/audio and text branches pass through the same updated matrix. Use the entire original-resolution CREMA-D feature package, all 4,984 training clips, 1,147 development clips, 1,311 previously studied held-out clips, and all 72 original prompts. This is a new editing task on a previously studied actor split.

## Six interventions

Use the original sentence and emotion order in the source roster. At stage s=0,…,5 change the label for every training clip in sentence s from 6s+e to 6s+((e+1) modulo 6). In subsequent stages these changes persist. Sentences 6 through 11 always retain their original labels. At each stage report the current intervention sentence, all earlier edited sentences, the six permanently protected sentences, the yet-to-be-edited sentences where present, and the complete gallery. Use every eligible training example for its stage; order by clip ID with Woodbury blocks of at most 32. Use no development or confirmation example for updates. Keep all 72 text keys and exactly 128 protected training AV keys as anchors. Sample the 128 without replacement from sorted eligible training IDs with NumPy RNG seed 912731, then sort them by clip ID, identical across all methods and parent seeds.

Let K be the pre-head activations after the parent's final LayerNorm, and let W0 be its original projection in row-vector orientation, 96 by 256. For each edit AV key k use a value equal to the original target prompt's unit pre-normalization output direction, multiplied by the original AV output norm. Thus V(k) = norm(k W0) times normalize(k_text,new W0). All anchor values are V0=K0 W0. Report exact code, true shapes, parent/anchor/feature hashes and actual changed entries. In held evaluation recompute both normalized AV and gallery representations under W, then take the original 12-times cosine score. Check eager parent logit parity on both development and held subjects.

## Editing methods

Use Wang et al., *Beyond Hard Writes and Rigid Preservation*, IJCAI 2026, Eq. 3, on this defined linear layer. Minimize the sum through the present stage of squared key/value residuals, plus lambda squared times squared Frobenius distance to W0, plus mu squared times squared anchor residual. Fix lambda=1 and the two anchor coefficients mu=1 and mu=4. Use the complete rows of K without additional rescaling. The two regimes are both reported. Each stage starts from the recursively edited head and inverse Gram matrix. Initialize P as the inverse of I+mu² K0-transpose K0, and update W by the residual against the pre-update W using the Woodbury gain. Independently solve cumulative normal equations in FP64 at every stage and report relative and maximum absolute differences before casting to the deployed FP32 head.

Enroll the original parent, cumulative RLSEdit at both mus, a most-recent-edit-only closed solve at both mus with all older edit pairs discarded, gradient descent on the identical cumulative quadratic at both mus, and a same-head gradient control on downstream 72-way cross-entropy at both mus. For both gradient controls use full cumulative edit information, Adam with fixed learning rate 0.003, zero optimizer weight decay and fresh optimizer at each stage initialized at the preceding stage's 128-update weight. Save 8, 32 and 128 full-batch updates. The quadratic is divided by cumulative edit row count times 256; this constant preserves its optimum. The downstream task control uses mean cross-entropy plus the two quadratic regularizers divided by that same quantity and recomputes the full shared gallery on every update. The parent and algebraic methods have one candidate per stage.

## Outcomes and clocks

Seal every stage, method, seed, budget, weight hash and development score before first scoring this experiment's held interventions. The primary evaluation uses the fixed 128-update gradient heads and the unique algebraic heads. Report 72-way NLL, top-1, prediction agreement with the original parent, mean original-to-edited distribution KL, edit-class target margin, and parent-to-head weight drift. Record scalar scores or full logits and clip IDs. On protected clips measure changes in NLL/top-1 and KL, with the original labels. On older edits measure retention against the cumulative corrected labels. Report paired contrasts for cumulative RLS against original, recent-only and both matched-mu gradient controls at each stage. Cluster the original held actors jointly across the three matched seeds using 4,000 resamples, RNG 83573.

For a fixed intervention stage, the quality gate requires current NLL improvement at least 0.10 against the unedited parent, earlier-edits NLL improvement at least 0.05 when that group exists, protected NLL deterioration at most 0.10, and protected absolute top-1 drop at most 0.02. Choose the earliest registered budget passing on development, if any, before held scoring, then check the same fixed candidate against that gate on held actors. Equivalent speed claims require all three seeds of both methods to qualify for the same stage and gate. Report boundary cases and censoring. Charge data/parent I/O, pre-head feature and target acquisition, matrix construction, anchor setup, actual optimization, serialization, development selection and held evaluation separately. Report cumulative end-to-end clocks through the relevant stage and inherited parent full-BP training/selection costs separately. The work tests one linear subalgorithm on a supervised, trained HCSN. It cannot establish gradient-free pretraining, general LLM editing, or storage of unrelated capabilities outside this corpus.
