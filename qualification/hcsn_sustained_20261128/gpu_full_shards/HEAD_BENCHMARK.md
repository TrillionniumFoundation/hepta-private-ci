# Matched readout optimization on the complete frozen embeddings

Use the same 4,984 training clips, 1,147 development clips and 1,311 held clips as the pooled ridge study. All timed linear-head work runs on the same physical CPU with one Torch and BLAS thread, using float64. GPU feature extraction is common to these head methods. Input standardization and random seed, architecture and readout dimensions are identical in each comparison. Encoder weights remain fixed.

For the 15 frozen audiovisual matrices (five arms × three seeds), train a 256→72 affine multinomial head using actual Torch reverse-mode gradients and full-batch L-BFGS with strong-Wolfe line search. Start each candidate at zero coefficients and intercept equal to the log empirical training prior. Use cross-entropy plus half λ times squared coefficient norm, with λ in 0.01, 0.1 and 1. Use up to 25 iterations and 32 closure calls per candidate (the backend line search may take additional closures). Select the lowest development NLL with tie order as written and no temperature scaling. Save selected parameters before opening cold outcomes. Report total candidate fitting plus development time, actual closures and cold top-1 and NLL. The matching ridge classifier uses its own squared-loss model-selection grid as predeclared. Their loss functions differ; their final predictive metrics and measured elapsed times can be compared directly at the bounded optimization budgets.

A separate experiment compares exact and iterative optimization of *identical* centered ridge objectives. Use the λ and score multiplier of each preselected class ridge head. Time single-thread, float64 Cholesky factorization and two triangular solves after a common covariance/RHS construction. Time full-batch Nesterov gradient iterations on those same sufficient statistics, including the symmetric eigenvalue computation needed to select its step and momentum. The iterative solver starts at zero. Stop at the first relative objective gap ≤0.000001 of its initial excess or at 1,000 iterations. The exact optimum supplies stopping diagnostics, and the cost of diagnostic gap checks is reported separately from update time. Report the number of iterations, relative gap, numerical status, times and any failure to reach tolerance. This comparison concerns convex readout optimization and is distinct from training or editing hidden network layers.
