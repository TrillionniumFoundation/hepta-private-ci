# Excitability under sustained drive with separate physical and financial drifts

The existing finite neural population translates its grid with the change of endogenous boundary. With constant boundary and a reset at fixed relative distance, the transport kernel settles to the same finite matrix for every drive. Add a trainable physical drift gain, μphys=μfinancial−g tanh(u), to both physical transport and future-hit probabilities. The equity and credit-loss pricing operators continue to use risk-neutral pricing coefficients. The new gain is optional and absent from legacy state dictionaries.

Check the separate gain in both actual layers, dense and FFT propagation on uniform grids, and with direct pricing and cached pricing. Preserve the existing default model outputs and gradients. Validate the new gain by finite differences and compare dense to FFT for all parameters, input gradients and recurrent state. Measure the stationary response to negative/zero/positive constant drive on finite meshes and compare to the analytic continuous renewal rheobase in the unbounded model. The bounded model has a top bin and will in general have a positive steady flux even when the unbounded rate is zero.
