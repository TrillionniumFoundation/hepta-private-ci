# Differentiation through the full stationary contract

The physical-network benchmarks set most He–Xiong contract coefficients in advance. Implement a standalone batched PyTorch pricing function that allows ordinary reverse-mode derivatives through margin, rate, payout, volatility, tax, recovery, aggregate coupon, aggregate principal, maturity and liquidity premium. It returns endogenous boundary, equity, credit loss, first passage and discounted first passage. Use the closed resolvent and finite Gaussian tail primitives, not root finding, ODE integration in the forward graph, or parameter lookup tables. Follow the original absorption and pricing conventions, including zero premium and vanishing log drift. Support FP64 reference execution and report precision rather than assuming it.

Compare values and first-order Jacobians against a separate SciPy finite-maturity first-hit integral, smooth-fit boundary, and adaptive equity initial-value ODE. Use at least 10 fixed contracts, positive margins, the zero-premium limit with one-sided differences, and a contract with zero log drift. Test batched versus scalar calculations and the zero-margin boundary convention. Distinguish inference-time quote differentiation from evidence about how identifiable or effective a whole trained encoder is.
