## Remaining deliverables with concrete boundaries

1. A separately frozen expansion must register actual old abilities across the
   requested families; the current complementary-labeled receipt retention
   panel must remain intact as historical evidence.
2. Genuine externally controlled evaluator/owner identities and authenticated
   receipts must be connected prospectively. A new receipt cannot create
   historical FinalUse authority.
3. Source grouping and grouping heuristics need independent origin/custody
   assurance if the claim requires more than a hash-consistent single-domain
   dataset. Generic multimodal generalization needs actual additional domains.
4. A future confirmation study needs its own sample-size/precision justification,
   frozen comparator/model identity, and unexposed source allocation; present
   four-fit pilot results and failed thresholds remain unchanged.
5. Full machine energy/charges/setup costs and the selected owner-approved
   conversion remain outside this metadata audit. The research latency weight
   `0.01` is explicitly a research preference, not that conversion.
