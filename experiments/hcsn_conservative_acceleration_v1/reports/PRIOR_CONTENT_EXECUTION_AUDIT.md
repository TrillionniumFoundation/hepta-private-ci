## Stage 4: retained distributions and mechanism comparisons

CONTENT002 provides an executed comparison of the eight-cell active distribution with two Erlang refractory phases, an active-mean-collapse control, an input-conditioned boundary, same-information ordinary features and ordinary dual gates. Its exact numerical profile and approximations remain part of the model definition. Retained marginal distributions do not preserve every high-dimensional correlation, and active-mean collapse preserves the refractory phases; it is not a no-reset or full reset-law ablation. [C]

The original strict analysis is **175/176 valid**. The one invalid search is `fsdd / transformer / seed9202`, with `AssertionError: cross entropy summary`. That original decision is retained. Any separate numerical diagnosis must be identified separately and cannot silently convert this report into a 176/176 result. [C]

Each mechanism statistic averages the two fixed tasks within each of eight paired seeds. Four exact two-sided sign-flip tests share one Holm family:

| Comparator to free-boundary distribution J/G | Paired difference, percentage points | Nominal 95% interval, percentage points | Holm p | Positive gate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Input-conditioned boundary J/G | +0.470 | [−1.939, +2.879] | 1.00000 | No |
| Same-information ordinary physical features | +0.012 | [−2.418, +2.443] | 1.00000 | No |
| Active-mean collapse J/G | +0.360 | [−2.504, +3.224] | 1.00000 | No |
| Ordinary two gates | −48.398 | [−51.039, −45.756] | 0.03125 | No |

All four positive mechanism gates fail. The first three comparisons do not establish equivalence; the intervals remain compatible with either sign of a modest effect on these fixed tasks. The ordinary-gates comparison favors the control under this registered budget. These outcomes do not prove that every possible free-boundary architecture must fail. They do prevent treating these particular mechanisms as empirically established benefits. [C]

Joint trainability and mechanism benefit are distinct questions. The original analysis records actual parameter-group gradients and changes; the companion stage-1–3 audit reviews those records. The current polynomial rearrangement changes numerical execution cost, not the meaning or outcome of this old mechanism experiment.

## Stage 5: content quality, traditional SNN and multimodal scope

The core uses three seeds, paired frontend/readout initialization, matched active backbone counts within the registered allowance, the same actual minibatch prefix, two learning-rate candidates and fixed 15/30/60 training-CPU selection gates. CPU allocation permits different update counts. It is a post-baseline study on previously observed datasets, not fresh-test confirmation. [C]

The LIF supplement has 18/18 valid pairs and three paired seeds per task. Its recurrent current-based LIF cell uses hard 0/1 forward spikes, a fast-sigmoid surrogate backward, learned positive leak/threshold and subtractive reset with a detached spike. It has no explicit refractory phase. Dense PyTorch execution provides no neuromorphic energy measurement. [L]

Distribution J/G exceeds that LIF control on copy (+9.001 percentage points) and MQAR (+7.031 points), but trails on irregular ID (−3.516), MNIST (−16.471), FSDD (−2.799) and digit multimodal selection (−9.766). On irregular OOD it trails by −19.336 points. These are descriptive, task-specific differences; they do not support a general claim that HCSN is better than SNN. [L]

### Multimodal and sampling limits

CONTENT002's multimodal task combines queried digit image/audio information. It is not a natural paired audiovisual retrieval experiment. FSDD uses 600 speaker-disjoint recordings with offline features and repeated episodes; real tasks have no registered OOD split. Synthetic OOD doubles sequence length, and irregular OOD also multiplies the duration distribution by three. [C]

The separately published real-AV design uses a fixed 32-observation clock with `dt=1`, replacing an unexecuted original profile whose duration input could expose a paired-retrieval shortcut. Its scope is a small acted audiovisual-emotion corpus. The published design and train-only preflight do not supply a completed heldout result at this parent commit. [A]

The sampling protocol holds each full input token constant while subdividing its interval by factors 1, 2 or 4. It preserves external time endpoints and the exact label-integral oracle. This does not imply a semigroup for the learned encoder: duration/elapsed-time inputs can change intermediate embeddings, coefficients and gates. The parent publishes this design and registration, but no completed refinement result in the audited paths/indexes. [S]

## Stage 6: computation, recovery and real owner actions

### Earlier v1 scaling evidence

Published `scaling002` records 48 performance configurations and six width/depth configurations on CPU, with one warmup and three timing repetitions. Its final model includes regenerative J/G values and the initial event atom. It explicitly marks `quality_measured=false` and `energy_measured=false`. [V]

For its specific B2, width32, depth2 inference stream, persistent tensor state is 2,056 bytes at lengths 32, 64, 128, 256 and 512. Maximum batch-versus-step logit difference is `3.5762786865234375e-07`. A separate numeric-state recovery case resumes 143 suffix steps in a child process and has zero reported logit/state error. This supports the tested recurrent execution and recovery behavior. Baseline caches were unmeasured, and peak RSS is a cumulative whole-process high-water mark. Neither result establishes memory or cost superiority at matched task quality. [V]

### Earlier trained stopping-head pilot

Published `native003` trains a price-conditioned stopping head on one fixed HCSN backbone and routes its continuous score through the actual pinned native owner. The read-only receipt audit reports 1,157 native child processes, 7,324 actual neural steps/owner ticks, zero nonzero child exits and zero float/Q24 action disagreements. Its 964 recorded errors are deliberate post-STOP rejection probes. [N, R]

For 128 matched first-prefix image pairs, changing only the public read price changes actual KEEP to STOP and actual model-call counts from 32 to 1. This demonstrates real price-responsive execution for that trained head. It does not isolate the contribution of every internal neural channel. Its exercised restart probes use the same Python interpreter; the selected ordinary MLP stops immediately and supplies no exercised recovery case. [R]

The value head's reported macro read utility is 0.127708, versus 0.076563 for the registered ordinary MLP, which collapsed to first-prefix stopping. A clearly labelled posthoc ordinary fixed-prefix schedule selected on validation achieves 0.173333, exceeding the value head by 0.045625. The value head wins none of the 36 complete CPU-cost scenarios in that adversarial audit. Consequently, the pilot supports trained causal execution, but not controller or neuron-architecture superiority. [N]

### Trained size/cost extension and this new acceleration package

The published cost-scaling design specifies 72 candidates, two tasks, four models, three sizes and three paired seeds. Its quality qualification requires both test accuracies to reach the target and differ by no more than two percentage points across every paired seed. The read-only review explicitly describes a static design review, not execution of that matrix. Completed aggregate quality/cost claims are unavailable from the audited parent evidence. [Z]

The current package's `model_tests003`, `stream_smoke002`, `native_smoke002`, `native_causal_smoke002` and `benchmark002` are separate engineering evidence. They test implementation fidelity, normalization, recovery, real-owner value-message causality and synthetic execution cost. They do not revise the old content scores or establish an accuracy, energy or mechanism advantage.

The new 70-job study's outcomes are deliberately excluded from this prior-evidence audit. This report asserts neither its completion nor trained-checkpoint acceptance. The separately published `EXECUTION_BINDING_PLAN.json` fixes the future trained execution check to `fixed / selective_copy / hcsn_accelerated / seed98031` and that job's validation-selected `selected.npz`. That selection rule is not based on the best test result.

## Interpretation

The parent commit supports implemented, differentiable finite-distribution neurons and several executed small empirical and engineering studies. The published content results do not establish general superiority over ordinary gates, Transformer or LIF. Natural-AV, sampling and the new trained size/cost completion claims require published outcome evidence beyond the design materials present in this parent.

No published result audited here measures GPU training, accelerator memory, hardware joules or foundation-scale quality. CPU seconds must remain CPU seconds; constant recurrent state must remain a statement about the measured state; actual native causality must remain a statement about the tested execution route.
