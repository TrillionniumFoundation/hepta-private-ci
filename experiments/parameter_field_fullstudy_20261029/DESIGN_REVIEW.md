The substantive new candidate changes three things together: labels continue under the same strong ordinary baseline used for fallback; label construction and deployment use the same guard48 transition; the deployed fixed head is calibrated on a separate schedule partition. Full branch-state restoration prevents a candidate action from inheriting another action's parameters, momentum, gradients or randomness.

For identical data, normalization and ridge penalty, fitting differences of labels is algebraically equivalent to subtracting the corresponding fitted linear outputs. Calling old Q differences advantages would therefore add no predictor. New aligned branch outcomes and compatible dynamics are the actual intervention.

Old `NOT_REGISTERED` labels describe an earlier unexecuted proposal. They do not override this session's authorization to prepare a new study. Conversely, new authorization does not rewrite an old failed gate, manufacture an old exact-regression pass, or authorize an unrelated raw-data destination.

## 3. The state, the label and the actual transition must agree

For baseline policy b, the new stored label is

`A_b(s,a;omega) = G_b(s,a;omega) - G_b(s,b;omega)`.

Every branch starts from an independently restored full state, executes one candidate guard48 transaction and then follows b to the common terminal step 288. All four branches share declared data/noise marks. Baseline advantage is structurally zero. The label is a realized paired return, not an exact conditional expected Q and not the value of the subsequently replanned policy.

Reference-tail values can support policy improvement. Under a complete state, identical legal actions and transition kernel, and true nonnegative expected reference advantages at every state occupied by the new policy, telescoping gives improvement relative to that baseline. The fact that `Q_b` differs from `Q_pi` is not by itself an algorithm error. What is unproved here is the substantive hypothesis on the new policy's occupancy and estimation error.

Each transition includes the original immutable guard, rollback, consumed schedule, remaining resources and optimizer semantics. Rejected work advances the clock and is charged. Zero multiplier still executes the scheduled gradients and guard while leaving parameters and momentum unadvanced. It is not a free stop action.

The restore contract includes incoming gradients and RNG and applies only between complete transactions with no pending autograd graph or concurrent writer. Successful PyTorch copies change tensor version counters; no claim is made to restore an already constructed autograd graph.

Compare effective actions using dynamically relevant after-state, not just the requested multiplier or parameter vector. Momentum, RNG, clock, guard, budget and controller memory matter. If a history controller records different past actions, identical physical model state alone does not establish equality for arbitrary future policies. Costs remain separate even when scientific transitions coincide.

## 4. Field, full-current-state and history are distinct comparisons

### Field12

Use the existing twelve named observables, including directional response and momentum information. The model, probe identities, guard, clock convention, baseline, fitted head and normalizer are declared context. This is not a context-free twelve-dimensional Markov sufficiency claim.

### Complete-current-state predictor

Richer30 is not complete state: additional norms, losses and margins remain summaries. The new comparator instead receives every declared currently mutable parameter/buffer, momentum and relevant gradient value, absence masks, and logical progress/resource coordinates in a fixed immutable environment. Exact ordered names and numerical dimension must be recorded at freeze.

A float64 dual ridge solve can fit this high-dimensional linear predictor without constructing a dense parameter-by-parameter normal matrix. It is algebraically the same fixed ridge model. High input dimension may still worsen estimation, conditioning or effective model complexity; equal ridge lambda does not make the three representations equally expressive.

This is a full-current-state **predictor**, not a full-information optimal oracle. An empirical field/full-state difference mixes available information, estimation and policy errors. It cannot automatically be relabeled the irreducible information term in the paper's exact tangent-oracle identity.

Replay-only remaining tapes and future outcomes are excluded. Having a future tape in a checkpoint does not make it a legitimate predictor input. The full-state comparator cannot see the actual future mini-batches, realized branch labels or endpoint scores.

### Same-information history6

History retains six chronological slots from the lawful observation stream. Each nineteen-coordinate completed slot contains its H12, four-coordinate executed-action indicator, observed guard acceptance, scheduled microsteps divided by 48, and validity mask. The current decision slot contains only H12 and validity; its future action/guard/work fields stay zero. Future slots are right-padded with zeros. Dimension is exactly 114; no future transaction is supplied.

History adds memory of already disclosed observations and decisions, not a new observation channel. It still computes expensive H12 probes and must not be called a cheap controller. It receives no audit-observer or withheld task information. Field/history is the relevant test of compression versus access to the same past stream. Full-current-state intentionally measures a different, richer current-information comparison.

All three learned heads use the same paired outcomes, fit/calibration partition, fixed ridge and threshold procedure. Record matrix ranks and conditioning rather than claiming equal effective degrees of freedom.

## 5. Calibration and occupancy

Schedules 6000–6002 are fit only; schedule 6003 is calibration only. Both prefixes and all six states of a schedule remain in one partition. Step-zero duplicates are recorded and are not counted as independent cases. Normalizers are fit once from fit rows; calibration and deployment do not change them.

For each fixed model and head, tau is the nonnegative maximum calibration overprediction of a nonbaseline advantage. A unique best nonbaseline action is used only when its predicted advantage is strictly above tau; ties and inadequate margins retain b.

There are only twelve dependent calibration states per model. Their maximum does not imply a deployment-uniform bound, ordinary 95% split-conformal coverage, or an expected-value guarantee. `certificate=false` remains mandatory. A new policy may visit states not represented by baseline/exploration prefixes. Distances and residual diagnostics can reveal this problem; they cannot prove it absent.

No post-outcome threshold relaxation, temperature tuning, extra bank collection or head refitting is included. A future data-aggregation experiment would require its own prospective scope, sample count and cost.

## 7. The genuine architecture factorial

The implementation provided by the architecture work package derives a trainable portfolio from the existing `NeuralPortfolio`. It replaces only the text encoder and decoder with 24-to-width input projections and `_ContinuousCell(width, mode, direct)` from commit `68eb16caa9c14c7b5729585c86d9c293018a987c`. Proposed portfolio width is 32, giving encoder width 16 and decoder width 32.

Dual and ordinary modes have the same content/voltage/adaptation/event state shapes and the same active parameter tensors; they differ in the FPT/interval-reset versus sigmoid event law. Common modules and matched temporal tensors start bitwise equal within a seed. Every portfolio parameter is trainable. The implemented width-32 models contain 540,328 trainable parameters, including 12,064 temporal parameters; actual nonzero gradient coverage remains a separate warm-up gate rather than following merely from `requires_grad=True`.

The 784-pixel digit / 1568-pixel pair and caption task remains real generator training. Remaining latent MLP actions are unchanged. Do not describe this as replacing all generator control, a literal analytic Call-Put model, a task-value-derived natural free boundary, or a large language model experiment.

The primary four arms are dual/baseline, dual/field, ordinary/baseline and ordinary/field. The interaction is

`(J_dual_field - J_dual_baseline) - (J_ordinary_field - J_ordinary_baseline)`.

A positive field increment inside Hepta alone does not establish that interaction. Report both architecture-specific increments, the interaction, dual-field versus ordinary-field, every seed, retention and costs. Eight paired training seeds give limited replication and approximate intervals, not a universal architecture theorem.

New architectures require compatible labels, guard semantics and head fitting. Importing old generator heads is a separate transfer hypothesis, not automatically the same controller. C's fifth field coordinate is relative full-parameter displacement from its own warmstart, `||theta-theta_warmstart||_2 / max(||theta_warmstart||_2, epsilon)`, with the numeric epsilon frozen in code. This replaces the old LoRA effective-operator displacement because C trains the whole portfolio. C uses development seeds 701/702 and evaluation seeds 801–808, shared within architecture/controller pairs. All twenty architecture/seed warmstarts train all parameters for 400 steps, batch 32, SGD learning rate .01/momentum .9, clip 1, joint loss without a teacher anchor. Carry warm-up SGD momentum, parameter groups and RNG into continuation rather than silently resetting them. The declared C microstep ends in `zero_grad(set_to_none=True)`; all-None boundary gradients are real state, not an omitted inventory.

Continuation is 96 steps in six macro16 transactions, using fit_gradient batch 32 and fit_rehearsal batch 16, teacher anchor .1 to that path's immutable warmstart, and its own immutable warmstart reward-clean guard at threshold zero. The conventional baseline is fixed action index 2 for every architecture and seed; it is not retrospectively selected as a tuned optimum.

For each architecture and each development seed, schedules 7000–7003 and two predetermined prefixes supply 48 states. The balanced action is `(schedule_index + decision_index) % 4`. Four actions per state produce 768 total paired reference-tail labels across both architectures and seeds. Pool each architecture's two development seeds: 72 fit rows and 24 calibration rows. Fit its field12 head at ridge 1 and apply the same one-sided strict gate. Evaluation has exactly 32 main paths using common schedule ID 8000 and distinct paired training seeds. The full `ArchitecturePlan` is copied into the draft as explicit execution choices. Warm-up uses horizon `t % 5` and the direct action sequence `ACTIONS[(t+j) % 4]`; continuation and fixed panels use one `couple` action. Displacement uses norm epsilon `1e-12`. An optional GRU arm requires its own pre-outcome activation and budget and cannot substitute for any main arm.

C's persistent model checkpoints are four development warmstarts, sixteen evaluation warmstarts and thirty-two endpoints. Complete branch states are independently restored in memory; their exact input/transition/output hashes, labels, schedule, trace and replay recipe are retained. A reconstructed branch is not a literal restore of original saved branch tensor bytes. Four independently reconstructed branches reload each development architecture/seed warmstart and context, then replay schedule 7000, the balanced prefix, decision 2 and action 1. Each costs two prefix and four candidate/tail macro16 blocks, adding 384 audit microsteps to 57,152 scientific microsteps: 57,536 physical microsteps in total. Compare deterministic state, tape, guard, output and transition identities, excluding elapsed/CPU time and work counters from equality. A mismatch stops the phase. The planned C raw cap is 350 MiB with 2 GiB allocatable reserve.

## 8. Costs, statistics and stopping

Measure training-bank construction, feature reverses, teachers, observers, head fitting, attempted/rejected/zero work, checkpoint/replay, audit, wall/CPU time, peak memory and storage. Ordinary does not compute an unused field. Equal gradient counts are not equal total compute. Squared parameter increments are not physical energy. Scientific utility and resource use are reported separately; no favorable post-hoc utility-per-second price or amortization horizon is introduced.

B adds a genuinely low-overhead fixed taper `[b,b,2,1,1,0]`, where b is the historically selected ordinary index. It has no fit and no additional feature probes; it is not called a tuned optimum or a fitted cosine schedule. Field-versus-taper task and taper-versus-frozen clean are two secondary comparisons in the same ten-comparison interval family as the original eight; actual cost is reported separately.

B uses paired schedules within each of three fixed models and shared source-pair resampling; it does not resample those three models into a claimed model population. C resamples the eight paired training seeds across all four arms plus shared source pairs. Both retain both observers, predefined families and approximate interval limitations. Original gate definitions are preserved; any new favorable result has the new protocol's stated scope only.

Integrity failure or resource exhaustion stops the affected phase and preserves partial evidence. Missing libraries, assets or unsupported runtime are named precisely and produce prepared/not-executed or execution-incomplete status. A synthetic replacement cannot be reported as the requested real-kernel run. A recovery must preserve exact state and be independently documented before continuation.

If field makes zero overrides and complete same-guard terminal identities match ordinary, this candidate is inactive. Keep that result, account for its extra observation cost and do not reduce tau. B remains a prespecified independent comparison, not an attempt to turn equality into significance. Main C arms remain required for the separately frozen factorial.

Draft ceilings are A: 6 CPU / 3 wall hours; B: 8 CPU / 4 wall hours; C: 12 CPU / 6 wall hours. Root can revise them using engineering measurements before freeze. After outcome access, a cap is an actual cap. Serializer-measured raw, partial-write, archive and full-restore needs plus reserve govern storage; no compression saving or deletion of adverse runs is assumed.

Completion of all work packages means every registered operation and comparison has a truthful disposition. It does not require every proposed mechanism to win, does not overwrite old rejected studies, and does not activate a production owner.
