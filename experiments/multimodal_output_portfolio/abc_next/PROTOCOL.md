## Public information and conditional value

The deployed reader is a separate image/mask CNN trained on the generator's
existing training role. It does not load either outcome observer. Its probabilities
are approximate features, not qualified posteriors. The new 90-dimensional summary
uses original public pixels, declared hint reliability, the command, actual draft
pixels and actual emitted tokens. The generator and its actual Bernoulli/AR
sampling law remain frozen. The fitted values never receive hidden target labels,
future draws, hindsight-best plan identities or private-source IDs as features.

Four candidates cross base/enriched public features (673/763 dimensions) and
32/128 hidden units. They share the same fit sources, training steps, source folds
and selection budget. Input dimension and hidden width change actual compute and
are measured, not asserted equal. Every candidate uses two source folds for Q1:
all views and generation scenarios from one source pair remain together. Q2
regresses an actual-child backup from the other fold's Q1. Deployed Q1 is then
refitted on all fit sources; this differs from the fold heads and is an approximate
fitted policy, not an exact Bellman solution or a uniform-error guarantee.

Before any main development outcomes, the already-completed common-history
diagnostic identified a structural constraint: `refine_semantic` at H1 retains
the actual draft and pays 0.002. Stop therefore strictly dominates it for every
target and future draw. Every new candidate masks that H1 action and every new
one-shot controller masks programs ending in semantic. H2 semantic stays legal.
The historical controller is replayed unchanged. This shared structural fix is
not evidence that enriched features alone caused an improvement.

The separate H1 information experiment has its own frozen finite target prior,
public histories, fit futures and independent evaluation futures. Empirical
target/future oracle relaxations are diagnostics for that law. They do not bound
causal control on the original partially observed task population.

## Training and inference estimands

Generator arms are the existing SFT continuation, actual-output mean-loss
training and actual-output risk-moment training. The latter two share the fixed
0.05 auxiliary term, warmstart, sources, update budget and generation law. This
round independently evaluates their frozen operational objectives on new source
identities; it is not fresh generator training or a risk-only CE objective with
the auxiliary term removed. Training seeds are the same five historical seeds.

The two primary net-utility joint-CE contrasts are:

1. Risk-moment minus mean-loss generator under the original fixed policy.
2. New feedback minus its development-selected strongest nonfeedback policy,
   under the risk-moment generator.

The core training×feedback table, interaction, feedback versus original fixed,
new versus historical feedback, both observers, all views, completion, factual
success, mean loss and tail loss are also reported. They cannot replace failed
primary contrasts. Net utility uses the pre-existing explicit proxy penalty
0.002 per continuation action; measured milliseconds and feature/critic work are
reported separately. No market price of computation or universal NDU profile is
inferred from that proxy.

Primary intervals are nominal 97.5% paired source×seed percentile bootstrap,
4,000 draws. Views, scenarios, candidate replays and policy copies do not create
new independent source groups. Seeds are averaged after computing within-model
risk. A source-only interval conditional on the fixed five trained models is
also reported. Other intervals are descriptive 95%. Five training seeds and four
generation scenarios do not guarantee population coverage or identify precise
conditional tails. Positive benefit requires a positive lower primary endpoint;
negative/uncertain outcomes remain blockers for the corresponding benefit claim.

## Execution and costs

Development trees execute all 21 actual candidate paths. Actual binary pixels
are losslessly bit-packed with unmodified AR tokens, closed, reopened, identity
checked, and only then assigned offline outcomes. Large raw archives are not
pushed. Formal control execution commits root decisions before producing any
future child and commits child decisions before its grandchild; common owner-side
forks pair policy comparisons. Batched counterfactual work is explicitly distinct
from single-request latency. Main-study JSON decision records are closed before
their corresponding future draws, but are not fsynced or claimed crash-durable;
native durable journal tests are a separate, explicitly measured chain.
Separate sequential timing includes initialization,
public feature extraction, critic decisions, all actual generator work, and
serialization/witness components as named in each receipt.

## Native and temporal scope

The native profile is a calibrated observed-output correctness head encoded into
the existing neuron confidence interface. It is not a future latent-dynamics
predictor. Actual native admission, tick resources, durable journal, restored
anchor and same-context proposal are checked separately; no calibration record
stands in for a candidate-update qualification or authenticated host authority.
Resident-head cost is separated from complete upstream generation/feature cost.

The temporal branch produces real new text and Piper speech from shared semantic
state, with committed prefixes, delayed observations and independent frozen
Whisper outcome evaluation. Online Vosk is a controller observation, not the sole
independent outcome judge. A fixed acoustic generator plus learned semantic
control is not end-to-end joint acoustic training, natural open-domain speech,
video synthesis or a proof of global multimodal portfolio optimality.
