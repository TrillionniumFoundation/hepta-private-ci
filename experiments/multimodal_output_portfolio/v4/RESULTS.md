## What was implemented

The text encoder supplies a fixed eight-class semantic condition q. Four
separate latent states hold synthetic text, audio, image and video generation
states. `REFINE_m` executes the learned refiner on one selected state; the other
three stay unchanged. `EMIT` executes all four learned decoders. This is actual
modality compute allocation; q is not a modality allocation vector.

The finite controller compares emission and all feasible refinements through a
two-step entropic Bellman recursion. It maximizes the joint four-output utility
under a 351,232-Linear-MAC online cap and reserves all 330,240 MAC needed for
complete emission. A separate Rust owner selects from real candidate values,
binds the initial committed plan when applicable, charges the chosen operation,
and requires actual four-state and four-output acknowledgments.

All weights are inherited and frozen. There are no new training updates in v4.
The model has eight known synthetic concepts, a known clean anchor, artificial
initial disturbances and a known three-branch innovation law. Future draws are
revealed only after native action commitment. The complete reachable support is
compiled before evaluation worlds. This does not validate unseen-support or
natural all-modal foundation-model generalization, or a scalable learned critic.
