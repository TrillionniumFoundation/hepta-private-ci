This package implements the next five work packages and records the remaining
qualification barriers. `BLOCKERS.json` is the machine-readable register. A
mechanism result, an empirical development result, a fresh qualification result,

## Objective and execution identity

For a declared input/task distribution and complete policy, the risk objective is

\[
J_\gamma(\theta,\pi)=-\gamma^{-1}\log
\mathbb E[\exp\{-\gamma(U_\psi(Y_T,X)-C_{0:T})\}].
\]

The versioned profile binds terminal scoring, cost scope and prices, stopping,
resource/admission rules, risk aggregation and the actual continuation algorithm
family. Generator/actor parameters remain optimization variables; each physical
episode separately binds their exact values together with the observer, emission
law and native owner. A frozen policy cannot borrow another policy's calibration.
The gamma-zero mean learner is explicitly identified and evaluated under the
common gamma-two evaluation profile; it does not masquerade as the risk learner.

Population risk applies the logarithm after averaging the exponential moments
across sources. It is not the average of source-conditional certainty equivalents.
The actor fits moments of the complete return under the action and continuation
that will actually execute, including refusals and failures. It does not combine
independently scored modalities into a fictitious joint event.

`profile_contract.py`, `control_episode.py`, `control_actor.py` and
`control_calibration_run.py` implement this contract. Model setup, gradient replay,
backward passes, checkpoint and report work are reported separately from the
registered deployment episode boundary; they are not hidden in the total study
bill. This is a declared episode objective, not an unqualified claim that every
process-lifetime cost is inside every episode's exponential.

## Natural task and completed observer result

All 18 registered observer candidates and 3,072 MLP updates completed. On the
independent frozen 64-source calibration, action accuracy is **21/64 (32.8125%)**
and sound accuracy is **30/64 (46.875%)**. Both fail the unchanged 60 percent gate;
sound also trails its 37/64 majority baseline. Neither calibration label set
contains an unseen training class. Independent arithmetic and source auditing
confirms the negative result. Actual judge/study entrypoints refuse to proceed.
Warmstart003, actual NDU/mean generation training, policy calibration and final
qualification have zero new executions in this study record. See
[the natural report](natural_v3_report.md) and
[the retained result](natural_results/NATURAL_V3_RESULT.json).


This is bounded missing-segment reconstruction plus official annotation-template
caption generation. It does not establish open-domain speech/video generation,
independent static-image semantic adequacy, or general foundation-model retention.
Actual generated text, audio, image and video are written, reopened and scored as
one event. Inside a deployed episode, private targets and labels are read only
by the terminal scorer. Warmstart and observer fitting use their explicitly
assigned training targets. The independent scorer owns the frozen task vocabulary;
model or checkpoint metadata cannot redefine the expected caption. Reuse of
policy-calibration/qualification sources across freezes is rejected before
loading their rows, and an initial codec failure remains preserved even if a
later retry delivers successfully.

The training-coverage amendment is based only on official training metadata and
was registered before the new observer fit or model evaluation predictions. It
retains the original 768 source identities and roles and adds 128 generator-only
and 128 observer-only training videos. The resulting roles are 256 generator
training, 512 observer training, and 64 each for development, observer calibration,
policy calibration and qualification. The original held-out cohorts and 60 percent
observer thresholds remain fixed. Actual training coverage is 54/63 action and
14/17 sound classes for the generator, 55/63 and 14/17 for the observer; the full
63/17 ontology remains in the task.

Warmstart 003 remains blocked; its registered plan requires replaying all 2,048
updates from the initial seed. The interrupted 002 checkpoint lacks Adam/RNG recovery state and cannot be
represented as an exact optimizer resume. Sequential microbatches preserve the
specified batch size and update count; their measured finite-precision difference
from a dense batch is recorded. Full recovery snapshots include model, optimizer
and sampler/global RNG states.

Actual NDU/mean training runs two complete `no_grad` physical episodes under the
same parameters before likelihood replay. Replay uses the identical public
inputs and already sampled pre-codec actions, with parameter, tensor and density
checks; it does not resample, replan or repeat scoring. The joint two-episode score
and predictable historical baseline avoid assuming that sequential CPU costs are
independent. Remaining direct parameter dependence of physical timing is an
explicit conditional-exogeneity assumption.

The final primary policy is fixed in advance. Strong interpolation, frozen neural
means, frozen stochastic generation, matched mean training, and same-generator
fixed keep/retry controls retain their actual costs. An inadequate independent
observer prevents access to generator qualification. A failed efficacy gate is
retained as a failed result.
