# Independent review of the V3 results

Both registered continuation routes failed their scientific gates. The learning
route stopped at development because H12 did not improve on the selected strong
ordinary training control. The independently registered frozen-model runtime
route completed its held-out audit and failed both dual-observer gain
comparisons. Its two clean noninferiority comparisons passed.

The source, actual-output, arithmetic, and original-kernel verification receipts
all passed on their first actual-data executions. They support the integrity of
these failed qualification decisions. Primary confirmation, final retention,
learning audit/final training, and non-frozen runtime qualification remain
unrun under the registered stopping rules.

## Learning: the strong ordinary control remains decisive

The independent endpoint audit reconstructed all 96 complete development paths,
the original endpoints, controller identities, output byte hashes, and the
registered comparator selection. Its selection agrees exactly with the study
selection: maximum absolute discrepancy is zero. A separate observer audit
recomputed all 253,440 retained hard outputs and all 506,880 reward/audit values
at the original arithmetic batch size. The receipts join exactly on all three
completion and endpoint-array hashes.

The ordinary control selected action indices 2, 3, and 3 for seeds 17, 29, and
43, corresponding to the registered multipliers 1, 2, and 2. The globally
selected eligible adaptive comparator was `richer`.

| Paired development utility difference | Reward observer | Audit observer | Registered condition |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Ordinary minus frozen | +0.02481205 | +0.02371545 | Passed the pooled and per-seed gain conditions |
| H12 minus ordinary | -0.00981172 | -0.00764090 | Failed the strictly positive pooled-gain condition |
| H12 minus selected richer control | +0.00295071 | +0.00241157 | Passed the nonnegative pooled condition |

Five of the six registered development checks passed. The failed
H12-versus-ordinary condition is sufficient to stop the learning route. These
are developmental point comparisons, including comparator selection on this
panel. No independent learning-audit confidence interval or confirmatory
learning superiority claim was obtained. The favorable development comparison
against the richer adaptive control does not satisfy the ordinary-control gate.

The actual-output audit confirms the values used in this decision; it does not
turn a developmental comparison into a confirmatory one. No later representation,
ridge, controller, or ordinary comparator was substituted after the result.

## Runtime: reward-only evidence does not satisfy the joint gate

The runtime audit used 512 source-pair clusters, four conditions, the three fixed
historical model seeds, and five arms. All 30,720 selected episodes were retained.
Source-pair draws were shared across arms, conditions, seeds, observers, and the
clean-condition subset. The registered 4,000-draw bootstrap, seed 271034, and
four-comparison correction give a 98.75% two-sided interval per observer and
comparison. Gain acceptance also requires a positive mean in every fixed model
seed. Both observers must pass.

All values below are differences in the registered net utility, including the
initial-plus-selected-revision charge. They are not changes in classification
accuracy.

| Registered comparison | Reward mean [98.75% interval] | Audit mean [98.75% interval] | Joint result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Native minus fixed | +0.00689463 [+0.00325445, +0.01061896] | +0.00083995 [-0.00266021, +0.00421949] | Gain not established |
| Native minus full history | +0.00095486 [-0.00219384, +0.00408191] | +0.00019802 [-0.00288085, +0.00323427] | Gain not established |
| Native minus fixed, clean | +0.00756903 [+0.00145740, +0.01351795] | +0.00193752 [-0.00344069, +0.00732172] | Noninferiority passed |
| Full history minus fixed, clean | +0.00482069 [-0.00074516, +0.01050144] | -0.00403348 [-0.00957016, +0.00131614] | Noninferiority passed |

The native-versus-fixed reward comparison passes its interval and all-seed sign
conditions. The audit observer's interval includes zero, and seed 29 has a
negative mean. Native-versus-history intervals include zero under both observers,
with mixed seed signs. Consequently, zero of the two registered gain comparisons
pass the dual-observer rule. The intervals do not establish equivalence or a
general absence of benefit.

The clean comparisons use the registered lower-bound threshold of -0.01, which
both methods clear under both observers. This supports clean noninferiority
relative to the selected fixed plan within this bounded runtime qualification
task. It does not establish the unrun final retention family or ordinary/field
model retention.

## The evidence chain is complete for the executed stages

The six fitting/selection banks were checked independently from their actual
H3M bytes: 2,304 complete trees, 195,840 terminal rewards, 48,384 public events,
and 46,080 partial-verifier feedback packets. All stored values and events match
exactly. The separate native fitting audit rebuilt 96 feature matrices and 432
candidate-owned continuation heads across the three seeds, with exact numerical
agreement for its reported coefficient, prediction, and MSE checks.

For held-out selected execution, the independent media audit reconstructed:

- 80,870 logical PNG/UTF-8/logit deliveries, represented by 44,385 physical H3M
  records containing 48,655,017 bytes;
- 70,398 public events and selected decisions, with each history derived from
  its own previously delivered outputs and permitted feedback;
- 39,678 post-delivery feedback packets and 61,440 complete terminal observer
  score records;
- 26,849 current/history head decisions, plus all executed fixed-plan choices;
- the complete media-to-native input join for 54,330 original-kernel ticks.

The original-kernel audit independently reexecuted every native/reset session.
Its input hashes and per-seed decision-coverage digests exactly match the media
audit, including causal prefixes, actions, stages, controller identities, and
terminal coverage. The media audit also reconstructs the complete selected-work
ledger and confirms the common initial output for each source case across arms.
There are no discrepancies in these checks.

Additional verification work is recorded separately from study execution. The
bank media audit used 258,048 reward-observer digit forwards. The selected media
audit used 64,682 reward and 42,110 audit digit forwards. The separate native
verification used 54,330 additional kernel ticks. These verification processes
performed no generator forwards and did not add independent experimental
observations. Retained public logits were checked against H3M bytes and their
public softmax recomputed; neural decoder logits were not regenerated.

## Implications for the proposed relationship

V3 supplies a more demanding test of the proposed architecture: full reference
tails for learning decisions, fresh source roles, complete policy rollouts, a
strong ordinary control, a delayed-feedback runtime task, and equal-information
history controls. Its executed evidence supports the use of those distinctions
when evaluating the relationship between parameter-based learning decisions and
runtime state control.

The current H12 implementation has not qualified an incremental learning benefit
over ordinary training. The original native state mechanism has not qualified
an incremental runtime benefit over full history or the required dual-observer
benefit over fixed control. The combined-layer confirmation was not reached.
V2's negative and uncertain conclusions remain part of the evidence record.

The learning endpoint is the historical single-action hard-output task, while
the runtime endpoint is a new constructed task with simulated external review.
The results cannot be presented as a single identical training/deployment
objective. Runtime feedback is simulated source-grounded evidence, and the
experiment remains a bounded MNIST-based PNG/UTF-8 setting with three fixed
small models. It does not test unrestricted LLM behavior or the paper's full
parameter-field theory.

The measurements do not isolate feature choice, the linear readout, reference-
tail mismatch, empirical guard behavior, or task/model scale as the cause of the
failed gates. Those explanations would require new prospectively specified
interventions. The native kernel retains `requires_calibration=true`, authority
`DENY_ALL`, and no production owner integration; local readout fitting makes no
production calibration claim.

## Final reporting and archive publication review

The final read-only review on 2026-10-27 confirmed that all four requested
reporting corrections were applied. The paper comments now state that the
independent learning audit was never opened after the development failure;
distinguish the measured derivative along positive `v = beta*m + clip(g)` from
the actual parameter motion `-eta*a*v`; describe parameters, optimizer, and RNG
as unchanged while incoming gradients are restored; and limit the verified
access claim to source-role allocation and executed stages. The blocker ledger
adds `inherited_evidence_paths_base` for inherited V2 relative evidence paths.
Every original V2 item field, including every closure criterion, remains
unchanged, and the original scope limits are preserved.

The reporting quantities agree with the authoritative receipts: zero of two
runtime gain comparisons pass, both local clean noninferiority comparisons
pass, and no new final six-comparison confirmation was executed. The execution
ledger records 21 completed and 42 qualification-skipped tasks, 1,181 file
bindings, and 18 completed verification receipts. The reports retain the fixed
three-model scope, the distinction between Qref and the deployed policy's own
value, the untested training-to-feedback-task transfer, and the absence of a
registered superadditive interaction claim. The only unresolved Markdown links
at this review were the intentionally pending restoration instructions and
external archive verification receipt.

The archive helper review identified and then confirmed closure of a specific
publication race: an ordinary rename could replace an empty destination created
after the initial existence check. The corrected implementation publishes only
through Linux `renameat2(RENAME_NOREPLACE)`, propagates errors, and has no
overwrite-capable fallback. Its new race contract creates that destination
immediately before publication and verifies that both its inode and the complete
verified `.partial` payload survive `FileExistsError`. The bound synthetic
receipts report 48 integrity checks and 16 archive-reanalysis checks passing.
No remaining launch blocker was identified in this focused source review.

This review does not certify an actual archive, persistence upload, or newly
executed scientific phase. Complete archive verification and archive-only
numerical reanalysis require their separate external execution receipt. No
scientific source, helper source, or root report was modified by this reviewer;
only this independent report was appended.
