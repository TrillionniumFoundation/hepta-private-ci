### Preserve the negative comparisons

The learned stateful study compared DP with same-data myopic VOI and obtained
0 wins, 143 ties and 1 loss in true enumerated utility. All 144 primary lifecycle
lower bounds at the registered 1,000 deployments were negative. The positive
comparisons against selected ablations or prior STOP do not replace that strong
baseline. The 88,866.778 acquisition total uses declared synthetic score prices;
it is not money paid to a provider. Hardware and engineering costs were unpriced.
[Original experiment](../ndu_learned_stateful/README.md).

The later pure-information intervention model makes terminal reward answer
correctness, independent of internal state. It isolates why preparation can be
useful. Its new proposed decision statistic is exactly equivalent to the
same-structure MLE plus DP rule, including the feasible count cases checked.
That proposed superiority comparison was therefore closed negatively before
sampling. The 36 contract/law pairs and 968 count triples are exact checks, not
new confirmation data. [Mechanism and diagnosis](../ndu_decision_relevance_20261106/README.md).

The finite-catalog result narrows the claim further. All candidate laws are
provided, calibration is regenerative and fully charged, and the efficiency
statement is pointwise as the error target tends to zero. The 45 deterministic
checks do not estimate empirical coverage or asymptotic performance. Its example
also has a 13-probe Bayes error about 0.09848678 despite passing a necessary KL
condition for a 0.05 target. The separate idealized deployment thresholds do not
amend the failed endpoint of the older study.
[Finite-catalog evidence](../ndu_finite_catalog_20261106/README.md).
