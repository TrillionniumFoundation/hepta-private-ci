## Why this change is needed

The retained research leaves three distinct gaps: necessary information bounds
do not prove that a common policy can meet a hard deadline; the JAC improvement
gate previously depended on oracle value intervals; and a runnable frozen-model
interface does not establish correct or beneficial tool use. Conflating these
gates would overstate the evidence and justify unnecessary expansion.

## What changes

- Add a finite observable-history solver with executable rational policies and
  independent rational lower certificates for deadline/resource attainability.
  Preserve the initial implementation and the budget-rounding counterexample.
- Build fixed-sample known-support confidence intervals from all 20 retained
  JAC banks, propagate safe downstream gains, and fix three independently found
  acceptance defects. Preserve the superseded implementation and all results.
- Add a known-law headroom diagnostic: even the true optimum cannot pay the
  inherited `.01` one-episode audit fee above any retained strong baseline.
- Execute the same frozen Qwen model through a fixed finite-action interface
  and actual SQLite/source tools; retain every result and the stronger literal
  routing comparison.
- Record the integrated scientific disposition, costs, limitations, source
  identities and review material without rewriting earlier negative studies.
