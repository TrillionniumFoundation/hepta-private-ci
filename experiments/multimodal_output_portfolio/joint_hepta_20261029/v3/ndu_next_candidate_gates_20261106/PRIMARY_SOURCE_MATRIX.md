# Nearest-neighbor audit for the next NDU candidate

Audit date: 2026-11-06. This is an internal primary-source scope audit, not an external review. No positive novelty verdict is asserted. Abstract-only items are marked accordingly. Search coverage is substantial but is not a proof that no other relevant paper exists.

## Findings that affect the research decision

The broad ingredients are already occupied: controlled observations with nonuniform cost, terminal performance versus acquisition cost, decision-focused experimental design, entropic best-policy identification, recursive discounted risk learning, real-time metareasoning, and anytime planning certificates. A new candidate must identify a specific algorithmic or complexity improvement under a frozen objective and access model. Combining these labels is insufficient.

| Primary source | What is actually established / modeled | Relation to this project and remaining distinction |
|---|---|---|
| [Essakine & Vernade, COLT 2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v336/essakine26a.html); [full arXiv v2, 2026-09-21](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.13717v2) | Finite-horizon tabular, forward episodic interaction, deterministic rewards in [0,1], trajectory entropic utility of the reward sum. KL bonuses, sharper exponential-utility concentration, and a stopping certificate close an exponential sample-complexity gap. | Mandatory neighbor for any entropic BPI or sharper-confidence claim. The theorem is not directly about fixed-risk local recursion with random duration, hard censoring, paid acquisition plus deployment utility, or measured computation time. Those differences require an actual new result; they do not establish one. |
| [Mortensen & Talebi, arXiv2506.00286v3, 2026-05-18](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.00286v3) | Infinite-horizon tabular discounted recursive ERM, generative samples, known deterministic rewards. Bellman operator is R + gamma CE_beta(V). Plug-in model estimation and Q-value iteration have PAC upper bounds and exponential lower bounds. | Direct recursive-risk learning neighbor. For fixed deterministic gamma and reward, the current core CE_eta(R + gamma V) equals R + gamma CE_(eta gamma)(V), so this difference is a parameter change. Random duration and correlated transition reward are outside its stated theorem. |
| [Kone & Jamieson, AISTATS 2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v300/kone26a.html); [full PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.03921) | Expected-return policy identification, posterior sampling plus online allocation, asymptotic sample optimality, O(S^2 A H) computation per episode. The detailed setup assumes a unique optimal policy and unit-variance Gaussian reward distributions with means in (0,1). | Mandatory neighbor for computationally efficient BPI. Its detailed assumptions do not automatically cover entropic utility, a finite deadline, censored duration, or lifecycle net utility. Do not quote the abstract as a theorem for all bounded-reward models. |
| [Lazzaro, Russo & Pacchiano, arXiv2607.17201, 2026-07-19](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17201) | Online tabular BPI with deterministic rewards; non-asymptotic analysis of Navigate and Stop, including connectivity and instance geometry beyond characteristic time. Abstract and introduction verified. | Rules out treating generic finite-sample BPI as untouched. Applicability to the exact paid-clock setting has not been proved here. |
| [Nitinawarat & Veeravalli, 2014 revision](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.1844) | Controlled Markovian observations, nonuniform control cost, asymptotically optimal multihypothesis tests, with a variant satisfying prescribed decision-risk constraints non-asymptotically. | Cost-sensitive adaptive sensing and decision-risk allocation are established. Their framework is a necessary comparison for decision-relevant calibration, rather than only uniform sampling or MLE. |
| [Kanarios, Zhang & Ying, RLJ 2024](https://rlj.org/papers/kanarios2024cost) | Best-arm identification with a cost distribution per arm, minimum expected testing cost, asymptotically matching CTAS, and a low-complexity square-root allocation rule. | Both unequal sampling costs and computation-aware simplification already have direct precedents. A hard observed-clock feasibility result is a more specific issue. |
| [Harding & Kandasamy, NeurIPS 2025](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/b0dfe8236981ba84b4d98a7ed0426f0b-Abstract-Conference.html) | A risk functional explicitly combines sampling fees and terminal recommendation penalty; DBCARE matches lower bounds up to polylogarithmic factors on the stated instance classes. | “Pay only if the terminal improvement repays acquisition” is not by itself a new objective. Different time and risk semantics must be justified and compared. |
| [Emmenegger, Mutny & Krause, NeurIPS 2023](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/5491280797f3192b895bce84eb83df8d-Abstract-Conference.html) | Likelihood-ratio anytime confidence sequences for adaptively collected observations; includes sequential decision settings and survival analysis. | The finite-catalog mixture confidence sequence below is a simple established construction. Handling censoring by its correct observation likelihood is necessary implementation, not a claimed new confidence principle. |
| [Wan, Liu, Grigas & Shen, arXiv2602.05340](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.05340v1) | Decision-focused experimental design for downstream linear optimization, finite hypothesis classes, bounded polyhedral decision sets; uses decision directions without an optimization-oracle acquisition call. | Decision-specific uncertainty and cheaper decision-aligned acquisition already have algorithms. The paper is not a general risk-sensitive sequential controller, but is a mandatory neighbor. |
| [Guo et al., ICML 2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/guo26g.html) | Constrained BED combines offline amortized policy/posterior training and online scenario-tree planning under dynamic budgets, costs, and physical design constraints. Official abstract verified. | Budget constraints plus multistep design are established. The target there is information gain; a decision-reward objective is distinct, but already has other neighbors. |
| [Lin, Kolobov, Kamar & Horvitz, IJCAI 2015](https://www.ijcai.org/Proceedings/15/Papers/229.pdf) | Planner execution state and external state form a meta-MDP; thinking incurs physical cost while the world can evolve. BRTDP structure supports approximate metareasoning. Full model definition verified. | Internal state, real time, computation cost and stopping are an old modeling combination. A new compute scheduler needs a specific improvement over qualified anytime metareasoning baselines. |
| [Barenboim & Indelman, AI 350 (2026), 104442](https://arxiv.org/html/2310.01791v5) | Finite-horizon expected-return POMDP planning with deterministic bounds from partially explored state/observation trees; action exploration and anytime certification. Full objective and principal bound inspected. | “Act once an upper/lower decision certificate separates actions” is established. The published theorem is for expectation, not this nested entropic objective; ordinary monotone interval propagation is still not a novel theorem. |
| [Asienkiewicz & Jaśkiewicz, 2017](https://www.impan.pl/shop/publication/transaction/download/product/92198) | Discounted recursive entropic utility on Borel-state MDPs; stationary optimal policies under standard assumptions. Its introduction writes the local preference recursion explicitly. | The local-versus-trajectory risk choice has a long literature. The current event-refinement example is a useful semantic diagnostic, not a new RL phenomenon. |
| [Bäuerle & Rieder, MOR 39 (2014), 105–120](https://publikationen.bibliothek.kit.edu/1000039663/3282526) | Certainty equivalents of total/discounted costs are solved on an extended state space. Theorem 3.6 propagates accumulated cost and discount state z to z beta. | Time-0 utility can be preserved by carrying the discount/risk state. The exponential special case explains the risk-parameter transport. This is established augmented-state dynamic programming. |
| [Bhabak & Saha, 2022 / arXiv2101.04510](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.04510) | Semi-Markov control with general utility of discounted cost, random finite/infinite horizons, and state augmentation. Primary abstract verified. | Random duration plus discounted nonlinear utility also has a direct planning precedent. This source alone does not supply a paid-calibration learning guarantee. |
| [Pariente & Indelman, arXiv2601.20554, 2026-01-28](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20554) | Iterated-CVaR policy evaluation and online POMDP planners, including sparse sampling with finite-time guarantees. Primary abstract verified. | Risk-sensitive online planning and tailored exploration are active nearby work. CVaR is not entropic risk, so this is a conceptual/algorithmic neighbor rather than an identity. |

## Citation discipline

Use the publication or author/arXiv pages above. The 2024 Bäuerle–Jaśkiewicz overview was useful for locating originals, but primary 2014/2017 papers support the mathematical scope statements. A search also surfaced arXiv2607.27651 and arXiv2609.35874; direct primary access failed, so neither is used as a verified substantive basis here.

## Scope verdict

No source checked here proves that the entire proposed paid, censored, real-clock lifecycle problem is solved with optimal finite computational cost. Conversely, no checked distinction by itself yields an original algorithm. The concrete root-influence candidate is rejected as equivalent to its qualified classical occupancy baseline; see `CANDIDATE_IDENTITY_NO_GO.md` and the adjacent executable audit. A future candidate needs a separable computational rule and a matched baseline before any new-RL claim.
