# Registered development after the first study

The first study at `00b85f9646aa39f86592c20f14f01397b55ff759` remains intact,
including its failed mechanism and natural-observer gates. This revision changes
the training method and evaluator development, then measures the consequences.
It does not infer efficacy from passing code tests.

## Controller experiment

`controller_v2_protocol.json` registers seeds 17/29, widths 32/64 and eight arms.
Each core receives 384 supervised updates on random legal generation sequences,
including their initial states and each sampled prefix. Each arm receives 64
joint updates with eight real four-file trajectories per update. Development
uses 32 new synthetic source identities with two draws per identity. Namespaces
for training, distillation and development differ from the original experiment.

The actor and generator retain the unnormalised population exponential moment
objective. Each random action and terminal emission now has a separate,
predictable control variate from the critic and previously incurred cost.
Independent-episode residual leave-one-out correction is retained. Exact
enumeration tests check the gradient identity, including a deliberately invalid
future-dependent baseline counterexample. The physical-timing independence and
parameter-exogeneity assumptions remain limitations. Coefficient mean-square is
a diagnostic, not a proof of smaller gradient variance.

The two additional controls, ordinary dynamic risk and Hepta fixed risk, compare
the operator and allocation at the same risk objective. The original mechanism
study's mean-versus-mean controls were valid comparisons. The old native
teacher-versus-ordinary comparison additionally changed planning and the training
objective and therefore did not isolate the operator.

Three DAgger rounds query the bounded critic planner on student-visited states.
Only actor parameters change. Student inference uses the greedy actor without
planner queries. Every query during training is measured. A trained student is
a candidate; generating its checkpoint is not deployment promotion.

## Independent observer development

`observer_v2_protocol.json` extends the original observer-training prefix from
64 to 128 sources (639 retained events). It preserves the complete 63-action and
17-sound ontology, original source roles, five source-grouped folds, the 60%
accuracy threshold and the requirement to beat each fold's training-majority
control. The old 64-source prefix must match byte-bound archived allocation.

The same-cohort controls are S3D and PANN global features. New candidates use the
previously pinned TSM model and PANN global plus three fixed temporal pools.
Only training folds fit normalisation and regression. Candidate selection and
reported OOF accuracy share development data; neither is independent
confirmation. The command cannot read the 64 reserved fresh sources, even when
development passes. Generated-output observer reliability remains a separate,
unpassed requirement.

## Frozen acceptance and costs

`development_plan_v2.json` and `development_plan_distillation_v2.json` are
write-once plan envelopes for the exact complete panels. The effect estimate
first evaluates the original gamma-2 CE inside each training seed and then
averages paired seed effects. Crossed seed/source bootstrap preserves pairing;
Bonferroni intervals account for the declared comparison-by-width family.
Two training seeds still provide limited population inference.

The student development margins for both utility CE and audit-inclusive net CE
are -0.005, alongside strictly positive serving-cost savings. This is an explicit
development design choice, not an accepted domain standard or a positive-benefit
claim. Confirmation requires new sources/seeds, frozen model identities and full
lifecycle costs at a registered 10,000-episode horizon; net CE must improve.
Failure utility stays at the original contract's -1, with incurred costs retained.

`process_meter_v2.py` measures the entire child interpreter from launch through
exit, including imports, loading and reaped codec children. Per-episode costs
are subtotals of that measured work and must not be added a second time. Package
installation and infrastructure charges remain outside this runtime tariff.
On abnormal exit, unreaped descendant CPU may be unavailable; such a receipt is
explicitly a measured lower bound, not complete lifecycle evidence.

The combined workflow first checks existing contracts and new gradient/stage
tests. A commit containing `[joint-v2-development]` then starts the four
controller configurations and the train-only observer development. No workflow
in this revision automatically starts confirmation or promotes a model.
