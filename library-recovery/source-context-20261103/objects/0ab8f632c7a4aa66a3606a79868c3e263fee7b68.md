## Exact mathematical invariants

For an initial `RenewalState.initial(a)`, the physical clock is zero and no exponential transition has occurred, so `ever_fired=expected_count=0`, and the killed no-fire populations must equal their unconditional initial populations (up to at most local floating-point representational slack). The previous probability sum/subset checks do not establish this time-origin invariant.

With **strictly positive** fire and recovery rates, any positive elapsed physical time carries strictly positive first-event probability and expected event count in real arithmetic, for any initial active probability in [0,1]. Real transitions which lose these strictly positive increments at binary64 precision already fail closed in the parent; persisted checkpoints should not be permitted to bypass that policy by supplying zero counters.
