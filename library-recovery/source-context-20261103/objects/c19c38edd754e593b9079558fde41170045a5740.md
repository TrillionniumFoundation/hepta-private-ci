## New independent numerical method

The existing `stopping.py` defines arithmetic Brownian membrane dynamics with drift `mu`, volatility `sigma`, discount `rho`, firing reward `x-cost`, delayed reset `reset`, mandatory refractory duration `tau` and `D=exp(-rho*tau)`. Its stationary value satisfies `max{L V-rho V, x-cost + D V(reset)-V}=0`.

`regenerative_grid.py` solves this QVI in **state space**, without calling the Lambert-W closed form or the analytic threshold. We enforce `V(xmin)=0` at a finite artificial lower wall and an immediate intervention at `xmax`, and use a positive-coefficient finite-difference generator. Near advection dominance, switch to an upwind generator instead of allowing negative Markov rates. An off-grid reset is convexly interpolated. Solve the resulting coupled LCP by sparse Howard policy iteration; independently solve the same discrete Bellman problem by *Jacobi iteration*. Measure both the maximum value-unit Bellman residual and (in tests) the unnormalized generator QVI complementarity.

This is a **discretized stationary scalar QVI**, not a fully solved coupled-cell network QVI, generalized state-dependent impulse kernel, full-domain theorem, rigorous interval certificate, certified derivative or actual GPU precision behavior. The lower wall and forced-fire upper wall are modeling truncations; domain and mesh sweeps are required and quantified for selected points only.
