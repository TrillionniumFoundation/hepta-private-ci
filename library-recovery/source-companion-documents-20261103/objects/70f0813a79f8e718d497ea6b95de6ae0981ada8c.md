## Same-64-case comparisons from the exact original ZIP

| Arm | Granite97 correct | EmbeddingGemma2 correct | Meaning |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Frozen random linear Head | 32/64 | 32/64 | Original randomized linear Head baseline |
| From-zero 40-step SGD affine Head | **61/64** | **61/64** | Both 95.3125% on original public TEST |
| TRAIN-label supervised cosine prototypes | 62/64 | 60/64 | 96.875% vs 93.75%; exploratory, only two independent errors apart |
| Gated residual 2K / 8K / 16K | 32/64 each | 32/64 each | In this specific source/seed/learning-rate, logits remain close to frozen baseline |

At the default 0.50 threshold, Granite and Gemma `from_zero` predictions agree on 59 correct and 1 wrong case, with **2 Granite-only correct** and **2 Gemma-only correct**. The nominal paired exact sign/McNemar two-sided p-value is **1.0**. This is **not evidence of equivalence**, nor grounds for choosing an Encoder. The 95% Clopper-Pearson interval for 61/64, **[86.91%, 99.02%]**, is conditional on unproven i.i.d. sampling; it does NOT confer statistical production or future generalization coverage for a selected public panel.

The original `from_zero` accuracies with retrospectively chosen, **non-preregistered** fixed thresholds:

| Fixed binary decision threshold | Granite97 | EmbeddingGemma2 |
|---|---:|---:|
| 0.49 | **55/64 (85.94%)** | **55/64 (85.94%)** |
| 0.50 | **61/64 (95.31%)** | **61/64 (95.31%)** |
| 0.51 | **62/64 (96.88%)** | **61/64 (95.31%)** |

No re-fitting or threshold optimization was performed; these thresholds were chosen for an *after-the-fact sensitivity inspection*, not before the underlying original experiment. The from-zero model probabilities lie between 0.42562–0.56789 for Granite and 0.45202–0.54581 for Gemma: much of the probability mass remains near 0.5. Their retrospective TEST Brier scores are ~0.2120 and ~0.2289. Do not claim these values prove calibrated production certainty or a robust decision policy.

Within each model, from-zero learned vs frozen has 31 wins / 2 losses on Granite and 32 wins / 3 losses on Gemma (net +29 correct for both). The 2K, 8K and 16K residuals retain 32/64 correct despite separate parameter budgets. That finding is **not universal evidence that residual learning is inferior**; an untuned frozen-baseline residual with zero-initialized final projection and 40 steps of full-batch SGD is a different optimization/initialization mechanism, and source-panel parameters plus future tasks are not a controlled matched-cell selection study.
