# Identifiability of the nine-parameter finite constructor

Within any admitted reset-interpolation stratum, the R22 constructor has the factorization

\[
F(\theta)=G\bigl(b(\theta),\ \mu(\theta),\ D(\theta)\bigr),\qquad
\mu=r-\delta-\sigma^2/2,\quad D=\sigma^2/2.
\]

Here F is the complete 2,001-coordinate boundary/Q/READ/REFRESH/prior output, not merely one measured reward. The grid, recovery rates, content-retention matrix and emission tables are fixed by the declared profile. Active nearest-neighbor rates use D/h² ± mu/(2h), boundary absorption uses 2D/h², and reset/recovery weights and the prior use the interpolation of log(90/b). These exhaust the constructor's parameter dependence.

Consequently the ordinary Jacobian obeys J_F = J_G J_(b,mu,D), so its rank is at most three wherever that stratum is differentiable. This derivative statement concerns the declared real-arithmetic model and numerical AD convention, not a derivative of the discontinuous bit-rounding map. All nine named parameter columns can be nonzero while remaining linearly dependent. At an admitted regular point, there are at least six local tangent directions that do not alter this finite output to first order. Exact reset knots remain excluded; no ordinary derivative is asserted there.

The 595-point Jacobian confirmation establishes numerical correctness of the declared native parameterization. It does not establish nine separately identifiable dynamical mechanisms. Statistical learning may still benefit from a physical parameter prior; that is a separate same-information, same-budget learning comparison.

The R20 pathwise equivalence result also continues to apply when an ordinary CTMC/HMM is given exactly the same constructed law, prior, reset, controller and tie semantics: its real-arithmetic actions and utilities coincide on every shared owner tape, so that matched finite-law D−B is zero. More samples of that identical mechanism cannot establish an exclusive gain. Comparisons with different fitted laws, compression, approximate filtering, learned values or complete financial equity/debt channels must identify the actual difference and count its complete cost.

Full equity/debt/risk functions can have additional parameter dependence beyond this constructor. They require their own implementation, derivative references and task-consumption tests. No result from this finite-constructor factorization rules out such a future network; no result here qualifies its quality or production ownership.
