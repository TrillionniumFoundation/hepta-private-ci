# Registered trained-cell retention probe: executed secondary results

## Execution and interpretation

The prospectively published probe completed **16/16 fits, 96/96 selected fitted models and 480/480 horizon records**, with exit 0 and no failed or omitted model. It verified all 384 sealed models, all 40 main scientific source files and all sealed TRAIN-data identities before and after execution. No confirmation cohort or outcome was accessed.

This is a controlled **fitted-dynamics information probe** with one noisy completed observation (reliability .85), balanced hidden classes and no additional READ. Each observed-sign-conditioned law was propagated separately before class mixing. The Bayes decoder is calculated from this declared finite law; it is not an empirical neural-head accuracy measurement or a task-utility result.

The inherited finite law permits at most one fresh event per interval and end-of-interval recovery. Horizons compose the declared intervals .25, .5, .75 and 1.5. These results do not certify continuous Brownian renewal or invariance to interval repartition.

At time zero the impulse model has just received its one injection, whereas sustained input has not yet accumulated any elapsed drift. That .35 accuracy difference is a declared initialization/semantics consequence. Later, sustained input repeatedly reintroduces the remembered public observation into the cell. Its rising information is not a violation of data processing for an isolated state: the state is continually receiving that driver. Impulse full-cell information is nonincreasing under its neutral subsequent law, as checked in every fit.

The actual policy head still receives public time, schedule information and the last observation. Consequently, cell-only forgetting is not whole-policy information loss. In the main task, impulse changes both interval drift and the bank's future-driver features while injecting at READ completion. Its task contrast is the total declared input-mechanism intervention, not a drift-only causal effect; this probe does not identify bank-feature versus state-propagation contributions.

Sustained event sign can contain more information than the interval-start source cell alone because the remembered driver remains an additional input. Its valid upstream bound includes source plus driver. The probe does not impose the incorrect source-alone-to-sign data-processing chain for sustained models. It does check this chain for impulse models, as well as joint-source/sign coarsening and class-independent reset maps in both modes.

For first-passage/sustained at this interval, event-sign accuracy .847322 falls to .838841 under the fitted signed reset (paired mean loss .008481), while forcing identical reset distributions yields .502631. This supports preservation of sign information by the fitted reset kernel in this controlled population. It does not establish a utility benefit of reset, natural recovery-time optimality or superiority over the ordinary comparator. Equal-reset accuracy can exceed .5 because event selection changes the class prior.

## What is resolved and what remains open

The secondary requirement now has **trained-parameter**, probability-preserving before/after information evidence for all registered models, including the correctly selected source/event/reset population. It closes the absence of a fitted-cell information probe for the declared one-observation finite law.

Whole-policy maximal information under arbitrary histories, an independently fitted decoder on actual task states, event/reset causal task utility, free-endpoint-bank versus propagation attribution and continuous-time efficacy remain outside this probe. The earlier initialized fit-101 probes remain separate historical diagnostics. These new records use all 16 formal fitted models and do not turn either probe family into main-task confirmation.

All five horizons, six candidates and 16 raw fit records remain in `digital-runs/trained_retention`. No sample was selected or discarded. A preliminary ad hoc summary command had a token typo and failed at parsing; the registered probe itself succeeded on its first authorized execution.
