## Jump-aware parameter derivatives

The proposed fifteen coordinates are the full **declared trainable set** for
the observed-quality task: log volatility, log WAIT carry, log recovery carry,
two log reset fees, reset location, four row-softmax content-transition logits,
two base logits, two amplitude-share logits, and log payoff slope. Domain,
lattice, horizon and deterministic recovery duration remain fixed. Their
exclusion must not be described as a full financial-parameter or global
continuous-model gradient result.

An analytic tangent must propagate both left and right values at recovery
admission jumps and differentiate reset-location interpolation. A sparse
global-system reference should assemble its own couplings and right-hand
sides; a finite difference used to construct the tested derivative is not an
independent derivative check. Finite differences of the actual primal remain
a useful secondary check. Every coordinate needs nonvacuous coverage.

STOP/RESET/continuation ties, reset interpolation knots and changes to the time
or recovery lattice need an explicit directional or rejection contract.
Derivative agreement on a fixed finite active branch does not establish a
continuum free-boundary derivative or an argmax derivative. Capturing a primal
frame must bind the exact frozen function, verify its required locals and
restore any installed profiler in a finally block.

## Actual native numerical execution

R18 Rust replay treated Python numerical states as opaque receipts. R19 must
compute transition, filtering and planning successors from the native-owned
probability state. An oracle stream of Python after-states or actions cannot
qualify that claim. Provider completion and scorer-only owner tapes must remain
outside the native controller's information boundary.

For uniformization, a small absolute Poisson-tail bound alone does not preserve
rare-event support. A tiny positive observation likelihood can be erased by a
low-order cutoff and then amplified by Bayes normalization. Require a declared
structural path-order/support contract and rare-observation relative/posterior
checks, or reject unsupported cases. Neither epsilon floors nor silent
renormalization can supply missing evidence.

Repeated-event expected count is not a substochastic probability augmentation.
If a conditional count is bounded by k times r_max/lambda after k uniformized
steps, its omitted contribution is bounded using the first Poisson tail moment,
not the unweighted probability tail. Binary composition must propagate both
transition and reward errors. Floating-point roundoff assumptions, support
underflow rejection and conditional-likelihood limits remain explicit.

## Actual reuse and complete economics

An actual N=1000 measurement must state whether N is per fitted model, per
model/representation, or total service uses. Preparation and prewarming are
charged; shared kernels do not make search, training, owner construction,
checkpoint I/O or cold restoration free. Keep measured current-machine costs,
historical other-machine CPU and unknown portions separate. A lower bound on
recorded costs is not a whole-project cost.

Report the successful optimization together with failed earlier candidates.
Same-matrix ordinary models receive the same implementation optimization.
Adversarial elapsed-time inputs must not evade the stated memory contract:
bounding a wrapper while its underlying transition cache grows is insufficient.
Argmax ties and complete state/fee parity remain unchanged acceptance criteria.

## Cross-family uncertainty calibration

Public training/validation can fit mixture weights and choose a static
reference. New held-out families cannot tune either those choices or the
admission guard. Runtime controls receive only the permitted raw prefix and
observations; true family labels, generator parameters and targets remain
scorer-only. Keep the original risk limit .10.

With two models, four thresholds, three calibrated families and two risk
metrics, simultaneous calibration uses 48 fixed tests. Holdout multiplicity,
primary candidate, fractional-answer minimum and zero-denominator bootstrap
rule must be declared before outcomes. A gate that requires useful fractional
coverage cannot pass through universal refusal. Failure on any required held-out
family remains a failure, followed only by a separately frozen study.

Finite tested families cannot establish distribution-free reliability for all
unknown generators. CPU evidence cannot replace GPU or measured energy data;
API receipt replay cannot replace authenticated production ownership or actual
full-model activation. New evidence must close exactly the claim it tests.
