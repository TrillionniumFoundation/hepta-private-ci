# Finite native numeric scope

The program consumes the frozen 40-state R15 row generator, READ/REFRESH emissions and one causal prior as numeric model data. It computes every retained successor in Rust. It does not consume a Python-computed successor tape. Parameter-to-generator construction, training, control selection and production owner admission are separate work.

For a conservative row generator Q, choose lambda=max(-Q_ii). The embedded matrix P=I+Q/lambda is nonnegative and stochastic in exact arithmetic. A killed generator removes only the declared natural-event edges while retaining their diagonal losses; its embedded matrix is substochastic. Starting at p_0, use p_(k+1)=p_k P. The expected natural-event count has embedded reward recurrence c_(k+1)=c_k+p_k r/lambda, c_0=0. Poisson-weighted sums of p_k and c_k give propagation and integrated flux. The killed version accumulates only the first natural event. It preserves the full surviving successor including recovery and re-entry; expected repeated events are not capped at one.

Each segment has mu=lambda*dt/segments<=8 and retains terms k=0,...,159. For exact arithmetic, its omitted Poisson mass is at most w_160/(1-8/161), where w_k=exp(-8)*8^k/k!. For repeated-event reward, r_max/lambda<=1 and the omitted weighted count is at most 8*w_159/(1-8/160). The declared maximum lambda=100 and dt=20 requires at most 250 segments. A deliberately loose bound including accumulated omitted mass times future count remains below 1e-130. These are truncation bounds, not FP64 roundoff bounds. The recorded independent matrix exponential comparisons qualify only the tested finite profile.

The program never clips negative probabilities or normalizes a propagation step. Bayes conditioning divides by the actual observed evidence. Signed edges, nonconservative generators, unsupported duration, nonfinite numbers, malformed streams and zero/underflowed evidence are rejected. A positive duration whose uniformization clock underflows to zero is rejected. No all-input relative accuracy guarantee is made near subnormal products or vanishing conditional evidence.

RESET restarts recovery phase zero and preserves each content marginal. This is the synthetic R12/R15 intervention convention, not a newly proved financial stopping action. The frozen input format has no authenticated model identity or provider identity; the source/model/binary hashes belong to the research evidence and do not authorize production activation.

Cold-process verification serializes the actual native posterior with round-trip FP64 decimal precision and passes that posterior to a fresh native process. It proves the retained numeric continuation for this stateless input protocol. It does not prove power-loss atomicity, a deployed owner callback, task-level gain or production lifecycle correctness.
