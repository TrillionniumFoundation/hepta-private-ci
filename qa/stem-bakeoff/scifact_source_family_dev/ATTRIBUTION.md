ODC-By does not itself grant all rights in individual abstracts. Corpus and
citation text are retained only locally for this bounded research computation;
the candidate publication consists of our code, source hashes, IDs and aggregate
metrics, not a redistributed corpus or a claim that all abstract rights are open.

this experiment uses only the original publisher's download and license notice.

No model is trained/run. No inference about clinical truth or treatment follows
from this retrieval diagnostic. It is not a future unseen Memory A-I window.
