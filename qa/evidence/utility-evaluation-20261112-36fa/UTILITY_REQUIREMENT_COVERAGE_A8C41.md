3. **The admitted deterministic provider cannot manufacture the requested positive counterfactual evidence.** Its actual support/critic/frozen-plan restrictions must remain intact.
4. **The retention guard is refusal, not new aggregate adjudication.** Existing complete primary-negative NoUpdate remains; a new aggregate negative contract has not been authorized or implemented.
