## Remaining external and full-candidate gates

The existing read-only exact source/synthetic-merge and measurement workflows
remain the full-candidate evidence producers. Focused successes do not mark those
pending or failed runs successful. `CURRENT_STATE.json` retains false
accepted/activated/released/production truth; no static pass field is patched.

The selected-host workflow remains on registered `objective-target-host`
self-hosted runners and its protected environment. It must execute the immutable
candidate, retain V2 artifact-bound measurements, and acquire resource/storage
acceptance from the owning authority. Hosted CI timings cannot close those gates.
The resource measure is OS-reported waited-child peak RSS, not simultaneous
process-tree aggregate memory, compilation cost or per-phase allocation. The
updated measurement guide specifies the distinction.

Independent review, operation/canary/promotion/rollback, FFI and release receipts
are not generated or appointed by the source patch. The product continues to use
private validated state, exact identities, DENY_ALL compile artifacts and the
existing destination-owner transaction and final-use authority. Legacy records
remain inspectable with their frozen identity but do not acquire a new admission
proof or execution authority during recovery or compaction.
