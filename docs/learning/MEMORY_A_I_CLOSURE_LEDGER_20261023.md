## Two-phase research preregistration dependency

The prospective model cannot be required to publish a final trained tensor SHA
**before training happens**. Either train and seal the candidate before fixing
the **future** evaluation cohort, or use two separately authenticated freezes:

1. **Before training:** independently timestamp sources, architecture, eligible
   training roots, all candidate arms, objective, total budget, decision rule,
   contrasts, and no-access-to-future-labels policy.
2. **After training / before future evaluation:** independently seal original
   tensor bytes, Head/Router/Graph/evolution complete journals, validation
   feedback and frozen model/Reader identities. No new training after opening
   future labels. Exact CPU and FLOP measurements may be finalized in an
   independently sealed execution receipt without changing model bytes.

Current #3411 single-plan scheme mixes a pre-training freeze claim with
pre-known resulting artifact hashes. Do not claim independent prospective
qualification without reviewing this sequencing and immutable owner.
